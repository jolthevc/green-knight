# Visual Style Standard

This extends the portfolio VISUAL_QUALITY_STANDARD.md, which defines the floor.

## 1. Identity
The channel is a rulebook come alive: restrained, documentary, and graphic-led. Paper and ink textures meet clean vector diagrams. It should feel crafted and editorial, never like a highlight reel.

The signature is the Amendment: old rule text struck through in red, with the new clause written in.

## 2. Building Blocks
- **Original tactical and mechanical diagrams:** the primary explanatory layer.
- **Rule and document graphics:** rule text, minutes, statements.
- **Licensed archival stills and press clippings:** evidence and era.
- **Licensed or public-domain footage:** used when available, never relied upon.
- **Maps and venue context, and crowd or stadium stock:** sparingly, and as texture only.

## 3. B-Roll Rule
Every visual shows the actual team, object, era, or mechanism. Generic sports stock used to illustrate a noun is prohibited.

## 4. Diagrams
- Every exploit gets a step-by-step animated diagram.
- Players are simple tokens.
- The exploiter is flag yellow. Everyone else is ink.
- Every rule change gets the Amendment.
- Timelines appear only for multi-year arcs.

## 5. Text and Type
- Rule quotations in a classic serif with an official-document feel. Titles also use the serif.
- Labels and data in a clean grotesque sans.
- Monospace allowed for scores and timestamps.
- Every archival item carries a source label with publication and date.
- Keep on-screen text to short phrases, except quoted rules.

## 6. Color
- Off-white paper and ink black.
- Flag yellow is reserved for the exploiter.
- Amendment red is reserved for rule changes.
- Team colors are allowed inside diagrams only.

## 7. Motion and Pace
- Motion: ink-draw reveals, diagram builds, and slow push-ins. No whips, shake, or glitch.
- Opening: brisk.
- Mechanism: patient.
- Backlash: a faster clipping montage.
- Amendment: a held beat.
- Adaptation: relaxed.

## 8. Sound
- Understated, mischievous score. Music never competes with narration.
- Paper and pen foley on the Amendment.
- Silence before reveals.
- No comedic stings.

## 9. People and Evidence
- People appear only through licensed archival or press photos. No AI-generated real people.
- Recreations carry a subtle RECREATION tag and remain clearly diagrammatic.

## 10. AI Imagery
**Allowed:**
- Textures.
- Stylized period backdrops.
- Object illustrations in the channel's illustrative style.

**Forbidden:** photoreal depictions of real people, events, documents, or scoreboards.

## 11. References
**Reference:**
- Tifo Football, for tactical clarity.
- Chain Bear, for technical animation.
- Secret Base, for documents as story.
- Summoning Salt, for patient escalation.

**Anti-reference:**
- Highlight compilations.
- Stock-built top-10s.
- Neon hype edits.

## 12. Editor Latitude
**Open to the editor:**
- Diagram choreography.
- Archival selection.
- Montage rhythm.
- Texture.
- Music.

The Amendment should vary in staging so it never feels stamped on.

**Required:**
- Amendment device.
- Yellow-exploiter convention.
- Recreation labels.
- Source labels.
