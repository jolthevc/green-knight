# Visual Style: Near Miss

Sits above the portfolio VISUAL_QUALITY_STANDARD, which sets the floor.

## 1. Identity
Restrained, dark, evidence-first. An investigation file brought to life: report pages, transcripts, schematics, and real photos organized by a clean instrument-panel graphic system. Cinematic in tone, informational in function.

## 2. Building Blocks (in priority order)
1. **Primary documents:** reports, declassified memos, commit logs. Proof.
2. **Archival photos and footage** of the actual place, aircraft, facility, or people.
3. **Hero diagrams and schematics:** the core of every mechanism.
4. **Maps and flight paths** for spatial stakes.
5. **Transcripts as typography:** CVR, ATC, chat logs.
6. **Type-correct stock:** right aircraft, reactor type, city. Texture only.
7. **Stylized 3D or illustration** for reconstructions and counterfactuals.

## 3. B-Roll
Show the actual thing or the correct type of thing, down to model and livery. Generic servers, keyboards, and control rooms are filler.

## 4. Hero Diagram
Every episode has one. Build it progressively, then return to it as the flaw escalates. Minimal labels, generalist complexity.

## 5. Signature Devices
- **The Clock:** a minimal persistent timestamp during escalation, matched to the source timeline.
- **Counterfactual Treatment:** desaturated blue-grey stylized render with a persistent label (COUNTERFACTUAL, plus the evidence tier). It must never read as real footage.

## 6. On-Screen Text
Short labels, times, names, record quotes. A source label on every document and data point. Full sentences only for transcripts.

## 7. Typography
Clean grotesque sans for labels; monospace for times, transcripts, and code; two weights; no display fonts.

## 8. Color
- Near-black and deep navy grounds, off-white text.
- **Amber:** the flaw or warning.
- **Red:** the decision moment, or red that exists in the real evidence.
- **Blue-grey:** counterfactual mode only.
- Flat charts, two colors maximum.

## 9. Motion
Slow push-ins, deliberate map moves, step-by-step diagram builds. No whips, shakes, or glitch effects, except one motivated glitch in a digital episode.

## 10. Pace
Setup unhurried. Escalation tightens and the Clock dominates. The save holds on image and silence. Counterfactual measured.

## 11. Sound
Low drones and pulses building through escalation, silence or a near-drop at the decision, warm understated resolution. Sparse diegetic cues (alarms, radio static). No trailer hits or inspirational swells. Music never competes with narration.

## 12. People
Real photos of documented people, credited. Silhouettes or hands for reconstructed moments. No generated faces of real people.

## 13. AI Visuals
**Allowed:** stylized reconstructions, environmental backgrounds, labeled counterfactual renders, thumbnail ideation.
**Forbidden:** photoreal depictions of real events or people, fake documents, photoreal disasters that did not happen.

## 14. References
Air Crash Investigation for reconstruction logic without the grief. LEMMiNO for restraint and silence. Kyle Hill and Mentour Pilot for diagram-led mechanisms. HBO's Chernobyl for dread without spectacle.

## 15. Anti-References
Content-farm compilations, explosion-heavy CGI, hoodie-hacker stock, red-arrow meme editing.

## 16. Editor Judgment
Required: the Clock, the Counterfactual Treatment, the hero diagram, evidence labeling. Open: their execution, montage texture, music within the palette, diagram styling within the system, pacing within sections. One look across all pillars; no pillar-specific styles.
