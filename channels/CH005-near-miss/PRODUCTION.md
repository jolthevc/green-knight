# Production: Near Miss

Extends the portfolio EDITOR_CREATIVE_STANDARD, VISUAL_QUALITY_STANDARD, and EDITOR_BRIEF_TEMPLATE. No channel-specific tooling.

## 1. Model
**AI (standard Green Knight pipeline):** ideation, research grounded in primary reports, timestamped timeline, outline and script, counterfactual evidence grading, packaging options, production packet.

**Humans:** fact-check of every margin and counterfactual claim against the source (the heaviest human check), one editor for edit and diagrams, thumbnail execution, final QA.

## 2. Format
- Long-form 16:9; 4K preferred, 1080p minimum.
- Runtime target 18 to 24 minutes; 15 to 28 when the story demands.
- Cadence: roughly 2 to 3 per month during calibration, revisited at the five-video checkpoint.
- Narration synthetic or human; a calm low-register voice either way, selected during calibration.
- Complexity medium-high, driven by diagrams and the Clock.

## 3. Inputs Before Edit
- Approved script and narration audio.
- Timestamped event timeline.
- Source log linking report pages and figures.
- Hero diagram spec.
- Counterfactual script with evidence tiers.
- Pronunciation notes and packaging direction.

## 4. Production Packet
By section and narration block: visual objective; REQUIRED evidence (documents, correct aircraft or facility); PREFERRED footage; OPEN texture; Clock timestamps; diagram build steps; counterfactual scene boundaries; sound notes at decision and save.

## 5. Editor Owns
Type-correct licensed footage, diagram build and animation, Clock and counterfactual execution, pacing, music, sound, and flagging any visual that implies a fact the script does not claim.

## 6. Green Knight Owns
Research and source log, script and narration, primary documents placed in Assets, the brand kit (Clock template, color, type, counterfactual template), final approval.

## 7. Thumbnails
Green Knight supplies 2 or 3 concepts at packaging. The editor or designer executes after first cut, once key assets exist. Green Knight approves.

## 8. Revisions
Two rounds, notes labeled MUST FIX, STRONG PREFERENCE, or OPTIONAL THOUGHT. Narration changes return through script review.

## 9. Channel QA
- Any title margin matches the sourced figure.
- Every counterfactual scene carries its label and tier.
- Aircraft, reactor, and hardware types are correct.
- Clock times match the source timeline.
- No photoreal AI depicts real events or people.
- Photos of named people are correctly attributed.

## 10. Rights
- US federal reports and many federal images are generally public domain; verify each.
- Licensed stock and library music only.
- News footage only under license or a documented fair-use review.
- Respect licenses on open-source code shown on screen.
- Log provenance for every asset.

## 11. Files
Naming: CH005-V0001_firstcut_v1.mp4, CH005-V0001_master.mp4, CH005-V0001_thumb_final.png, CH005-V0001_captions.srt. Retain project files, diagram sources, templates, and narration.

## 12. Access
Revocable Drive access to the video folder only. Written, timestamped feedback.

## 13. Cost
No validated figure yet. Diagram labor is the expected swing cost. Record actuals per video during calibration.

## 14. Failure Modes
Stock filling diagram gaps, counterfactual visuals drifting photoreal, under-checked margin claims, pillar-specific looks fragmenting the brand.
