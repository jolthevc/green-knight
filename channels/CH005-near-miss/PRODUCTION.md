# Production: Near Miss

Extends the portfolio EDITOR_CREATIVE_STANDARD, VISUAL_QUALITY_STANDARD, EDITOR_BRIEF_TEMPLATE, and NARRATION_STANDARD. No channel-specific tooling.

## 1. Model
**AI (standard Green Knight pipeline):** ideation, research grounded in primary reports, timestamped timeline, outline and script, counterfactual evidence grading, packaging options, production packet, and narrator-definition materials.

**Humans:** fact-check of every margin and counterfactual claim against the source (the heaviest human check), one editor for narration generation, edit and diagrams, thumbnail execution, final QA.

## 2. Format
- Long-form 16:9; 4K preferred, 1080p minimum.
- Runtime target 18 to 24 minutes; 15 to 28 when the story demands.
- Cadence: roughly 2 to 3 per month during calibration, revisited at the five-video checkpoint.
- Narration: synthetic channel voice, selected and locked during channel calibration; calm, controlled, precise, and never sensational. The editor generates narration during production.
- Narrator readiness: REQUIRED once the channel enters production. Finished narration audio is not required before PRODUCTION_READY.
- Complexity medium-high, driven by diagrams and the Clock.

## 3. Inputs Before Edit
- Approved script.
- Locked Near Miss narrator profile and editor access to the approved voice.
- Timestamped event timeline.
- Source log linking report pages and figures.
- Hero diagram spec.
- Counterfactual script with evidence tiers.
- Pronunciation notes and packaging direction.

## 4. Narration
Green Knight owns the approved words and narrator identity. The editor owns generation, delivery, and timing against picture.

The editor may regenerate approved passages, adjust pauses and pace, and create room for visual explanation or tension. The editor may not silently change wording, factual meaning, or narrator identity.

Relevant narration exports should be retained under `Production/Narration/` when useful for revision or transferability. No automated narration manifest or segmentation scheme is required.

## 5. Production Packet
By section and narration block: visual objective; REQUIRED evidence (documents, correct aircraft or facility); PREFERRED footage; OPEN texture; Clock timestamps; diagram build steps; counterfactual scene boundaries; sound notes at decision and save.

## 6. Editor Owns
Narration generation using the locked channel voice, type-correct licensed footage, diagram build and animation, Clock and counterfactual execution, pacing, music, sound, and flagging any narration or visual that implies a fact the script does not claim.

## 7. Green Knight Owns
Research and source log, approved script, narrator identity and voice access, primary documents placed in Assets, the brand kit (Clock template, color, type, counterfactual template), final approval.

## 8. Thumbnails
Green Knight supplies 2 or 3 concepts at packaging. The editor or designer executes after first cut, once key assets exist. Green Knight approves.

## 9. Revisions
Two rounds, notes labeled MUST FIX, STRONG PREFERENCE, or OPTIONAL THOUGHT. Wording changes return through script review; delivery-only narration changes remain within editor latitude.

## 10. Channel QA
- Narration matches the approved script and locked narrator identity.
- Any title margin matches the sourced figure.
- Every counterfactual scene carries its label and tier.
- Aircraft, reactor, and hardware types are correct.
- Clock times match the source timeline.
- No photoreal AI depicts real events or people.
- Photos of named people are correctly attributed.

## 11. Rights
- US federal reports and many federal images are generally public domain; verify each.
- Licensed stock and library music only.
- News footage only under license or a documented fair-use review.
- Respect licenses on open-source code shown on screen.
- Log provenance for every asset.

## 12. Files
Naming: CH005-V0001_firstcut_v1.mp4, CH005-V0001_master.mp4, CH005-V0001_thumb_final.png, CH005-V0001_captions.srt. Retain project files, diagram sources, templates, and relevant narration exports.

## 13. Access
Revocable Drive access to the video folder and revocable voice-platform access where supported. Never share raw provider API keys with editors. Written, timestamped feedback.

## 14. Cost
No validated figure yet. Diagram labor is the expected swing cost. Record actuals per video during calibration.

## 15. Failure Modes
Stock filling diagram gaps, counterfactual visuals drifting photoreal, under-checked margin claims, pillar-specific looks fragmenting the brand, narration becoming melodramatic, or narrator identity drifting between episodes.
