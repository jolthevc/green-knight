# Narration Calibration

**Status:** Canonical channel-template artifact  
**Purpose:** Define and audition the narrator identity before production begins.

---

## 1. Narrator Brief

Describe the voice as part of the channel brand.

Cover only dimensions that matter to the sound and viewing experience, such as:

- conversational vs theatrical
- vocal register
- accent where relevant
- energy
- restraint
- humor treatment
- technical-explanation behavior
- reveal behavior
- qualities to avoid

Do not default every channel to the same documentary voice.

---

## 2. Voice Design / Search Prompt

Write a polished natural-language prompt that can be used directly in the selected voice platform to design or search for candidate voices.

---

## 3. Calibration Passage

Write approximately 100-180 words that test the channel's important delivery modes.

The passage should be representative of the channel but must not depend on unverified factual claims about a real story.

Useful dimensions may include:

- opening hook
- explanatory mechanism
- denser factual language
- emotional beat
- humor where relevant
- reveal or payoff

---

## 4. Scorecard

Create a small scorecard tailored to the channel.

Judge full-episode durability and brand fit, not merely which voice sounds most impressive in a short demo.

---

## 5. Locking the Voice

When a winner is chosen:

- save the provider voice to the Green Knight account / workspace
- record its provider `voice_id` in `narration.yaml`
- record the baseline `model_id` and relevant settings used in the audition
- set `profile_status: locked`
- set the portfolio Sheet `narration_status` to `READY`
- record the winner and lock date below

The production editor should then receive revocable access to the locked voice where supported.
