# Car Lore: instructions for agents and collaborators

Read current `README.md`, `CHANNEL.md`, `VOICE.md`, `CONTENT_AND_AUDIENCE.md`, `HOOKS_AND_PACKAGING.md`, `SCRIPT_STANDARD.md`, `NARRATION_AND_VISUAL.md`, `EDITOR_WORKFLOW.md`, and `PILOT_SLATE.md` before ideating, scripting, directing or reviewing. Also consult `business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md` and `entertainment/AGENTS.md`. Car Lore-specific standards override any other channel's subject scope, narrator personality and runtime.

## Immutable creative mandate
1. **Sound obsessed with cars, not performatively excited.** Enthusiasm lives in exact observations, vivid sensory description, affection, earned judgment and spontaneous delight, never generic hype or endless shouting.
2. **Every car is a character.** Show its dream, aesthetic, driving character, reputation, people, conflict, consequences and legacy where evidence supports each.
3. **Two-viewer test.** The enthusiast must find credible specificity; the newcomer must never feel excluded. Expand jargon only when relevant to what viewers can feel or understand.
4. **Popcorn quality.** Make the opening irresistible; build consequential reversals; include at least one earned pure car-appreciation beat; finish by paying off the title and deepening affection or understanding.
5. **Truth and rights are non-negotiable.** Verify facts and disputed history. Do not fabricate test drives, personal experience, dialogue, motives, horsepower, performance, accident causes, historical quotes, claims of sound authenticity, or false original footage. Publicly accessible media is not automatically cleared for reuse.
6. **Be playful, not smug or mean.** We can lovingly mock an odd design or bad decision, not demean victims, owners, drivers, enthusiasts or real people.
7. **No generic auto reviews, specifications dumps, dry corporate histories or empty luxury slideshows.** A distinctive narrative is compulsory.

## Before declaring any work complete
- Check all channel documents for agreement on voice, audience, runtime and workflow.
- Identify exact real-world claims that require sourcing, footage/audio/imagery needing clearance, and reputational/technical claims needing extra review.
- Mark idea pitches, pilot concepts, hypothetical hooks and examples **unresearched** until verified.
- The owner approves topic, final script, selected narrator and launch. Never imply approval, spending, commissioning or publication.
- Follow the three-file editor packet rule in `EDITOR_WORKFLOW.md`; do not contaminate spoken script with directions.
- Do not make unrelated edits to the main portfolio configuration, business records or other channel workspaces without instruction.
- When writing to GitHub, give exact paths, commit and next unapproved steps.
