# Channel constitution: Car Lore

## Vision
**For the love of cars. And the unbelievable stories behind them.**

Cars are engineering, art, freedom, status, rebellion, nostalgia, competition and obsession wrapped in metal and rubber. Some are masterpieces. Some are magnificent disasters. Some are objectively difficult to love, and have devoted fans anyway. Every legendary machine is surrounded by people and decisions that made it matter.

We tell astonishing automotive stories with the love of the most passionate car person in the room and the discipline of a first-rate documentary maker.

**Not merely to explain automobiles, but to make the world fall in love with them.**

## The distinctive promise
- **Click:** I recognize that car, brand, rivalry or cultural icon, and its unanswered mystery is irresistible.
- **First 30 seconds:** I am looking at the actual machine, can feel the stakes, and want to know how this happened.
- **Watch:** I am entertained by a sequence of consequential decisions, tensions and turns. The car gets vivid hero moments, not just stock B-roll.
- **Finish:** I can tell someone a surprising, specific story about this machine and understand something new about why enthusiasts care.
- **Return:** These are the people who make me want to love cars more.

## Personality
Incurably enthusiastic; knowledgeable without gatekeeping; cheeky without contempt; opinionated with evidence; deeply sensory; funny when the story warrants it; reverent when a car earns reverence. Genuine delight matters more than generic cinematic solemnity.

## Why a separate channel
Our center of gravity is **automotive obsession and storytelling**, not corporate strategy (Money Moves / Behind Everything), celebrity finance (Fame & Fortune), internet attention (Internet Famous), or company failure alone (Fallen Angels). An auto business decision belongs here only when it reveals why a car became loved, feared, iconic, controversial or culturally meaningful.

## Editorial obligations
1. At least one **vehicle-character moment** that illuminates what the car looks, sounds or feels like, with authentic evidence or clearly interpretive language.
2. A documented **human and narrative engine**, not an encyclopedia recap.
3. A defined **nonfan invitation** within the opening; explain engineering by consequences, not numbers alone.
4. A **gearhead credibility beat** beyond obvious widely recycled trivia, rigorously sourced.
5. A satisfying **return on curiosity** in the conclusion.
6. **Fact, inference, legend and taste are distinguished.** Where enthusiasts disagree, say so. No invented first-hand driving claims.

## What winning feels like
- Wonder: "That car is incredible."
- Entertainment: "I cannot believe that actually happened."
- Appreciation: "I finally understand why people love it."

**One-line editorial test:** If somebody who did not care about cars could have written it without changing anything, rewrite. If only an enthusiast understands it, rewrite.

## Working format
10-minute flagship long-form documentaries; 8-12 acceptable after measured pacing. Editorial experimentation before fixed cadence. Initial video topics and thumbnails remain owner-selectable, not approved commissions.

## Brand philosophy
Our obsession should be contagious, not exclusionary. We celebrate enthusiast culture without glorifying reckless public-road driving, unsafe stunts, harassment, illegal racing or preventable harm. We admire daring while being precise about real consequences.
