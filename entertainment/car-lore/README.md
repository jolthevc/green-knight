# Car Lore

**For the love of cars. And the unbelievable stories behind them.**

**The promise:** Incredibly entertaining, rigorously researched automotive documentaries made with unmistakable, infectious love for cars. Make lifelong car people feel understood and make newcomers fall in love with the machines.

**The distinguishing idea:** We are not a general business channel covering auto manufacturers, a vehicle review channel, an engineering lecture, or an automotive news feed. We are a group of incurable car obsessives who happen to be exceptional storytellers. The **car is a character**; the obsession is real; the story earns the next minute.

**North star:** *We make car lovers feel understood, and everyone else wish they were one.*

## Status and operating assumptions
- Editorial identity: approved in principle by owner 2026-10-20; this documentation codifies the brief.
- New standalone Green Knight entertainment workspace; **no YouTube channel creation, editor engagement, narrator approval, commissioning, video publication, automation enrollment or asset clearance is implied**.
- Default long-form film: **10 minutes target; 8-12 minutes acceptable when story warrants**, subject to revision after actual narrator timing and pilot feedback.
- Global and English-speaking mainstream audience: automotive enthusiasts **and** curious casual viewers.
- Lean, faceless, high-production-value research and narration documentaries. No requirement to own, drive, film or modify vehicles.
- Working channel title: **Car Lore**; brand availability/trademark clearance not established.
- Working idea/episode identifiers: `CL-I####` and `CL-V####` for this manual workspace. Do not claim a permanent `CH###` ID or registration in the automated portfolio.
