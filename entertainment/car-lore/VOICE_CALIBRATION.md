# Car Lore narrator audition and calibration

**Status:** Creative voice identity specified; no voice chosen or authenticated. This document is an audition brief and spoken test material, not a finished episode.

## Narrator profile
Natural, charismatic, warm, articulate, entertaining English-language documentary performer. Their smile and delight should be audible without sounding like they are faking automotive expertise. A storyteller with timing, a conversational gearhead's obsessive attention and enough range to get sincerely quiet. Avoid monotonous TTS, loud trailer narration, exaggerated radio-ad energy and imitation of a specific recognizable presenter.

## Audition plan
Record all three passages using the **same** candidate voice. Evaluate warmth, comedic rhythm, how they say car names and technical details, emotional range, speed, articulation, excitement without yelling, and listener trust. Owner selects actual voice based on listening.

### Test A: wonder and joy
"Just look at it. The gullwing doors. The stainless-steel body. The impossible shape. The DeLorean looked like it came from the future, which might explain why, decades later, people still stop and stare. It wasn't perfect. Far from it. And somehow, that makes the story better."

Performance: impressed but spontaneous; allow an affectionate pause after visual details. Not a movie trailer.

### Test B: mischief and sharpness
"Imagine spending years building the ultimate dream car. The designs are gorgeous. The promises are enormous. Every car magazine is watching. There's just one small issue: building a beautiful car and building a successful car company turn out to be two very different sports."

Performance: a smile, cheeky restraint, clear punchline, never mocking owners or victims.

### Test C: quiet affection
"Every generation has a car that lives on bedroom walls long after the original posters come down. Sometimes it's the fastest thing on the road. Sometimes it never really lived up to the promise. But it made someone fall in love with cars. And for them, it was perfect."

Performance: reflective, intimate, grounded; not sentimental theater.

These are original illustrative lines. They do not establish specific historical claims, license visual/sound assets or approve a voice.

## Scoring (1-5 each)
Authentic enthusiast energy; easy listening for a nonfan; range across three tests; comedic timing; quiet emotional control; proper pronunciation; natural pacing; consistent identity. Reject if any candidate gets full-range charisma only by shouting. Save provider, voice ID, version, sample references and approved settings **only after** human selection.
