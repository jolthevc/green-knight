# Car Lore Episode 003 - Ferrari vs Lamborghini

**Working title:** How a Tractor Maker Challenged Ferrari
**Episode:** CL-V0003 | Candidate: CL-I0003
**Status:** V1 narration draft for owner review. No narrator/editor production approval or rights clearance.
**Length:** 2341 spoken words | preferred 15-minute delivered runtime (actual timed narration required).
**Story promise:** What if the famous Enzo Ferrari insult is only the folklore? A tractor industrialist with a vision of a more usable GT assembled an extraordinary team that created the 350 GT, then pushed far beyond his expectations into the Miura and Countach.
**Opening:** Iconic insult story, immediately questioned by conflicting insider testimony; documentary shifts to verifiable ambition, founder philosophy, talent poaching and the extraordinary engineer-led Miura project.
**Major surprise:** Founder opposed racing; his engineers brought racing ideas to the road. A bare chassis with a rear transverse V12 attracted attention at the 1965 Turin show, then Marcello Gandini and Bertone shaped the Miura.
**Crisis:** Commercial adversity in early 1970s, Ferruccio sells control and exits before the Countach production era.
**Emotional ending:** Rivalry wasn't simply Enzo insult versus Ferruccio revenge. Ferrari's racing-first mythology and Lamborghini's audacious GT/performance vision gave car lovers two enduring icons.
