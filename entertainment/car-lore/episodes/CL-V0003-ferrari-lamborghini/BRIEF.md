**Core question:** How did a man famous for tractors challenge Ferrari, help create the Miura and Countach, and then leave before the Countach went on sale?

**Open:** November 1965 bare Lamborghini Miura chassis is the first picture, not a lengthy Ferrari biography. Name Lamborghini, Ferrari, and the founder's future exit before asking why.
**Act one:** Contested insult, founder identity and engineering excellence, imperfect 1963 350 GTV, successful 350 GT 1964.
**Act two:** The Dallara-Stanzani-Wallace mid-engine team and transverse V12 design; terrifying bare skeleton 1965, glorious finished Miura at Geneva 1966.
**Act three:** Miura's contradictory beauty/temperament, 1969 cinema, Countach 1971 design break, 1970s financial pressure, exit before LP400 1974.
**Emotional promise:** Supercar beauty can be born from the strange meeting of practical machines, wild engineers, design obsession, and impossible risks.

**Big Car x Modern MBA visuals:** Maps, Enzo and historic race imagery, Ferruccio tractor and heating equipment, 350 GTV show sketches, Bizzarrini V12, 350 GT, historical service scenes clearly credited, Dallara/Stanzani/Wallace, authentic 1965 black rolling chassis, Marcello Gandini sketches, Miura hero light and audio, `The Italian Job` reference if licensable, Countach 1971 LP500, business/tractor/1972 sale contexts, original LP400 1974.

**Must not fake:** Ferruccio's private internal statements; disputed quote as verbatim fact; Ferrari losing a specific road test; made-up Miura driving experience; unlicensed movie/engine media; falsely treating Ferruccio's staff as rebels against him.
