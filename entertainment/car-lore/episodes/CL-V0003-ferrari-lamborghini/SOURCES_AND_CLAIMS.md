## Deliberate factual safeguards
- **The Enzo/Ferruccio insult:** The precise encounter and quote are disputed; Balboni reports hearing Ferruccio tell it and Wallace reportedly disputes it. The script clearly frames it as a contested legend rather than authenticated verbatim dialogue.
- **Miura inspiration:** Lamborghini records support Dallara/Stanzani/Wallace working up a mid-engine road car, not actual customer-commissioned racing for the company. Avoid dramatized boardroom scenes or invented quotes.
- **350 GTV vs 350 GT:** 1963 show concept and 1964 production car are distinct. Franco Scaglione styled the prototype; Carrozzeria Touring refined the production car, while Bizzarrini and later engineers shaped the V12.
- **Miura 350 metric horsepower:** Lamborghini's P400 figure in *CV* is not identical to SAE mechanical bhp; keep "metric horsepower".
- **'First supercar':** A contested retrospective classification. Script says Miura 'helped establish the template,' not absolute priority.
- **Countach:** Prototype 1971; LP400 production began 1974 per detailed Lamborghini chronology. Avoid stating Countach production began in 1971.
- **Ferranccio's exit:** sold 51% in 1972 and left by 1974, amid multiple business pressures; neither 'Ferrari bankrupted him' nor 'oil shock alone did it' is justified.
- **Contrast with Ferrari:** An interpretive comparison of founder priorities, not literal assertion that Ferrari road cars were not designed for comfort or that Ferrari didn't build exotic mid-engine cars.
- **Visual licensing:** Manufacturer photography/footage, historical auto show content, *The Italian Job* movie scenes, factory footage, magazine pages, interview audio and engine recordings need source-specific rights vetting.
