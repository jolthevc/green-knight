## V1 diagnosis
1. **Weak initial narrative acceleration.** The first 3 minutes deferred the instantly visual Miura/Countach promise and repeated the disputed Ferrari insult twice. Worse, the film began with the disputed anecdote and later treated it again as the reason to care.
2. **Chronological exposition masquerading as drama.** A linear explanation of Enzo, Ferruccio, farm machinery, prototype, first GT and designer names kept building but withheld the best automotive images.
3. **Mechanical lead-up lacked a ticking question.** The engineers' radical chassis and 1966 Miura were strong, but the viewer needed to see the strange chassis at the outset to care about how it became beautiful.
4. **Over-explanatory phrases.** Remove repeated 'imagine', 'the remarkable thing', 'look at this thing', generic awe, and caveats that stop the story.
5. **Late-film loss wasn't planted.** Ferruccio's 1970s departure was strong but had no early setup; V2 flags at start he would leave before the Countach hit production.
6. **Factual nuance.** V1 claimed Ferruccio was initially skeptical of the Miura. Manufacturer's later historical account says he recognized its potential and approved it; V2 avoids contradicting that source or asserting an internal rebellion.

## V2 revised dramatic spine
1. **0:00–1:00** Bare 1965 Miura chassis: shocking visual; tractor maker founded company; Countach exit foreshadowed; Ferrari insult introduced as *disputed*.
2. **1:00–4:00** Parallel definitions of an exceptional car: Enzo racing prestige, Ferruccio operator pragmatism, 1963 startup, Bizzarrini V12, early prototype trouble and usable 350 GT. Mini-reveal: customer care by plane.
3. **4:00–8:00** Young engineers' transverse mid-engine challenge; display the bare chassis and engineer ingenuity, then 1966 Miura visual payoff, sound and mechanical reality.
4. **8:00–10:30** The founder's mission morphs into something more theatrical; Miura becomes celebrity/film culture, later Countach LP500 is an aesthetic reversal and a new icon.
5. **10:30–13:30** Countach's arrival coincides with financial distress in tractor and car businesses; majority-stake sale and owner exit, LP400 production after he leaves.
6. **13:30–15:00** Return to dispute: the verified engineering and cars matter more than the insult; end on earned affection for the extraordinary machines.

## Quality check
- **YouTube viewer:** Hook establishes a car, historical question, rivalry and an impending founder exit in under a minute.
- **Nonfan:** Explains GT, transverse engine, roles of engineers; minimal alphabet soup.
- **Car lover:** Miura aesthetic pause, original 350 GT refinement, visual/auditory detail, Countach shape and doors, real engineering significance.
- **Voice:** Personal but not imitative; alternate fast jokes and moments of serious awe; no false 'I drove' claims.
- **Editor feasibility:** Integrates engineering drawings, show floor, working lives and markets; premium Big Car + Modern MBA documentary, not car-photo wallpaper. Some rare image rights may be hard.
- **Remaining risks:** Narrator pace must be timed; source-checked asset plan required; original Miura chassis visual needs properly licensed rights; no invented dialogue.
