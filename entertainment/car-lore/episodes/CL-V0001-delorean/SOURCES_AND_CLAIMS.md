## Open review points
- Script is an interpretation and must not overstate that *specific* design features caused the company's failure; it presents multiple linked factors.
- Historic factory employment aspirations are not claims of actual jobs created.
- The film did not rescue the **original** manufacturer; distinguish later independent DeLorean businesses.
- Period horsepower and performance are US-market/test-specific.
- Crime coverage must preserve arrest, defense and full acquittal accurately.
- Film clips, engine samples, period car ads, automotive press photos and archival footage need a rights determination. They are not presumed licensed.
