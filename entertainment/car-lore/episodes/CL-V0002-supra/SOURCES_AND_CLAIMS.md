## Verification watch-outs
- The $550,000 figure refers to **one authenticated screen-used car in a June 2021 auction**, not the price of a typical Supra. Auction listing documents four-speed automatic and turbo 2JZ. The movie depicted a fictional 'ten-second car'; do not represent all prop/stunt vehicles as the same specification.
- 1993 4.6-second acceleration is the result of **one March 1993 Car and Driver test**, while a different comparison-test car took 5.2 seconds. Use exact attribution.
- Toyota's own US history reports **a little over 11,200 fourth-gen units sold US**. The A80 left **US market by 1998** but **Japan production lasted to 2002**. Do not claim all manufacture ceased in 1998.
- Toyota did not sell a reliably 1000hp factory engine. Big tuned outputs require substantial modifications and expert calibration.
- 950 meetings, 124lb weight savings and hollow fibers are attributed to the contemporary Car and Driver interview with Isao Tsuzuki; verify rendering of engineer comments.
- Gaming, enthusiast tuning and the Fast films are mutually reinforcing cultural forces; **do not imply one singular definitive cause** or quantified causal sales effect.
- Avoid illegal street-racing glamorization and respect safety. All film clips, game play clips, catalogs, advertisements and car photography require rights/provenance check.
- Fan reaction to 2019 BMW collaboration is nuanced, not a verified single consensus. Attribute differences of opinion.
