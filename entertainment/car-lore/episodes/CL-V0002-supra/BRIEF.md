# Car Lore Episode CL-V0002: Toyota Supra

**Working title:** How the Toyota Supra Became an Obsession

**Status:** Revised V2 draft, pending owner script/title/voice approval and production rights review.

**Length:** 2306 spoken words; target ~15 minutes, verify by recorded delivery and sound/design holds.

**Central promise:** A niche, expensive 1993 Toyota sports car vanished from U.S. showrooms before global pop culture turned it into an obsession. We explain why the mechanics, tuner community, video games, cinema and later collectibility reinforced each other.

**Opening:** 2021 $550,000 movie-car auction; hero vehicle has a four-speed automatic despite 'ten-second car' mythology. Explain that this is a particular film-used car, not typical A80 value.

**Middle reveal:** 950 weight-loss meetings, hollow-fiber carpet, 2JZ-GTE construction, 1993 on-road comparison win. Despite spectacular engineering, the high-priced A80 struggled to sustain American demand; sales stopped in U.S. by 1998 even though production continued in Japan until 2002. Enthusiast interpretation evolved through tuning and culture.

**Late surprise:** Real tuner and technical adviser Craig Lieberman's own car became the recognizable film hero vehicle; movie didn't invent the 2JZ or tuning scene. Revisit four-speed auto auction myth versus car reality.

**Final turn:** 2019 GR Supra comeback, Akio Toyoda personal attachment, BMW joint development, passionate fan debate about authenticity. Conclusion: the machine became an open canvas for other people's dreams.

**Editing model:** Big Car archival + Modern MBA human/cultural storytelling. Film is NOT a slideshow of Supras. Show 1993 rivals and road test spread, Tsuzuki engineering, manufacturing/testing, 2JZ block and sequential turbo schematics, Japanese tuners/owners/culture, PlayStation generation, Craig Lieberman and movie production, 2021 auction, Akio Toyoda and 2019 vehicle. Keep vehicle beauty/sound moments; clearance for every third-party item, game capture, movie clip and music.

**Suggested thumbnail:** Real orange 1994 movie-era Supra prominently foregrounded with a small auction placard '$550,000' and a visual bridge to a factory-correct A80. Do not imply any ordinary Supra is worth that figure.

**Editorial self-check:** First 30 seconds has shock, fact, contradiction, emotional question. Middle has engineering detail, test payoff and declining-sales reversal. Late act contains movie production provenance and automatics twist. Closing preserves fanatic car-love. Risks: 2019 return becomes overly brief or fan-polarizing; make it a short thematic payoff rather than a new documentary.

**Required before editor packet:** Owner approval, independent fact pass, actual 15-minute voice run-through, scene-specific third-party-rights assessment, exact narrator/visual director files.
