**Story:** Humvee engineering created a striking, purposeful form. Schwarzenegger helped popularize the H1; GM remade its image as a usable H2, which became an early-2000s celebrity status symbol. The same visibility triggered polarizing reactions; fuel prices, 2008 market pressures and GM's bankruptcy contributed to the brand's 2010 wind-down. GMC resurrected its outsized attitude as an EV.

**Opening:** 2003 LeBron James and three-TV Hummer H2, seats embroidered King James, Gloria's documented bank loan and eligibility clearance. GM's paid 2003 Hummer music-video placement previews the status strategy.

**Visual tone:** Big Car genuine H1/H2 machinery, period magazine testing and car hero beats. Modern MBA documentary imagery of military procurement, celebrity culture, GM product economics, H3 sales share, 2008 downturn, 2010 deal failure and electric GMC return. Never a slideshow of Hummers; minimal purposeful charts. All third-party footage must be cleared.
