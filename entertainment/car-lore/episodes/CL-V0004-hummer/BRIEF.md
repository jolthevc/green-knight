# Car Lore Episode 004 — Hummer
**Working title:** How the Hummer Became the SUV America Loved to Hate
**Idea ID:** CL-I0004. **Episode ID:** CL-V0004. **Status:** V1 owner-review narration draft.
**Length:** 2280 words, about 15–17 min depending on narrator and atmospheric holds. Shorten after human timed read if necessary.
**Emotional engine:** A military utility vehicle creates an improbable civilian culture machine. H1's authentic capabilities transform into H2's celebrity conspicuous consumption, provoke backlash, crash with the market, and reappear as an extreme EV.
**Hook:** Contemporaneous 2003 LeBron 18th-birthday H2 with 3 TVs triggers amateur scrutiny. The very same year, GM pays $300k for H2s in rap video. Bring in the mid-2000s brand peak, backlash and unexpected EV rebirth.
**Reveal one:** Arnold and Desert Storm coverage accelerate interest in authentic civilian AM General H1. H1 character: portal-geared hubs, rough-country purpose, shockingly compromised city comfort.
**Reveal two:** H2 is substantially re-engineered civilian GM truck, not military Humvee dressed up. Yet still honestly capable off-road; marquee value becomes **being seen**.
**Reveal three:** H2 creates culture, the H3 (not H2) drives 2006 volume peak; fuel economics and GM's finances reverse fortunes. 2010 end occurs after attempted sale fails.
**Ending:** GMC HUMMER EV goes electric without becoming modest; up to 1000 hp, ~3s, ~9000 lb, CrabWalk. What survives is the defiantly unmistakable shape/attitude.
**Visual approach:** Big Car archive + Modern MBA explaining people, SUVs, military spec vs civilian platform, celebrity coverage, GM promotion economics, 2006 H3/H2 model sales divergence, 2008 fuel prices and bankruptcy timeline; Hummer hero footage and mechanical engineering where earned.
**No-go:** Car picture slideshow, generic war montage, unlicensed combat/celebrity/rap video footage, fabricated specific emotional reactions by real buyers. Treat climate impacts and activism seriously without caricature.
**Owner gate:** Evaluate pacing and script V2 before release; source and rights inventory; audition British/American Car Lore narrators; 01/02/03 editor packet after approval.
