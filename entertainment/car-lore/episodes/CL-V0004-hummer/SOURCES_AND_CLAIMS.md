## Critical boundaries
- Arnold Schwarzenegger is documented as an **early civilian buyer and advocate**. Avoid asserting he alone caused AM General to build the civilian Hummer; there were commercial motives and multiple customers.
- Differentiate US Army military **Humvee/HMMWV**, AM General civilian **H1** (introduced 1992), GM-developed and AM General-manufactured **H2** (2002 production, 2003 model year), GM **H3** (2005), and **GMC HUMMER EV** (first deliveries 2021). H2 is *not* an H1 or a military Humvee on a plush interior.
- Original Humvee military roles, protection and survivability evolved over time; avoid presenting it as an armored tank or making combat safety claims.
- H1 center tire inflation, portal geared hubs and terrain engineering details must be checked against the specific vehicle variants used in visuals.
- $300,000 figure from 2003 Los Angeles Times is a particular Ms. Jade music-video placement (not generalized Hummer advertising spend).
- LeBron incident is from contemporaneous AP report: high-school eligibility investigation was **cleared**. Birthday timing corrected: **early 2003 he was 18**; he turned 18 in December 2002.
- H2 contemporary test logged 10.7s 0-60, 244ft 70-0 and ~10 mpg observed. Heavy-duty EPA ratings/test conventions differ, so script correctly attributes observed figure to road test, not an official EPA rating.
- The 2006 brand sales peak (71,524 USA) was principally **H3**, not H2. H2 volume had declined from its 2003 peak. Be precise.
- 2008+ decline is multicausal: gas prices, recession, brand polarization, GM restructuring. No unsupported claim that criticism alone killed Hummer.
- Illegal arson/vandalism is unequivocally condemned and used sparingly; no glamorization of violent acts or assertion of owners as a monolith.
- The 2010 wind-down was civilian **Hummer brand**, not AM General's separate military Humvee business. No claim vehicle support disappeared overnight.
- New electric model is **GMC HUMMER EV**, not revival of the defunct original as an independent automaker. It's electric, zero tailpipe emissions, but still consumes upstream energy/materials. Up to 1000 hp and ~3sec only on equipped trims.
- **Unlicensed archival news, filmed music-video, LeBron shots, military combat footage, advertisements, real engine sounds and photographs are not licensed** simply by appearing on websites or search results.
