# Hummer Episode 004: First-principles V2 editorial review

**Status:** 2,137-word V2 draft for owner approval, targeting about 15 minutes after real narration timing.

**Why V1 was weaker:** The excellent LeBron hook led into too much military history; H1/H2 engineering felt like independent exposition, while celebrity, backlash, sales collapse and EV rebirth arrived sequentially rather than creating a dramatic contradiction.

**V2 improvements:** Opening adds the documented bank loan, King James seat embroidery and official clearance. The military section focuses on engineering that explains the appearance. GM's civilian-platform H2 contrasts authentic off-road ability with its main commercial power: visibility. Period road-test figures are explicitly test-specific. Paid music placement and LeBron explain celebrity aspiration. The same public visibility creates polarization. A contemporary reviewer's hostile windscreen note gives the backlash a concrete scene. The smaller H3, not the H2, powered most of the 2006 brand sales peak. Gas prices, wider demand and GM insolvency—not critics alone—set up the failed 2010 sale. GMC HUMMER EV returns with extreme power, weight and CrabWalk, completing the visibility theme.

**Car Lore voice:** Conversational, affectionate, witty, not anti-Hummer. Real car-love beats: geared hubs, adjustable tires, genuine Baja H2 ability. No invented driving experience, fake celebrity quote or dramatic dialogue.

**Producer QA:** LeBron amateur clearance and Gloria's loan are documented; period H2 test recorded 10.7-second 0-60, 6,700 lb and 244-foot stop. H3 model-level sales are secondary-source figures and need final verification. Arnold is an early owner, not the sole originator. H1, H2, H3 and GMC HUMMER EV remain distinct. Film/news/celebrity/automotive assets need per-item copyright review.

**Packaging:** Main title: How the Hummer Became the SUV America Loved to Hate. Alternative: The Hummer Was America's Ultimate Flex. Then It Collapsed. Thumbnail idea: enormous H2 with early-2000s celebrity culture versus 2008 fuel-price/newspaper imagery, copy LOVED. THEN HATED.
