# Audience, editorial universe and topic greenlight

## Target audiences
1. **Car enthusiast, all ages:** knows and loves machines; wants informed affection, details, controversies and real stories, not another generic spec dump.
2. **Car-curious mainstream viewer:** knows Ferrari, Porsche, Tesla, Lamborghini, Hummer or a movie-famous car; clicks for the irresistible human story even without knowing the jargon.
3. **Nostalgia / status / culture viewer:** cares about periods, aspiration, fashion, celebrities, games, cinema and identity, whether or not they follow automotive media.
4. **International viewers:** stories should travel when cultural context is explained. Avoid treating US-only car habits as universal.

## Content pillars
- **Car Legends:** how a specific model became mythic: Supra, F40, Skyline, 911, Mustang, Civic.
- **Auto Obsession:** why the Hummer, Range Rover, pickups, supercars or tuner icons captured imaginations.
- **Rivalries & Battles:** founders, designs, motorsport-inspired road cars, manufacturers and competing visions.
- **Beautiful Disasters:** bizarre, beautiful failures that remained interesting or beloved.
- **People Behind the Metal:** designers, engineers, racers, founders and collectors, with the vehicle central.
- **Street & Fan Culture:** tuners, JDM, posters, auctions, collector communities, road trips and car subcultures.
- **Dream Garage:** extraordinary aspiration, exclusivity, collectibles and symbols of status, while staying grounded in car passion.
- **Strange Automotive Lore:** little-known episodes, daring gambles, unlikely champions and disputed legends explained carefully.

## Series concept filter
Each candidate must have:
- **Recognition or instantly comprehensible spectacle.** The viewer understands in one beat why the object is worth a click.
- **A concrete mystery or reversal**, with an actual answer.
- **An emotional engine** such as ambition, obsession, rivalry, aspiration, unexpected love, betrayal or redemption.
- **At least two meaningful developments** after the opening, so the story doesn't die at minute three.
- **A car-love moment** that a genuine enthusiast would recognize as affectionate and informed.
- **A credible sourcing plan and a workable, rights-aware visual plan** at lean budgets.
- **A broadly intelligible hook**, not only powertrain jargon.
- **A differentiated takeaway** beyond famous facts.
If one essential element is absent, reframe, research or reject.

## Strong story angles vs traps
Strong: "Why this seemingly impractical machine became a generation's dream car."
Weak: "Complete history of model X." Strong: "How a rivalry produced a car nobody thought possible." Weak: "All ten variants ranked."
Strong: "Why collectors still obsess over a difficult, flawed car." Weak: "The ten most expensive cars."
Strong: "What happened when a manufacturer bet the company on a vehicle enthusiasts hated, then loved." Weak: "Quarterly earnings explained" with cars as decoration.

## Portfolio boundaries
- A story whose main purpose is revealing an industry's incentives usually fits Behind Everything.
- Celebrity wealth and deal structure fit Fame & Fortune.
- Internet memes about cars fit Internet Famous unless **the machine and its enthusiast culture** are central.
- Pure failure analysis fits Fallen Angels unless the tragedy, car and passionate legacy are the real center.
- Reviews, new model news, purchase recommendations, mechanical tutorials and track tests are NOT the core format.

## Greenlight scorecard (1-5, subjective internal guidance)
- Instant curiosity and broad consumer recognition
- Human/emotional narrative engine
- Viral title + single-image thumbnail potential
- Enthusiast authenticity and car-worship opportunities
- Surprise, twists and payoff density
- Reusable archive availability and licensed production feasibility
- Distinctiveness from existing Green Knight channels
- Evergreen and potential for repeat-viewer affinity

Reject the candidate if the enthusiast credibility, evidence or honest click promise fails even when aggregate attractiveness is high.
