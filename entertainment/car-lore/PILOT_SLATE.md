# Car Lore: candidate pilot slate

**All titles are exploratory pitches, not verified historical claims, commissioned episodes, or approved scripts.** Each needs fresh primary/reliable sourcing, factual reframing, an authentic rights-aware imagery/sound strategy, and owner selection.

## Proposed testing mix, not production sequence
- **Instant recognizable nostalgia:** DeLorean, Hummer, Mustang, Civic.
- **Core enthusiast devotion:** Supra, McLaren F1, Skyline, Ferrari F40.
- **Rivalry and controversy:** Lamborghini vs Ferrari, Porsche Cayenne.
- **Accessible surprise:** Miata, Beetle, WRX STI.

## Launch recommendation
For rapid consumer growth, compare **CL-I0001 DeLorean**, **CL-I0004 Hummer** and **CL-I0002 Supra** as candidate first stories. Choose based on the strongest **verified** opening, enough unknown-to-expert evidence and rights-feasible hero assets. Do not equate first in the table with owner approval.

## Commercial hypothesis
Cars have a passionate, repeatable and visually attractive fan base with potential auto, enthusiast, collectibles and lifestyle sponsors, but no sponsor is committed and no RPM or performance is forecast. Start with compelling evergreen stories, not dependence on auto-industry news.
