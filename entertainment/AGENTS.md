## Principle
Internet Famous is not a biography recap, weekly gossip feed, influencer hit piece, or marketing explainer. Every film must tell a highly watchable internet-culture story while uncovering a sourced, non-obvious mechanism of fame, distribution, participation, audience conversion, platform incentives, culture or identity.

Optimize the title, thumbnail, first frame and initial 30 seconds for maximum truthful curiosity. Salacious and dramatic is encouraged in *delivery*, NEVER license to invent allegations, sexual details, crimes, motives, dialogue, private messages, algorithm access, statistics, or scandals.

## Default production interface
Target 15 minutes. Accepted 10-20 minutes. Do not inherit the business workspace's 8-10 minute check or validator without deliberate adaptation.

For a selected episode: research -> provisional package/hypothesis -> beat outline and claim map -> full spoken script -> full narration director -> full visual director -> independent and joint quality review -> owner approves final script and voice -> release editor packet -> inspect actual video.

The released editor packet contains EXACTLY:
- 01_SCRIPT.txt: exact approved spoken body, with a non-spoken TITLE header and no stage directions.
- 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md: exact full script reproduced by labeled segment, with separate clearly NON-SPOKEN voice cues, pauses, intensity, inflection and section purpose.
- 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md: one selected title, thumbnail, first frame and cold open plus paragraph-linked segment/scene maps, visual movement, evidence and sound directions.

Segment labels appear in 02 and 03 to avoid contaminating TTS. Source/evidence, internal segmented outline, ideation and review reports remain outside the editor packet. Do not release the packet if factual research, spoken-copy consistency, voice choice, owner script approval, thumbnail truth, rights plan, or cross-file review is incomplete.

Do not invent current events, internet-famous persons' biographies, precise metrics or causation. Browse primary sources for facts and current context when developing a real episode. A producer-authored first draft is unapproved until reviewed and expressly approved by the owner.

Never send messages to a producer, order work, claim contract terms, pay, publish or modify unrelated business records without instruction. Follow the actual current repository, not memories of what was in it. When done, report exact files committed and what remains not approved.
