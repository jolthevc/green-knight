# The Rabbit Hole

Green Knight entertainment documentary channel. **Editorial status: approved concept; no videos commissioned or published yet.**

## Audience promise
The strangest true stories, mysteries, and unexplained events in the world. Every episode takes a mainstream viewer somewhere unexpected and makes them want to watch another.

## Non-negotiable priority: ENTERTAINMENT
Not a Wikipedia summary, generic educational explainer, sensationalist conspiracy channel, or padded chronology. **Harvard MBA-level research, Netflix-level entertainment, YouTube-native pacing.** The mystery earns the click; the story earns the watch time. The opening 15-30 seconds must create an irresistible question. Each subsequent minute must deliver a new reveal, twist, escalating stake, or meaningful answer. Zero filler. End with a satisfying, truthful payoff, including when uncertainty remains.

## Scope
Real, documented mysteries; bizarre historical events; internet rabbit holes; unusual disappearances; codes and puzzles; extraordinary unsolved stories; strange places and phenomena. Avoid drifting into routine true crime, baseless paranormal claims, and unsupported conspiracy theories. Treat victims respectfully; distinguish evidence, inference, and speculation.

## Format and voice
- Target 10-15 minutes; extend to 20 only when the story earns it. Never pad.
- Weekly release aspiration: four episodes per month.
- Curious, cinematic, suspenseful, accessible and intelligent narrator. Clear stakes, characters, and narrative momentum.
- Archival footage, photos, maps, public records, headlines, location B-roll. Minimal polished animation only when needed to explain.
- Editor target: ~$60/video, excluding research, licensing, tools and other costs. Not a confirmed quote.
- Write and research scripts, select title/thumbnail direction, then hand off editor packet. Confirm rights and factual sourcing.

## Quality gate
1. Would a complete newcomer click based on title and thumbnail?
2. Does the first 30 seconds demand the next minute?
3. Does every scene advance the narrative or surprise the viewer?
4. Are uncertainty and competing theories accurately represented?
5. Is there a compelling ending rather than a fake answer?
6. Does the final video feel worth sharing and bingeing?

## Pilot sequence (proposed)
1. Cicada 3301: the internet's mysterious recruitment puzzles.
2. D. B. Cooper: the hijacker who disappeared.
3. Centralia: the town with an underground mine fire.
