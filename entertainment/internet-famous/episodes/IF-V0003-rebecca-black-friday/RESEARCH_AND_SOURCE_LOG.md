## Legal, ethics and clearance cautions
Original video and music rights are not automatically cleared. The June 2011 dispute is described as contested, with no implied resolution. Subject was 13 in 2011: no mocking edits, harassment montages, dubious invented comments, or insinuating personal allegations. Emotional-transmission research is context, not evidence of YouTube's specific causal ranking.
