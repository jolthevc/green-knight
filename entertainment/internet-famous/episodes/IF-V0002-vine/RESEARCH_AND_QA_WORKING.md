## Core qualifications
- The $1.2m-per-person amount is a **reported proposal** by 18 Viners in 2015, not an accepted payment or uniquely proven cause of shutdown. The package also asked for better features, safety and communication.
- Some top creators earned outside brand income. Vine lacked comparable robust direct in-platform revenue sharing/support, not all creator income.
- The 1600 Vine network featured creative collaboration **and** concentrated amplification, with platform staff and creator recollections sometimes differing.
- Reported downloads falling are not synonymous with the number of users, lifetime accounts, daily activity or video loops.
- Instagram, Snapchat, internal strategy and monetization combined; nobody publicly proved a one-variable explanation.
- Creator behavior and legacy should be represented fairly. Rights/copyright and privacy review is a separate prerequisite.
