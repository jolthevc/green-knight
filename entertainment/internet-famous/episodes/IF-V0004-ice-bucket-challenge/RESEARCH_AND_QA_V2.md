## Fact-check gates
- Chris Kennedy did not invent generic ice-water challenges. The family relay to ALS in mid-July 2014 is the relevant claimed origin.
- 17M = Facebook challenge-related videos shared; 10B = video plays; 440M = people reached in the Jun 1 to Sep 1 period.
- $115M = ALS Association 2014 US donations; $220M+ = amount across organizations worldwide.
- 2015 $500K = 2015 Aug report then-to-date for repeat, not all-year or all ALS giving. Denominators are unequal.
- 2024 $154.7M/560 projects is 10 years of research awards, not one-to-one spending from $115M. Clinic network expansion not purely ascribed causally to the challenge.
- 2022 drug Relyvrio/AMX0035 was funded in part by $2.2M from challenge receipts, then withdrawn Apr 2024 following failed phase 3. There is no ALS cure as of the present draft.
- In July 2026 the ALS Association described hundreds of videos for a revival connected to Chris Johnson and Hunter Mecum. No proof of 2014-scale comeback.
- No ongoing fraud or criminal allegation; all participants treated respectfully.
