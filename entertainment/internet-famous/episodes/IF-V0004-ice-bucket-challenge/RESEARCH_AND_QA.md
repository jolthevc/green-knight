## Fact and wording gates
- $115M refers to U.S. ALS Association; $220M+ global includes other organizations.
- 17M Facebook videos, 10B video plays, 440M viewers are different metrics.
- 2015 revival $500K is the late-August report, not all 2015 ALS fundraising.
- Ice-water stunt existed before ALS-specific Chris Kennedy relay.
- 2024 $155M grants refer to decade-long funding from multiple financial sources; neither proof all came from $115M nor proof all observed impact would not otherwise have happened.
- Relyvrio won FDA approval in 2022 but was voluntarily withdrawn in 2024 after larger failed trial; absolutely no claims of a cure.
- Human subjects and health suffering must not be mocked or exploited; no false quotes/AI recreations presented as news.
