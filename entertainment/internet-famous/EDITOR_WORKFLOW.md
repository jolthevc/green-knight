# Editor collaboration, packaging and delivery workflow

## Working relationships
Two supported models:
A. Green Knight researches, scripts and directs; Fiverr editor produces from approved three-file packet.
B. Fiverr producer researches and drafts the full first script; Green Knight reviews, fact-checks, revises, directs; owner approves exact full script and narrator BEFORE full recording or edit.

Do not presume agreement with any editor or price, revision policy, timing, recording access or purchased batch.

## Commissioning steps
1. Owner selects concept. Draft 8-12 title options internally, test first-30-second cold opens, one source-grounded angle, proposed thumbnail and 15-minute scope.
2. Research and produce full internally segmented script with source ledger and claim map.
3. Select ONE final title/thumbnail/hook, pending owner approval; write complete script, not just an opening sample.
4. Create complete narrator and visual directors; independent script, voice, visual and integrated QA.
5. Owner expressly approves final script and actual narrator/voice audition.
6. Release exactly three documents to editor in editor-packet/:
   - 01_SCRIPT.txt: clean, exact spoken script
   - 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md: segment-labeled, full verbatim spoken copy + unmistakably non-spoken performance direction
   - 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md: selected thumbnail, first frame, cold open, scene map, transitions, sourcing notes
7. Request integrated 60-90 second actual pilot sample showing voice, first-frame clarity, graphic style, visual change and sound. This is a proposed test, not commissioned yet.
8. Review pilot, then approve full editing; evaluate finished 10-20 minute cut, sound, rights, content, factual screen text and exact spoken copy.
9. Consolidate revision notes. Approve only the actual final output; publication is a separate owner decision.
10. Use publish analytics to evolve packaging, not to overwrite facts.

## Exactly five standard final deliverables
1. Final MP4 video, preferably 1080p or better, 16:9.
2. Clickworthy custom thumbnail.
3. Clean narration audio track.
4. SEO package with approved YouTube title(s), description, relevant tags and chapter timestamps.
5. Synced SRT subtitles.

Source links, rights/provenance records and editable project source files should be retained or supplied as separately agreed. Never imply third-party assets are cleared merely because the editor could download them.

## What the editor must know
- 15 minutes is the target, not permission to stretch.
- Every act has a clear dramatic job and approximate timing.
- The first 5 seconds must land, and first 30 seconds must fulfill the title/thumbnail.
- Music, voice intensity and visuals should distinguish setup, spread, mechanism, fallout, reversal and payoff.
- Headlines/posts/quotes must be authentic, dated and accurately depicted.
- Thumbnail and title are not optional late-stage add-ons. Build opening around them.
- The words in 01 are fixed; any material rewrite returns to owner/editorial approval.
- Do not voice 'TITLE:', act labels, timecodes, paragraph IDs or production notes.
- Do not unilaterally introduce allegations, invented reactions, exaggerated stats or staged evidence.
- Editor can propose a simpler effect while preserving what needs to be communicated.

## Post-cut review
Score initial 30 seconds, weakest middle stretch, turning points, clarity for a newcomer, depth for an informed viewer, natural narration, music, footage rights, script sync, on-screen text and ending. Real attention metrics after publication are observational and not guaranteed.
