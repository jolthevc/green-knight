# Internet Famous: initial story slate (unresearched candidates)

Every title below is a provisional creative direction, NOT an established factual finding or approval to produce. **People and phenomena are equal-status subjects.** All concepts must pass [respectful coverage](PEOPLE_AND_FAIRNESS.md) and the [broad scope standard](SCOPE_AND_STORY_TYPES.md).
