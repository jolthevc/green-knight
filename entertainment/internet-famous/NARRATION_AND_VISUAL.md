# Sound, narration and visual identity

## People and phenomena
The central subject may be a person OR a viral phenomenon, meme, challenge, video, audio clip, community, game or platform behavior. Visualize the actual spread, remixing and consequences. Never use mockery of real subjects, humiliating reaction edits, insulting performance or misleading crime/controversy implications. [Human dignity and fairness](PEOPLE_AND_FAIRNESS.md) governs all scripts, performances and visuals.

## Narration direction
Natural, confident, intimate, premium documentary storytelling. The voice should feel like a smart friend who knows something astonishing, not a synthetic movie trailer or an aggressive gossip commentator.

Dynamic profile:
- Hook: immediately interesting, measured disbelief, tightly controlled urgency.
- Context: warm, transparent and accessible.
- Escalation: shorter phrasing, rising tension and movement.
- Mechanism/reveal: clear, deliberate and specific; allow the listener to understand before moving on.
- Consequences: appropriate empathy, emotional restraint rather than melodramatic condemnation. Never caricature the real subject's speech or suffering.
- Reversal: sharpen contrast and leave room after the key fact.
- Closing: satisfying, quiet conviction, no preachiness.

The initial narrator/provider/model/voice has NOT been selected or approved. Do not invent a voice ID. Once selected, store a versioned profile and reference audio, and compare full-length delivery before production batch.

## Editor script segmentation convention
Each Sxx has:
- Dramatic purpose and planned approximate time range
- Exact opening and ending P### narration anchors
- Intensity and cadence note
- 1-3 meaningful stress words (not ALL CAPS in spoken copy)
- Optional silence / reset if justified
- What the listener should understand and anticipate afterward

02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR must contain the entire script verbatim in grouped sections marked 'SPOKEN COPY' and keep cue paragraphs separately labeled 'NON-SPOKEN'. Never feed the directive text or section labels into TTS.

## Visual style
Lean but purposeful documentary visuals: real person/photo, authentic video/post or headline, simple clean infographic/timeline, expressive text, layered editorial collage, subtle kinetic transforms and recurring visual motifs. Avoid both a slideshow and purposeless motion.

Preferred visual motions:
- Build a cascade of shares, posts and media pickups to show amplification.
- Split screen: fame vs approval; views vs fans; attention vs sustained career.
- Show a platform or format changing incentives with a clear before/after.
- Track a single protagonist through meaningful decisions with a persistent time rail.
- Return to the opening screenshot/image when ending its question.

Photographs/footage/posts must be licensed, owner-cleared or used only after documented case-specific rights analysis. Public availability does not equal license. Never fabricate newspaper covers, screencaps, interviews, deleted posts or screenshots that could be mistaken for authentic evidence. If a graphic reconstructs an interface, label it 'illustration' when needed.

## Scene map contract
03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR scenes each identify:
- Sxx and precise P### paragraph span
- Purpose and exact essential verified on-screen text
- Start frame -> change/transformation -> end frame
- Asset class and source/provenance, with rights status or clearance action
- Audio bed, meaningful silence, pause, or transition
- What's flexible for the editor and what cannot be changed

The first 30 seconds require a *shot-by-shot*, fully conceived cold open. Do not hand the editor three possible hooks to choose from. Preserve economical production and allow better equivalent execution that keeps the narrative function.

## Music, editing and pacing
Tension comes from changing information, not constant risers. Use music changes at earned turns, a moment of silence after an extraordinary fact, and occasional visual breathing room. No artificial pause every 30 seconds and no permanent wall of subtitles. Music must be properly licensed and remain under intelligible narration.
