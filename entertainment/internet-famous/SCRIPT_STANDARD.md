# Internet Famous script standard: 10-20 minutes, ideal 15

This channel explicitly overrides Green Knight business episodes' 8-10 minute target. **Target final video 900 seconds (15:00), acceptable 600-1200 seconds (10:00-20:00).** Measure actual assembled video. Do not padding-farm to meet 15 minutes.

## Word planning
At a provisional 145-165 spoken WPM, aim approximately 2,050-2,300 SPOKEN words for a 15-minute video with natural pauses and a few brief visual holds. The correct word count comes from an auditioned narrator's measured delivery, not an automatic fixed target. At 10-20 minutes, adjust based on evidence density, narrative scope and recorded timing.

## Recommended 15-minute dramaturgy, ADAPTABLE
- S00 | 0:00-0:30 | **Cold open / irresistible hook:** start at a decisive scene or contradiction; deliver verified partial proof; establish central question. NO logo, greeting or episode summary.
- S01 | 0:30-1:45 | **The irresistible setup:** introduce protagonist and the apparently impossible outcome; orient absolute newcomers; establish stakes and first promise.
- S02 | 1:45-4:15 | **The fuse:** what initially happened and why it mattered; vivid specifics; the first answer; forward-motion turn.
- S03 | 4:15-7:00 | **The explosion / amplifier:** reconstruct how this spread. Explain mechanics in concrete human and platform terms with evidence, not imaginary algorithm internals.
- S04 | 7:00-10:15 | **The price / conflict / conversion:** hardest middle. Who got attention, who benefited, who did not, what it cost and which early assumption breaks? This act MUST deliver a new reversal or substantial insight.
- S05 | 10:15-12:45 | **The unexpected turn:** decision, reinvention, backlash, outside actor, different outcome or genuine counterexample. Test the thesis against what doesn't fit.
- S06 | 12:45-14:35 | **The reveal and intellectual payoff:** answer opening mystery with a sharper distinction, supported by evidence. Return to opening visual/image.
- S07 | 14:35-15:00 | **Closer:** strong final line, brief relevant invitation or adjacent next story. Never weaken the ending with a long generic CTA.

These are editorial targets, not forced chapter-card appearances or fabricated twists. A 20-minute story may need more acts; a well-sourced 10-minute story needs fewer. Every Sxx boundary maps to consecutive P### narration paragraph IDs and scene IDs in the directors.

## The segmented-script system
**Internal authoring file:** a segmented working draft (see templates/INTERNAL_SEGMENTED_SCRIPT.md) with S00-S07 headings, paragraph IDs, approximate timing, dramatic job, what changes, next curiosity, source references, visual concepts and complete narrated prose. Explicitly mark everything non-spoken.

**Canonical spoken source:** script.txt begins with TITLE: [final approved title], one blank line, then only verbatim spoken narration paragraphs separated by blank lines. Title header is NON-SPOKEN. P001 etc are assigned by paragraph order, not literally inserted. Never place sections, timestamps, Sxx tags, shot notes, sources or director cues in this clean script.

**The THREE final editor documents:** 01_SCRIPT.txt = canonical clean script; 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md = COMPLETE IDENTICAL spoken copy under clearly labeled S00-S07 segments plus NON-SPOKEN vocal direction; 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md = segment-to-scene map, asset treatment, music, visual changes and time estimates. Thus an editor sees act boundaries and exact recording instructions without TTS reading production headings.

## Story structure for NON-PERSON subjects
S00-S07 still work, but the central character may be a meme, video, image, game, challenge, platform feature or internet ritual. In S01 introduce the recognizable object/behavior; in S02 trace the first documented appearance (or clearly acknowledge unknown origin); in S03 demonstrate spread and imitative participation; in S04 examine a surprising contradiction or cost; in S05 examine transformation or a competing explanation; in S06 deliver a generalizable mechanism. Do not invent a private emotional arc or force a human figurehead onto a collective phenomenon.

**Humane storytelling:** If the topic involves a person, the author is analyzing the internet's behavior, not making a roast. Make documented challenges and choices clear while excluding derogatory adjectives, humiliation reenactments, unverified accusations and mocking voice/visual edits. Apply [PEOPLE_AND_FAIRNESS.md](PEOPLE_AND_FAIRNESS.md) before marking a script ready.

## Required script research card before drafting
- Working title and thumbnail promise
- One-sentence central paradox
- What the viewer thinks at 0:00 versus what they learn at the end
- Protagonist (person, community, meme, image, app, product, format or other phenomenon) and meaningful human/cultural stakes
- What actually went viral; who amplified it; what changed
- 3-6 high-quality surprising facts, verified independently when possible
- Causal mechanism and strongest alternative explanation
- Conversion: views vs fans vs dollars vs influence, or why conversion failed
- One earned midfilm reversal
- Consequences for people, communities or internet culture, fairly treated where real people are involved
- Proof table: factual claim -> source URL -> evidence type -> caveat
- Long-form runtime justification: why this needs 15 minutes

## Narrative mechanics
- Drama from character decisions, reversals, risk and new evidence, never false mystery.
- At least one meaningful surprising development in the 7-11 minute portion.
- Present analysis THROUGH the story, not a boxed-off 'here is the lesson' lecture.
- Re-open a better question only after delivering the previous promise.
- Every sentence should be recordable naturally. Avoid overly dense statistics, nested names and endless 'however'.
- Avoid unearned moralizing, cyberbullying, intrusive private details, repeated visual clichés and unsupported generalizations.

## QA gate
1. Cold open gripping at 0-5 seconds; precise promise by 30 seconds.
2. First substantial payoff before 2 minutes.
3. Genuine story engine at every S boundary, especially S04.
4. Specific, sourced, non-obvious mechanism and credible alternative explanation.
4a. No demeaning personal commentary or unreviewed reputation-damaging implications, including in thumbnail and music/visual editing.
5. End delivers payoff and makes opening moment mean something new.
6. Spoken words match 01, 02 and mapped 03.
7. Evidence, audio, visual rights and owner script + narrator approvals verified before release.
8. No actual duration asserted before recording and cut review.

**Do not issue the final editor packet from an outline or unapproved producer draft.**
