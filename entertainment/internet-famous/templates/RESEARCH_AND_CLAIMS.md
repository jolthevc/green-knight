## Human dignity and reputational screening
- Are we explaining public behavior or recruiting viewers to laugh at someone?
- Would relevant context, response or a credible contradiction change the implication?
- Is any real person unnecessarily identifiable, vulnerable or a minor?
- Which precise title, thumbnail, voiced assertion or music/image juxtaposition might damage reputation?
- High-risk legal/editorial review required? By whom, and is it actually completed?
- Outcome: PASS / REVISE / STOP. The answer is NOT a guarantee against liability.

## Ethics and rights
- Is any controversy alleged rather than adjudicated/verified?
- Is a private detail actually germane and publicly documented?
- Are younger/vulnerable participants involved?
- Screenshots/photographs/footage: provenance, permissions or case-specific analysis
- AI reconstructions or composites require clear labels and must not impersonate actual evidence.
