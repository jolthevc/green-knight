# Studio Confidential

**The extraordinary stories behind the movies and television we love.**

**Channel promise:** We are genuine film and television obsessives making exceptionally entertaining, rigorously researched mini-documentaries about the people, productions, creative decisions, rivalries, economics, accidents of history and cultural phenomena behind beloved entertainment. We make familiar titles feel newly fascinating.

**Not a movie review channel. Not a plot-recap channel. Not a Hollywood business channel.** Economics, incentives and industry power are part of the lens when they make a story better. The central promise is unforgettable storytelling from people who love cinema and television.

**Creative compass:** Netflix-caliber narrative pull, WSJ/Bloomberg-style explanatory curiosity and affectionate Warner Bros.-level fascination with entertainment culture. These are creative analogies, not affiliations or licenses.

## Locked editorial decisions (2026-10-22)
- Working brand: **Studio Confidential**; channel name approved in conversation, not independently cleared for trademark, YouTube handle or other usage. An existing visual-arts podcast uses the name.
- Scope: film, TV, streaming, creators, franchises, studios, fandom, iconic characters and related cultural history; international stories welcome.
- Runtime: **10 to 15 finished minutes**, preference **12 to 13**, never pad to a target or weaken an excellent story to force length.
- Audience: mainstream movie/TV lovers and curious nonexperts, with worthwhile specificity for devoted fans.
- Publishing hypothesis: **four videos per month**, not a commissioned or proven output rate.
- Treatment: cinematic, expressive, entertaining, incisive and warm. Authentic footage, archival photos, interviews, publications and clean minimal graphics over heavy animation.
- Economics: occasional integrated storytelling flavor; never a compulsory angle or a separate lecture.
- Narrative: distinctive true conflict, compelling characters or objects, unfolding stakes, surprising turns and a resonant payoff.
- Status: **editorial specification only**. No YouTube account, editor contract, narrator, pilot commission, published video, rights clearance or automation registration is implied.
