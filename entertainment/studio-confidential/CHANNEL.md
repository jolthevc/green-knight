# Channel constitution: Studio Confidential

## The mission
**The extraordinary stories behind the movies and television we love.**

Audiences know the films, the shows, the iconic scenes and the characters. They rarely know the improbable human, creative, cultural and economic stories that brought those works into existence and changed what came after.

Studio Confidential takes viewers behind the screen: the creative gambles, production chaos, pivotal decisions, legendary partnerships and rivalries, strange successes, spectacular misfires and cultural obsessions. It also gives room to pure wonder and artistry. We tell those stories as beautifully as we can.

## The editorial equation
**Entertainment love + real human story + investigative curiosity + cinematic storytelling = Studio Confidential.**

Functional inspirations: captivating Netflix documentary narrative, WSJ/Bloomberg rigor and explanatory clarity, combined with a lifelong film/TV fan's fascination. Do not copy branded visual identities, imply affiliations or imitate identifiable talent.

## Viewer contract
- **The click:** An immediately familiar title/person/character or an irresistible genuinely extraordinary unknown story, paired with a specific truthful curiosity gap.
- **The first 30 seconds:** Show or immediately name the work, prove the intriguing premise and let audiences feel the stakes.
- **The watch:** Meaningful choices, conflicts, reveals and changes in the viewer's understanding, not a cast-list chronology or a sequence of trivia.
- **The finish:** Resolve the promised mystery and deepen appreciation of the film/TV work, the people who made it, or the broader entertainment phenomenon.
- **The return:** The channel feels like a group of movie lovers whose research and storytelling always reward the audience's time.

## Personality
Obsessed but not gatekeeping. Curious and sophisticated without sounding academic. Cinematic, witty when earned, compassionate about real people's lives, sometimes suspenseful, sometimes playful or nostalgic. Love the art while being lucid about its flaws. No sneering at fans or creators. No formulaic takedown engine.

## Content philosophy
- Movies **and** television are equal citizens, including streaming and animation.
- Creative development, behind-the-scenes production, fascinating character origins, iconic scenes, missing alternatives, franchises, fandom, studio strategy, distribution and economics are all eligible.
- A topic must have a specific, sourced **story worth telling**. "Interesting facts about X" is not a narrative.
- A financial or strategic angle should appear only when it makes the central true story clearer, more dramatic or more surprising.
- Fan theories may be examined as fan theories but never presented as documented production history.
- Negative stories are welcome when warranted; affection and delight must still be credible channel emotions.
- Older/forgotten films are welcome when the hook can orient mainstream viewers; celebrity familiarity alone is insufficient.

## Guardrails
No ungrounded allegations about real actors or filmmakers, counterfeit insider knowledge, fabricated quotes, invented set footage, misleading edited reaction shots, unsourced budgets/box-office claims, or reduction of complicated productions to one convenient villain. Careful treatment of death, injuries, alleged misconduct, sexual harassment and other serious claims.

## Distinction within Green Knight
This channel is neither an extension of Fame & Fortune's celebrity economics nor Behind Everything's systemic explainers. Business topics belong only when they explain a movie/TV story that the audience came to see. Car Lore covers love of cars; Internet Famous covers attention itself. Cross-referencing is encouraged, duplicate central theses are not.

## Story quality test
**The person who loves the subject discovers something new. The person who doesn't know it still wants to finish. Both leave wanting to watch something.**
