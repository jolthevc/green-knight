# Studio Confidential: researcher, writer, producer and editor workflow

## Proposed lean operating model
- Four 10-15 minute episodes per month **planned**, not contracted.
- Green Knight provides researched scripts and editorial direction. Editor uses rights-appropriate archival/stock imagery, real media with documented permission or reviewed justification, narration, clean graphics, music/sound, thumbnail and publishing assets.
- Low-cost outsourced editing is a hypothesis, not a guaranteed ~$60 quote. Copyright and footage licensing may make a given story economically unattractive.
- Use documentary-grade writing and source work to reduce postproduction complexity. Select topics that can be visualized responsibly.

## Sequence and approval gates
1. **Pitch:** choose from candidate ideas or propose a new one; prove familiar subject and story-worthy paradox. Owner selects subject and angle.
2. **Research:** strong primary/reputable secondary evidence, contested accounts, dates, rights/asset leads, exact version of film/show and spoiler sensitivity; do not hallucinate quotes or office politics.
3. **Packaging:** eight or more truthful titles, three thumbnail approaches, one proposed package and an exact honest 30-second opening.
4. **Outline:** a living narrative beat map specifying story turns, viewer questions, human stakes, surprising fan-specific details and whether economics genuinely belongs.
5. **Script:** 10-15 finished-minute spoken narration target; aim 12-13 when story supports it, no padding. Full source/claim log stays internal.
6. **Independent QA:** first-30-second curiosity, fan credibility, newcomer accessibility, narrative changes, factual/defamation fairness, rights feasibility, exact promised conclusion.
7. **Owner approval:** final script, proposed title/thumbnail, then selected narrator before editor packet release.
8. **Release exactly three editor-facing text files:** 
   - **01_SCRIPT.txt:** approved clean spoken copy, TITLE header only non-spoken.
   - **02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md:** full identical spoken copy with segmented non-spoken voice direction, pronunciations and emotional beats.
   - **03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md:** paragraph-ID-based Sxx/scene timing, first frame, shot map, real evidence, asset source, rights/alternatives, clean minimal explanatory graphics, music and sound.
9. **Optional sample:** after commission, request a short integration proof when useful to catch low-quality AI visuals or unsupported movie clips.
10. **Video QA:** screen complete export with full script and asset ledger; verify rights, source accuracy, pacing, mix, original commentary and thumbnail truth.
11. **Final publish gate:** separate owner signoff, approved title/description and correct chapters/SRT. Review actual performance and retention after distribution.

## Deliverables to request of an engaged editor
- 16:9 final 1080p+ MP4 and custom thumbnail.
- Separate narration WAV/MP3, SRT subtitles, accurate chapters, description, candidate SEO metadata.
- Asset provenance/licensing ledger, appropriate attributions, editable project source if agreed, revision history.
- Revision rounds, cost and turnaround are contract terms to confirm, not pre-approved promises.

## Hard stops
- Key dramatic fact or serious allegation unverified.
- Content implies access to confidential studio material without evidence.
- Central visual requires copyrighted excerpts with no reviewed alternative.
- Episode depends on a heavy animation pipeline that the edit budget cannot support.
- Too little story for a genuine 10 minutes; better to find another angle than pad.
- Title promises one thing while the script actually delivers a different story.
- Narration sounds like someone uninterested in cinema, or video is a generic stock slideshow.

## Measurement and iteration
After actual upload, track impressions, CTR relative to audience, first-30-second retention, AVD/average percentage viewed, returning viewers, source of traffic, comments identifying favorite reveals and real production costs. Never invent retention metrics. Use observed lessons to decide future pilot topics.

## Editorial status
This workspace establishes standards only. No episodes, voice, producer, rights, payments, publication or YouTube channel have been approved or created by these documents.
