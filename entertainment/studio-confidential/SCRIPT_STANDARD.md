# Studio Confidential: 10-15 minute documentary script standard

## Runtime
**10 to 15 finished minutes, ideally 12 to 13.** No filler, repetitious setup, padded chronology or artificial slow delivery. If the best story is 10:20, it ends at 10:20. If verified evidence justifies 14:45, let it run.

Provisional spoken-copy planning: **about 1,350-2,150 words** depending on narrator's measured words per minute, interviews, authentic sound, pauses and visual holds. An excellent 12-13 minute draft might be roughly 1,650-1,900 spoken words. These are planning estimates, **not quotas**. Time the narrator and final edit.

## Adaptable dramaturgy, not mandatory chapter headings
- **S00 | 0:00-0:30 | Cold open:** put us inside the real predicament or contradiction. Recognizable property, verified tension, immediate stakes.
- **S01 | roughly 0:30-1:45 | The invitation:** why the film/show matters, the protagonist, why this story demands attention.
- **S02 | roughly 1:45-4:00 | The dream and setup:** the creative intention, context and consequential choices.
- **S03 | roughly 4:00-7:00 | The beautiful mess:** the conflict, challenge, process or surprising early result. Not obligatory chronological order.
- **S04 | roughly 7:00-10:00 | The turning point:** a real reversal, revelation, inspired solution, unexpected commercial or cultural consequence.
- **S05 | roughly 10:00-12:30 | The full picture:** what followed, what collaborators and audiences made of it, strongest evidence and credible alternative interpretations.
- **S06 | roughly 12:30-13:00 | Resonant closer:** answer the exact title promise and make the film/TV work newly meaningful.

A 10-minute episode has fewer beats, and a 15-minute one can sustain additional complications. No scene is inserted merely to hit a timestamp. Document section beats internally but remove non-spoken production labels from TTS copy.

## Requirements
- A real story, not a sequence of trivia, review arguments or movie plot summary.
- At least one delightfully specific piece of production, creative or performance lore a fan would appreciate.
- A newcomer knows who matters and why. Necessary spoilers are flagged.
- At least one earned re-interpretation: the audience sees a familiar thing differently by the end.
- Economics or rights/industry mechanics included only where they alter decisions and consequences.
- No counterfeit intimacy: do not assign historical people private thoughts, fake conversations or false on-set scenes. Distinguish disputed accounts.
- Quote, box office, budget, viewership and casting claims have verified sources with contextual limitations.
- Narrative can be emotional, funny, surprising, tragic or triumphant. Do not force every subject into an identical failure-and-redemption format.
- Strong middle; genuinely consequential developments rather than synthetic cliffhangers or repeated promises.
- End with an earned return to the audience's original fascination, not generic business principles or a promotional outro.

## Canonical production text
- 01_SCRIPT.txt: first line TITLE: [approved title] (non-spoken), blank line, then **only spoken narration** separated into paragraphs. No inline source URLs, timestamps, scene headings, performance labels or instructions.
- 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md: exact complete spoken script in sections, with clearly **NON-SPOKEN** vocal cues, pacing, emotion, pronunciation and section purpose.
- 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md: scene/shot map anchored to those narration paragraphs P001..., with rights/provenance and visual alternatives. Script, narration director and visual director must refer to the **same revision**.
- Internal working materials may use S00-S06, scene IDs, paragraph IDs and a claim-to-source table; don't insert these into the TTS source.

## QA gates
A compelling first five seconds; a verified and legible first 30 seconds; genuinely new turns; fan credibility; mainstream accessibility; factual/fair treatment; footage feasibility; distinct final insight; owner script and narrator approvals before editor packet release. Reject excellent research that is boring and exciting claims that are unsupported.
