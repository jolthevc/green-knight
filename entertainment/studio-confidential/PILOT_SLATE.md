## Suggested first test mix, not an approved production order
1. **Shrek:** distinctive rivalry, family recognition, creative flair, potential fan surprise.
2. **The Office:** TV balancing the film candidates; accessible comeback story.
3. **Batgirl:** economics lens without becoming a business channel; unusual contemporary decision.
4. **Toy Story:** authentic creative development story and enormous nostalgic familiarity.

A second plausible lineup substitutes **Lord of the Rings** for Batgirl if archival feasibility and verified narrative support are stronger. Test at least one film and one TV property, one creative tale and one entertainment-economics story.

## Screening against Green Knight overlap
- A filmmaker's public feud is Studio Confidential if the film is central; celebrity wealth and personal holdings are Fame & Fortune.
- A viral movie-meme phenomenon is Internet Famous if attention dynamics are the subject.
- The business of streaming is Behind Everything if the consumer system is central; House of Cards' creative/deal story can belong here.
- Product shots of movie cars do not belong in Car Lore unless real automotive obsession is central.
