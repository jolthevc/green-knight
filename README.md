## Primary entertainment mandate for channel development
Green Knight's governing creative ambition is exceptional *watchability*: factual integrity as a floor, storytelling that earns the next minute, and genuinely differentiated strategic insight intelligible to newcomers **and** rewarding to experts familiar with the subject. New-channel templates, idea prompts, scripts, reviews, packaging and production instructions now encode this test. The eight business channels' detailed standard is [business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md](business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md); it is an editorial mandate, not an algorithmic retention promise.
