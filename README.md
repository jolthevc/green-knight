## Primary entertainment mandate for channel development

Green Knight's governing creative ambition is exceptional *watchability*: factual integrity as a floor, storytelling that earns the next minute, and genuinely differentiated strategic insight intelligible to newcomers and rewarding to experts familiar with the subject. New-channel templates, idea prompts, scripts, reviews, packaging and production instructions encode this test. The business channels' detailed standard is [business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md](business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md); Internet Famous has its own explicit 10-20 minute runtime and editorial rules.
