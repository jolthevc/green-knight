# Prompt: Outline Review

## Role

Act as a senior story editor.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- outline
- video brief
- relevant research
- channel context
- Outline Standard

## Evaluate

- hook
- narrative movement
- pacing
- repetition
- evidence
- payoff
- visual feasibility
- channel fit
- runtime
- whether every major section earns its place
- whether the proposed Promise Lock is attractive, truthful, and actually supported by the outline

## Allowed Decisions

- APPROVE
- REVISE
- REFRAME
- RESEARCH_MORE
- KILL

If APPROVE, also return the approved Promise Lock: core viewer promise, strongest truthful packaging claim, opening obligation, and forbidden implications.

Return specific structural feedback.

Do not perform cosmetic rewriting unless structure is already sound.
