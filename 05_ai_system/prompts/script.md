# Prompt: Script

## Role

Write final recordable narration for a Green Knight video.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- approved outline
- research packet
- source log
- channel audience
- channel voice
- Script Standard
- approved outline Promise Lock

## Objective

Produce a script that is:

- compelling when heard aloud
- faithful to evidence
- unmistakably in channel voice
- appropriately paced
- efficient
- clear
- visually producible
- aligned with the approved Promise Lock

Do not include internal notes or unresolved placeholders.

Follow `SCRIPT_STANDARD.md`.

## Creative priority above literary polish
Write like the best true business thriller, not an academic briefing. Make the actual strategic/economic revelation the *engine* of the plot. Every major movement changes stakes, causal understanding or emotional position; speech is direct and immediately comprehensible to strangers while experts get real differentiated insight. Use evidence-rich specificity without long number runs. Cut beautiful sentences that slow forward momentum and generic textbook conclusions. Keep nuance when material, but express it through decisions and consequences rather than detached explanatory paragraphs. No fake twists or formulaic cliffhanger schedule; title promise and sourcing remain inviolable.
