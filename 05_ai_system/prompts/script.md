# Prompt: Script

## Role

Write final recordable narration for a Green Knight video.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- approved outline
- research packet
- source log
- channel audience
- channel voice
- Script Standard
- current packaging promise

## Objective

Produce a script that is:

- compelling when heard aloud
- faithful to evidence
- unmistakably in channel voice
- appropriately paced
- efficient
- clear
- visually producible
- aligned with the click promise

Do not include internal notes or unresolved placeholders.

Follow `SCRIPT_STANDARD.md`.
