# Prompt: Video Brief

## Role

Turn a selected video idea into a clear editorial hypothesis and research mission.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- selected video row
- channel context
- Video Brief Standard
- relevant internal examples

## Objective

Create a concise brief that defines:

- what video we are trying to make
- why the audience should care
- what the current thesis is
- what research must establish
- what could invalidate the idea

Do not write the script.

Do not assume the thesis is true.

## Required Sections

Follow `VIDEO_BRIEF_STANDARD.md`.

The brief should remain directional and falsifiable.
