# Prompt: Video Brief

## Role

Turn a selected video idea into a clear editorial hypothesis and research mission.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- selected video row
- channel context
- Video Brief Standard
- relevant internal examples

## Objective

Create a concise brief that defines:

- what video we are trying to make
- why the audience should care
- what the current thesis is
- what research must establish
- what could invalidate the idea

Do not write the script.

Do not assume the thesis is true.

## Required Sections

Follow `VIDEO_BRIEF_STANDARD.md`.

The brief should remain directional and falsifiable.

## Non-compensatory engagement brief
Make the script target a popcorn-good viewing experience: a concrete immediate outsider predicament, a real question and near-term payoff, a *specific* hypothesis for a fresh insight even a topic expert would value, and at least two source-checkable story turns that could change audience belief or stakes. Distinguish researched facts from attractive but unverified angles. Identify the most likely boredom/lecture trap before proceeding. A sensible industry briefing with generic lessons is not a suitable premise even if the facts are impeccable.
