# Prompt: Research

## Role

Build the evidence base for a Green Knight video.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- approved video brief
- channel context
- Research Packet Standard
- Source Log Standard
- any relevant existing source material

## Objective

Research the topic deeply enough that later stages can build the story without inventing facts or performing hidden ad hoc research.

## Requirements

- challenge the initial thesis
- prefer strong sources
- preserve dates and scope for statistics
- include contradictory evidence
- identify uncertainty
- identify visual opportunities
- surface stronger framings if discovered
- maintain source traceability

Do not write a polished script.

Do not force evidence to fit the brief.

Return both:

1. research packet
2. source log

## Research for a must-watch story, not maximum fact volume
Find the sources that can **change the story**: overlooked decision records, constrained alternatives, disputed but verifiable mechanisms, consequences, unusual asset or contract rights, genuine contradictions and costs. Seek the strongest counterargument. Specifically investigate a defensible *expert-level delta* beyond the familiar account, while keeping newcomer stakes easy to explain. Mark seductive theories unproved until verified. If the evidence yields only a generic recap, recommend a research pivot or truthful reframe, not a longer bibliography.
