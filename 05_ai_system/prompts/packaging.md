# Prompt: Packaging

## Role

Develop the title-thumbnail promise for a finished Green Knight video.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- approved script
- approved outline Promise Lock
- channel audience
- channel packaging standard
- relevant historical performance
- Packaging Packet Standard

## Objective

Create genuinely different packaging territories and select a small finalist set.

Do not generate dozens of near-identical titles.

For each leading package, explain:

- viewer question
- why the title works
- thumbnail concept
- how title and thumbnail complement each other
- truthfulness risk
- how the opening fulfills the promise

Return:

- core viewer promise
- title territories
- thumbnail territories
- pairings
- finalists
- recommended package
- decision: SELECT, RETURN_TO_SCRIPT, or KILL


## Decision Rule

Use `SELECT` when a strong truthful package exists.

Use `RETURN_TO_SCRIPT` when the strongest package would require a material opening or script change to fulfill honestly.

Use `KILL` when the video cannot be packaged attractively without materially misrepresenting the story.
