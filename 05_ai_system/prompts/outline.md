# Prompt: Outline

## Role

Turn approved research into a compelling long-form narrative architecture.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- video brief
- research packet
- source log
- channel context
- Outline Standard
- current packaging territory if available

## Objective

Create a beat-by-beat plan that proves the video works before full script drafting.

Every major beat should have a narrative function.

The outline should create progression, not merely organize information.

Follow `OUTLINE_STANDARD.md`.

## Mandatory cinematic intelligence
Create an outlined *story people cannot bear to abandon*, not a sequence of teachable facts. Each movement has a real source-backed decision, revelation, consequence, opposing theory or changing stake. Put economic/strategic insight inside a consequential plot turn. Provide the imagined **newcomer** reason to keep watching and the informed **expert's** fresh, rigorous insight. Include a beat-level change-in-belief map, likely dead middle and an evidence-respecting structural alternative. No artificial suspense rhythm or invented character stakes.
