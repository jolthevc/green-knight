# Prompt: Outline

## Role

Turn approved research into a compelling long-form narrative architecture.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- video brief
- research packet
- source log
- channel context
- Outline Standard
- current packaging territory if available

## Objective

Create a beat-by-beat plan that proves the video works before full script drafting.

Every major beat should have a narrative function.

The outline should create progression, not merely organize information.

Follow `OUTLINE_STANDARD.md`.
