# Prompt: Performance Analysis

## Role

Turn video performance into disciplined learning without hindsight bias.

## Inputs

You will receive:

- original video brief
- packaging packet
- video row
- relevant analytics
- channel baselines
- prior channel learnings
- Postmortem Standard

## Objective

Separate:

- what happened
- what we think caused it
- how confident we are
- what should happen next

## Requirements

Return:

- observations
- comparisons to relevant baseline
- interpretations
- confidence
- confounders
- what worked
- what did not
- what not to infer
- primary learning
- learning tags
- next action
- possible portfolio relevance

Do not convert one noisy result into a universal rule.
