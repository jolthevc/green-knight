# Standard idea presentation

**GOVERNING AUDIENCE PRIORITY (2026-10-11):** Read [POPCORN_STANDARD.md](POPCORN_STANDARD.md). Within truthful/evidence constraints, **earned engagement is the primary creative objective**, with **mandatory newcomer *and* already-expert viewer value**. A precise but dull lecture or generic business takeaway is not publishable. Story beats must deliver genuine changing stakes, revealing strategic mechanisms and desire for the next minute. Critical review must actually reject/rework weak middle sections and generic insights; no empty claims of 'engaging.'


## Generate broad, differentiated candidates

Pretty Penny starts with familiar spending and surprises: subscriptions, travel, food, tickets, retail, consumer finance, marketplaces, invisible suppliers and ownership economics. Vary the object, money mechanism and emotional entry. Avoid ten near-identical corporate profiles or a banking-only slate.

Money Moves starts with recognizable products and consequential strategic choices. Vary pivots, pricing, distribution, constraints, reinvention and rival responses. Its cards must identify the decision, constraint, plausible alternatives and what evidence could establish the outcome. Do not make the hypothesis true just by calling a move brilliant.

Fallen Angels starts with a recognizable rise-to-loss contrast. Vary lost advantages, failed launches, slow deterioration, financing crises and unsuccessful rescues. Identify what failed, the suspected mechanism and which familiar explanation needs testing. Hypotheses remain unverified until research.

Changing Hands starts with consequential historical positions, takeovers, squeezes, buyouts and financial contests. Candidate cards identify the transaction, players, stakes, constraint and mechanism to investigate. Avoid narrow jargon, current stock picks and unsupported profit figures.

Fool’s Gold starts with a specific apparent-versus-real discrepancy in business or finance. Vary financial loops, false records, hidden losses, counterfeits and manufactured reputation. Candidate cards must identify the suspected deception and likely evidence of exposure, without assuming every disputed practice is fraud.

Silver Spoon starts with a family fortune, succession, inheritance, control contest or division of assets. Vary industries, places, generations and outcomes. Candidate cards identify the relevant rights/wealth transfer and why the story is broader than gossip or a rich-person biography.

First Billion starts with an understandable path from a first viable unit to a defined wealth/business milestone. Vary historical fortunes, worked models, industry structures, comparisons, ownership and repeatability tests. Every card names its format, billion metric and evidence/modeling limits.

On the Map starts with a place-based puzzle and a tangible entry. Vary its six formats. Each card defines geography/period, starting conditions, origin versus persistence hypothesis, plausible alternative, value capture, human stakes and the main uncertainty. Use channels/on-the-map/RESEARCH_STANDARD.md; do not substitute destination trivia for a causal story.

Use LANE_ROUTING.md and the channel lanes to route concepts. A collapse belongs in Fallen Angels; a decisive strategic choice in Money Moves; a deception in Fool’s Gold; a discrete consequential deal in Changing Hands; a dynasty in Silver Spoon; an end-to-end wealth-building engine in First Billion. Pretty Penny can use company examples when the system is the subject.

Do not confuse likely advertiser interest with proven RPM. Geography, audience, season, suitability and demand matter; validate monetization later with channel data.

## Broad-appeal and selection checks

Score familiarity, curiosity, stakes and explanation feasibility separately. Prefer a concrete audience puzzle over an abstract sector label. Show a plausible evidence path and sustainable visual approach before recommending research. A fashionable finance keyword is not evidence of either broad appeal or RPM.

Before selecting a revisit, write the prior episode's answer and the proposed answer side by side. If the payoff is materially the same, reject the duplicate even when the title or channel differs. A reframe that changes lanes preserves history using the LANE_ROUTING.md protocol.

## Story-first candidate card additions

Apply ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md. In each existing card include the recognizable situation or human stakes, the expected changed understanding, and the deeper question after the first payoff. State why a person without business training would care. Do not add a narrow prerequisite audience just to target finance advertisers. These are hypotheses until research; reject an angle that offers only trivia, generic lessons or a long chronology.

## Mandatory pre-research creative greenlight

Read [NETWORK_CREATIVE_CHARTER.md](NETWORK_CREATIVE_CHARTER.md), [CREATIVE_GREENLIGHT.md](CREATIVE_GREENLIGHT.md) and channels/<slug>/CREATIVE_CONTRACT.md. Before ranking an idea as a recommended commission, test its one-sentence nonexpert hook, familiar human stakes, honest package, non-obvious changed understanding, first payoff, major complication, source and production feasibility, lane distinctiveness and specific share impulse. Compare at least two materially different openings and three truthful title candidates during concept development; include the strongest in the existing requested idea card without making the user read a second redundant card. Mark boring-but-researchable ideas as REFRAME or REJECT rather than giving them favorable points for advertiser value. Ratings remain internal judgments and not predicted views. Do not reserve a story simply because the source packet is abundant.

## Popcorn-first idea cards (non-negotiable)
POPCORN_STANDARD.md is the main selection filter. For every proposed idea, independently state (1) **stranger click/stay question:** which vivid human predicament/contradiction makes the uninitiated care and what is the likely first payoff?; (2) **expert revelation hypothesis:** what already-informed viewers would *still* find novel and useful, and what evidence could prove it?; (3) **story fuel:** a real consequential choice, causal reversal, contradiction, hidden mechanism or escalating constraint; (4) **watchability risk:** why might this become a dull chronology, list or lecture?; (5) **killer final payoff:** what specific non-generic insight might change the opening meaning?

Recommendations rank actual entertainment potential **ahead of** research length, easy access, conventional sophistication and RPM assumptions, while requiring credible evidence and original perspective. If the expert insight is speculative or unverified, label clearly and designate research-to-prove; if no good version is viable, reject. Do not invent a virality score or multiple formulaic hooks.

## Built to Scale growth-first override (2026-10-14)
Prioritize a **scaling ambition** (first customers, hiring the first management layer, equipment/credit/debt, larger bonded projects, second branch, buying a competitor, managing cash, real roll-up) over general operational curiosities. Operation topics must reveal a growth bottleneck, not generic how-to advice. Use four story engines: Growth Playbook, Built From Nothing (real historical company/founder examples), Big Decision (financial/managerial forks) and Growing Pains. Include documented cases such as Quanta Services, A1 Garage Door Service, Roto-Rooter, Comfort Systems USA or Neighborly as **research leads**, not proven success formulas. BTS-I0013 onward in the ledger is the new preferred slate; avoid near-duplicates with HC-I0001, HC-I0007 and HC-I0010 and consider already-proposed BTS growth/decision mechanisms. A contractor-wide playbook must be interesting to an outsider AND useful to someone responsible for a real trade business. See channels/built-to-scale/CHANNEL.md and CASE_STUDIES.md.

## House Money eleventh-channel generation (2026-10-15)
House Money begins with a **housing mystery involving a real home, resident, developer, landlord, mortgage, land parcel or transaction**, then reveals who makes/moves/captures value or bears risk. Vary homebuilder economics, mortgages/servicing/securitization, renting/property operations, closing/title/agent platforms, HOA/insurance/taxes, land/supply restrictions and nontraditional housing models. Use working titles that earn curiosity without absolute unsupported claims about all houses/builders/investors. Company-led housing histories and financial mechanisms are welcome; public filings, FHFA/CFPB/HUD/Census, city/developer documents and local records are source leads, not presumed verified. Check precise overlap against HC-I0006 land buildability, FA-I0033 Evergrande, OTM-I0003 Aspen and consumer spending Pretty Penny ideas. Use HM-I#### IDs. One viewer has lived experience; the second is a housing professional who needs a genuinely fresh causal insight. Follow channels/house-money/CHANNEL.md and EDITORIAL_LANES.md.
