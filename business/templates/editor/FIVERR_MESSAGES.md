# Fiverr editor messages - reusable drafting patterns (INTERNAL)
Use the voice: friendly, efficient, collaborative, clear. **Adapt to the actual conversation.** Do not send anything without owner instruction. Confirm rather than assume rates, revisions, dates, rights, milestones and voice access. Refer to "other responsibilities," not "other channels" unless the owner asks otherwise. Never paste internal reviews or playbook into Fiverr. Prefer short paragraphs over checklist walls.

## A. First outreach / qualification
Hi! I'm producing a series of 8-10-minute illustrated business documentaries and really like your work. I provide the finalized script, detailed narration guide, and visual direction for each video, including the thumbnail and opening. I'm looking for an editor who can turn that into an engaging, polished finished film, with thoughtful motion, graphics, music and sound.

Would this be a good fit for your style? I'd love to see relevant examples and hear your pricing for an initial pilot and, if that goes well, a five- or ten-video package. I'd also like to confirm whether narration using an approved ElevenLabs voice, thumbnails, subtitles and SEO deliverables are within your scope.

Happy to discuss the visual approach and what is realistic at this budget!

## B. After a satisfactory pilot: proposed 10-video batch
Thanks again. I'm happy with the pilot and we can consider it finalized! I'd love to move forward with the ten-video package.

Since I'm managing other responsibilities, I plan to prepare and send the ten finalized scripts, narration guides and visual directions **together**. I need a little time to finish them, but this should give you flexibility to plan production. Quality matters more than an unnecessarily rushed turnaround.

What would be a reasonable timeline for **the first five** and **all ten**, including revisions, once you have the approved materials? And how do you normally handle first-cut reviews and revisions? I'd like to review each cut and send one consolidated set of comments before approval.

For each finished video, can we confirm five files: (1) final MP4, (2) separate thumbnail, (3) clean narration WAV/MP3, (4) SEO title/description/tags plus chapters based on the final cut, and (5) synchronized SRT? Excited to work together!

## C. Clarify an unusually fast turnaround
That sounds great! Just to confirm, when you say the first five videos will be ready in [TIME], do you mean five initial cuts or five fully finalized videos including revisions? And would that clock start once I send all the approved scripts, narration and visual guides? No need to rush at the expense of quality.

## D. Once terms are agreed; kickoff the first episode
Excited to get started! I'm sending the finalized three files for [STORY / CHANNEL]: 01_SCRIPT (exact narration; TITLE is not spoken), 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR (delivery/pauses and full spoken reference), and 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR (selected title/thumbnail and scene-by-scene direction).

Please use the agreed [VOICE / REFERENCE] and let me know early if any visual or source item needs a practical alternative. Once we've aligned on the first cut, we can follow the [AGREED MILESTONE / BATCH] schedule. Thanks!

## E. Consolidated first-cut revisions (fill actual timestamps)
Thanks for the cut! I appreciate the work on [SPECIFIC GOOD THING]. I have a few consolidated notes before we finalize:

- [00:00-00:00] **Required:** [Specific mismatch/problem and desired outcome].
- [00:00-00:00] **Required:** [Specific mismatch/problem and desired outcome].
- [00:00-00:00] **Optional:** [One improvement, if suitable].

Please flag anything that would be difficult within our agreed scope. After the revised cut, we'll verify the final narration, thumbnail, SEO chapters and SRT together. Thanks again!

## F. Final approval / next milestone
Thank you, the revised [STORY] looks great and the agreed final files have been received. I'm happy to consider this one complete. [Only if authorized: Please send the next agreed milestone / I'd like to move to the next episode.] Really appreciate your work.

## Agent rule
Message examples are **patterns**. Never claim 'looks great,' 'all files received,' 'approved,' 'we agreed,' 'sent,' or 'paid' without real evidence/authorization. The owner may ask for a shorter version. Don’t inflate a friendly Fiverr message with every internal standard.
