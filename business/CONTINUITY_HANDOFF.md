## Working relationship and production rules

See [PRODUCER_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md](PRODUCER_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md): Company Man is Fallen Angels' *narration tone* reference; Modern MBA is *overall analytical/storytelling* reference; Wendover/WSJ supply financial diagrams, B1M is authentic construction visual inspiration. Not one rigid style for all 11 channels. Producer may be an execution editor **or** a commissioned script/research drafter; in the latter case owner reviews/approves the **full script and voice before production**. The 60-second trial and later full pilot are proposed gates, not verification of a real order.
