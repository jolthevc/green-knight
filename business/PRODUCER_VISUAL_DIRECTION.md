# Green Knight: producer reference styles and collaboration models

**Recorded 2026-10-17; an editorial brief, not narrator/production approval.** Specific channel STYLE, CREATIVE_CONTRACT, VOICE and VISUAL_SYSTEM guidance takes precedence. These are references, **not instructions to copy another channel's designs**.

## Role of each reference

- **Company Man:** conversational, calm, clear narration, subtle wit, natural chronology and endings. Useful for Fallen Angels' *voice*, not generic bullet-point recap.
- **Modern MBA:** primary benchmark for analytical depth, story construction, non-obvious business strategy/financial economics and satisfying conceptual discoveries. **Closest overall reference**, but not an exact visual replica.
- **Wendover Productions:** diagrammatic clarity for money flows, time, logistics, ownership, systems and tradeoffs.
- **WSJ's The Economics Of:** legible, sober, trustworthy data visuals, documents and explanatory graphics.
- **The B1M:** especially for Hard Costs, real construction footage, workers, materials, equipment and project scale.

The desired **hybrid documentary** combines real archival/company footage, source documents and authentic location footage with **intentional** diagrams and selective illustrations/animation. Some parts can be primarily footage; others can rely on animated economics. Reject stock-footage slideshows and gratuitous wall-to-wall animation. Audio, pace, visuals and causal reveals must work together. Verify licensing/rights.

## Two legitimate producer models

**Model A — Green Knight-authored/script supplied:** We research and approve narration and director packet; an editor executes the approved script with practical artistic freedom. Existing three-file packet applies.

**Model B — Producer-authored pilot:** With the owner's specific agreement, a Fiverr producer develops research, source leads and an entire **first-draft** script. We rigorously review and may rewrite it. **Owner expressly approves the complete final script and voice before recording/editing/full production.** Trial sample → full pilot → possible batch is a proposal, not a fact about contracted services. Three-file canonical packets apply after the owner-approved direction is complete; do not force the final packet before initial scripting work.

Neither model permits invented producer pricing, contracts, delivery promises, project status, original footage, narration approval or rights clearance.
