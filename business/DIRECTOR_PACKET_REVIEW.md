# Mandatory three-artifact editorial review and revision protocol

Read EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md, SCRIPT_REVIEW.md, the channel's CREATIVE_CONTRACT.md, the full exact canonical script/evidence, current cues, packaging and internal visual treatment.

## A: 01 Script
Cold-read before the prior editorial notes. Assess broad recognition, title/thumbnail promise, truthful 0–10/30/60-second hook and first earned answer, real stakes, causal story movement, structural segmentation and earned breathing points, weakest middle, first-listen ease, evidence/counterarguments, nuanced mechanisms, 8–10-minute timed feasibility and earned final payoff. Identify paragraph-anchored blockers, revisions and strong points. Do not assume visual effects can rescue confusing spoken explanations.

## B: 02 Narration director
Check the FULL body of canonical narration appears verbatim, with no silently paraphrased/omitted lines. Assess sequence/paragraph mapping, changing emotional/energy arc, accents/numeric pronunciation, pauses after important reveals, breath and speed through dense facts, performance transitions and final line. No mechanical uniform delivery and no forced rushed speech to achieve runtime. Cues must be visually distinct from voice-engine input. Use real audio listening when supplied; otherwise say it's a text-only direction review.

## C: 03 Visual director
Judge the selected title and composed phone-readable thumbnail with alternatives compared internally, not supplied as unresolved options to the editor and its honest hook promise. Evaluate deliberate first frame and opening visual beats, sequence continuity, recurring scenes, concept and business-mechanism visual transformations, authentic evidence assets, meaningful movement dividers, viewer belief change, sparing text/captions, composition variety, editor cost/technical feasibility, music/hold synchronization, channel identity and meaningful closing callback. Reject merely illustrating each spoken noun with another interchangeable still.

## D: Integration
Check matching script hash/version and all paragraph/sequence references; 02 spoken lines exactly match 01 body; 03 covers the actual same story and facts. At every major movement, narration energy, planned pause, visual transformation and music reset agree. Title/thumbnail's promise, opening and closing actually form one coherent question-and-answer. No source claims or exact metrics invented in thumbnail/text/illustration.

## Workflow and decision
For REVIEW only: save separate STRONG / REVISE / BLOCKING verdicts for 01/02/03 and integration, with anchored evidence, even if some artifacts are missing. Do not edit canonical script or claim a packet is released.

For REVIEW_AND_REVISE: diagnose script first; change spoken words only when warranted and authorized; refresh script version/hash and factual/evidence/cue anchors; develop/revise complete narration director and visual director; cold re-review each plus integration. Save dated reports in reviews/ and QA summary. A fresh final review is necessary after meaningful change, not just fixing the first report.

## Release gate
No blockers, exact approved canonical spoken copy, all three final deliverables complete, linked to current metadata/script SHA and channel standards; 02 full-text parity; 03 selected thumbnail and detailed first 30 seconds, meaningful visual sequence/section/audio breaks, source constraints and ending. Then create/refresh ONLY 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md and 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md inside that video's editor-packet folder. Internal source files are retained outside. New revisions make prior packet stale and not for dispatch. Never backfill false approval for legacy stories.

## Extra plain-English production-readiness test
Evaluate whether 02 has a verified selected voice (or transparent blocker), a true spoken-copy versus NOT FOR TTS separation, numbered passage-to-pause mappings and practical retake approach. Evaluate whether 03 makes **one definite creative choice** for title, thumbnail and opening; gives start/change/end screen actions and exact anchors; and flags required versus suggested/optional assets. Internally archive alternate concepts in packaging.md and working visual-plan.md, not in the final director's packet. Confirm selected choice, source provenance, accurate text and integrated sample request. A vague instruction (“make the sequence feel cinematic”, “show something interesting”, “add drama”) fails review unless paired with an executable action.

## Engagement-first integrated release gate (REQUIRED)
Read POPCORN_STANDARD.md. **Do not release the packet because all three files technically agree.** 01, 02, 03 and their integration must create one continuously rewarding *viewing experience*. In addition to script accuracy and narrator identity:

- Test a **newcomer** and an **already expert** as separate imagined viewers. For each, identify a truthful reason to continue in the opening, the weakest middle, and conclusion. An imagined test is editorial judgment, not real audience feedback.
- For EACH major S sequence, record a real action/visual transformation or mood change; why it changes the viewer's understanding, expectation or emotional stake; the corresponding P insight; and whether screen activity is just decoration. Cut/change visually busy scenes with no narrative or explanatory job.
- Explicitly identify the most boring 60–120-second anticipated interval and state whether 01 restructuring, 02 delivery, 03 visuals or re-research is the **right** fix. Never rely on faster cuts/music to conceal a repetitive plot.
- Confirm the sharp expert-level business mechanism appears as a **story discovery** and is provable/reasonable, not a final mini-lecture.
- Record dual-viewer verdicts and `POPCORN-GOOD PASS/REVISE` in the story's dated review. A boring middle, generic insight or weak click/first-minute payoff is a BLOCKER on creative release even when exact-copy, reference and factual tests pass. Re-review all three after substantive changes.

## Fame & Fortune runtime / editor clarity exception
Review Fame & Fortune against its configured **600-1200 second range, 900-second ideal** instead of the default 8-10 minutes. In addition to all existing checks, verify first-15-second dramatic hook, first-45-second verified payoff, and eight (or thoughtfully adapted) synchronized **non-spoken** segment names, paragraph ranges, approximate timecodes, voice energy/pause notes, visual scenes and meaningful next-question bridges across 02 and 03. 01 remains byte-identical clean narration. REVISE any generic celebrity bio, empty click promise, unsubstantiated scandal or middle sustained by filler.
