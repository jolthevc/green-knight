# Business postmortem: 8–10 minutes

Research before choosing the explanation. Develop the thesis and internal beat outline, write the whole spoken draft in one pass, then review and revise internally. The owner does not need to approve each paragraph.

## Narrative architecture

| Approximate window | Story job |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Recognizable contrast between promise/advantage and outcome |
| 0:25–1:30 | Define the failure and explain why the business once worked |
| 1:30–3:00 | Introduce the pressure and first useful causal insight |
| 3:00–5:00 | Follow the warning signs and response through the mechanism |
| 5:00–7:00 | Show acceleration, trigger or failed rescue; test the easy explanation |
| 7:00–9:30 | Establish the outcome, human stakes where evidenced and the supported lesson |
| Final 5–20 seconds | Short relevant next-video invitation if available |

These windows are planning aids, not mandatory durations. Finished runtime must be 8–10 minutes. Narrow the period or business unit if the history is too large.

## Opening and momentum

Develop two or three supported hooks internally and choose one. Open on a concrete contrast or puzzle, not a long corporate biography, greeting or early subscription request.

The first 30 seconds must match the title/thumbnail. Name what failed accurately. Provide an early useful insight; do not wait until the closing to begin explaining the mechanism.

Use escalating pressure, observable warnings, a consequential response and credible counterevidence to sustain attention. Every beat should change understanding. A new date alone is not narrative progress.

About every 30–60 seconds, check whether evidence, stakes or understanding advanced. This is an editorial diagnostic, not an official rule or required interruption. Avoid repeated “then disaster struck,” unexplained twists and manufactured suspense.

## The causal spine

Explain the original advantage → changing pressure → mechanism of deterioration → response → outcome.

Distinguish trigger from fragility, symptom from cause and a company's public explanation from independent evidence. Do not automatically attribute failure to Amazon, digital disruption, debt or one executive. Test the explanation against data and alternatives.

A timeline establishes sequence, not causality by itself. Explain how the factor changed demand, costs, financing, execution or competitiveness. Use correct metrics with baseline and period.

Avoid unverified offer anecdotes, fabricated conversations, invented private thoughts and certainty about what would have happened under another choice. Clearly label supported inference.

## Spoken text and production

Write for the ear with concrete examples and varied rhythm. Explain terms through the business situation. Keep amounts and names pronounceable while preserving exact underlying figures in the source map.

script.txt contains only spoken words. Citations, headings, timestamps, visual descriptions and delivery tags belong in separate files. Paragraph-linked visuals should reveal what changed, not supply generic stock scenes.

Use documentary evidence selectively and let the audience read important excerpts. Reconstructions and illustrative charts are labeled.

## Ending

Answer the opening puzzle. Identify the warning that mattered and why its meaning was difficult or disputed at the time. State the limits of the diagnosis without dissolving into vague caveats.

Close on one specific insight, not “never stop innovating” or “this proves all giants fall.” Acknowledge documented human consequences without invented personal anecdotes or a moralizing detour.

Use one main CTA after the payoff, generally an adjacent published story. If none exists, use a short channel invitation. No phantom end-screen links or stack of engagement requests.

## Internal review

Check failure definition, evidence chronology, causal reasoning, alternatives, contemporary knowledge, allegation/fact distinction, title promise, early value, progression, repetition, tonal restraint and production feasibility.

The completed package uses the shared artifacts plus a failure timeline and cause/trigger analysis in research and synthesis. Validate cue/claim/visual anchors and estimated timing. Production QA requires listening and actual finished duration.

## Broad-audience discovery

Apply [ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md](../../ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md). This channel's story engine is an unraveling. Show why the business once made sense so its deterioration matters. Revisit a warning as its meaning changes. Build curiosity around the failure mechanism even when the ending is known; do not conceal a famous collapse or play continuous doom.

Use the newcomer and informed-skeptic lenses. Record each beat's changed understanding and why the next follows. Choose an earned first payoff and a specific final insight. Aim for substantial learning delivered through the story, with warmth and room to follow, rather than a list of business frameworks.
