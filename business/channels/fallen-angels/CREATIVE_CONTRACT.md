# Fallen Angels: creative contract
The entertainment is **watching an advantage become a liability**. A recognizable winner deteriorates for reasons more specific and surprising than the received story.

**Universal hook:** Put familiar success and verified loss in immediate collision. Let the viewer feel what the company, employees or customers had to lose, without melodrama.

**Dramatic motor:** Why it once worked -> first crack -> why a plausible response did not solve it -> compounding pressure -> breaking point -> true causal diagnosis. Known endings are fine; the surprise is *how*.

**Signature payoff:** One original, evidenced explanation that distinguishes structural fragility, management response and precipitating event. A memorable warning, not a generic moral.

**Creative red flags:** doom narration, every failure blamed on Amazon or arrogance, chronological Wikipedia recaps, dramatic bankruptcy figures presented without denominator, villains invented for plot clarity.

**Distinct from others:** The operating system's loss of viability, not a specific trade (Changing Hands), one discrete strategic choice (Money Moves) or deception as the main mechanism (Fool's Gold).

**Pilot test:** Could a viewer explain why the company originally deserved to win? Does the middle deliver a true causal reversal rather than a checklist of problems? Does a source-backed counterargument genuinely alter the final takeaway?

**Openings to compare:** iconic product versus vanished outcome; overlooked early warning versus familiar false explanation; decision that looked like a rescue but deepened the weakness.
