# Fallen Angels: creative contract
The entertainment is **watching an advantage become a liability**. A recognizable winner deteriorates for reasons more specific and surprising than the received story.

**Universal hook:** Put familiar success and verified loss in immediate collision. Let the viewer feel what the company, employees or customers had to lose, without melodrama.

**Dramatic motor:** Why it once worked -> first crack -> why a plausible response did not solve it -> compounding pressure -> breaking point -> true causal diagnosis. Known endings are fine; the surprise is *how*.

**Signature payoff:** One original, evidenced explanation that distinguishes structural fragility, management response and precipitating event. A memorable warning, not a generic moral.

**Creative red flags:** doom narration, every failure blamed on Amazon or arrogance, chronological Wikipedia recaps, dramatic bankruptcy figures presented without denominator, villains invented for plot clarity.

**Distinct from others:** The operating system's loss of viability, not a specific trade (Changing Hands), one discrete strategic choice (Money Moves) or deception as the main mechanism (Fool's Gold).

**Pilot test:** Could a viewer explain why the company originally deserved to win? Does the middle deliver a true causal reversal rather than a checklist of problems? Does a source-backed counterargument genuinely alter the final takeaway?

**Openings to compare:** iconic product versus vanished outcome; overlooked early warning versus familiar false explanation; decision that looked like a rescue but deepened the weakness.

## Current director-led creative production rule (all eight channels)

Green Knight now owns the title/thumbnail promise, first frame, detailed first 20–35 seconds, narration dynamics, story movements and breathing points, visual sequence design with recurring environments and transformations, selective evidence/text, and ending callback. The editor executes the work, rather than independently choosing the story's visual conception. Read business/EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md and business/DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW.md; these supersede any earlier channel-specific editor-owned guidance.

Per story, release only the three fully reviewed final files in `editor-packet/`: `01_SCRIPT.txt`, `02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md`, `03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md`. Script, performance, visual plan and their integration MUST all pass independent and joined QA after all revisions. No formerly scripted episode automatically qualifies for packet approval. Preserve this channel's distinctive audience promise and visual identity; do not force identical transitions or formulaic “Chapter 1” cards.

## Required popcorn-good channel contract (2026-10-11)
Read business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md. **Earned engagement is the first creative test, with truthful evidence as the minimum floor.** A first-timer is hooked by a recognizable success-versus-collapse paradox. An expert who knows the famous failure gets a source-backed explanation of original advantage, structural fragility, response and trigger that challenges simplistic headlines. **BAD:** chronological list of product flaws and a final 'product-market fit.' **GOOD:** an initially rational advantage becoming a constraint, with documented decisions that make the ending newly understandable. Quibi: content rights, paid-platform economics and Roku distribution should illuminate the failure, not just recap app complaints.

Run TWO explicit tests on every new channel concept, idea and scripted episode: **newcomer pull** (immediate human stakes, first-listen comprehension and real desire to continue) and **already-expert reward** (a specific warranted new lens or insightful causal model). Name the weakest likely middle sequence, create authentic reversals, and show how the best strategic insight *unfolds inside the plot*. A correct but predictable lecture, a generic takeaway or beautiful prose without audience momentum is REVISE/REFRAME, never 'ready.' Never invent twists or inflate unsourced certainty. On review/revise, record both verdicts and actual paragraph/beat fixes rather than praising general quality.
