# Built to Scale creative contract
**Promise:** The audience watches a fascinating business predicament unfold and leaves with a more precise understanding of how excellent businesses work. Instruction is embedded inside the narrative, not an instruction manual.

**Hook:** "Why does one busy crew finish early while another falls behind?" or "Why would a contractor refuse a huge job?" only when defensible evidence supports the premise. Establish a vivid, relatable problem, not a glossary, keynote, list or dollars-only headline.

**Dramatic engine:** a real company/person/decision -> plausible answer -> new constraints -> earned discovery -> actual obstacle, downside or countercase -> informed revised choice -> a final mechanism that changes understanding. Be flexible: the true story sets its shape.

**Expert reward:** an operator learns a sourced mechanism, decision rule or exception that goes beyond "work harder, hire well, watch costs." **Newcomer reward:** the same story works as entertainment without prior knowledge of trades or management.

**No narrow job-title channel:** stories may mention foremen or project managers, but never assume that is the whole audience. Include growth, quality, training, culture, customer relationships and choices with universal business appeal.

**No imitation or parallel uploads:** distinct editorial purpose and source-led scripts versus Hard Costs, not mere different edits. No fake internal documents, earnings, past decisions or testimonials. Models are explicitly hypothetical and sensitivity-tested.

**Producer owns intellectual film:** Green Knight specifies research truth, exact spoken script, selected title/thumbnail/opening, narrator directions, 6–9 purposeful visual sequences and graphic transformations. The editor retains technical execution freedom. After research/voice/visual/integrated QA, release exactly three final editor-facing files under the governing director-packet standard.
