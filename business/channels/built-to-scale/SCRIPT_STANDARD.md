# Built to Scale script standard
8–10 minutes of finished video (480–600 seconds measured on the actual cut).

**Sound like a story.** Open on an understandable business predicament, person, customer, job or decision, and deliver an early genuinely useful payoff. A deeper complication should raise stakes, test the attractive strategy and produce a changed understanding. Never narrate "five steps to improve efficiency" or start a recap of management concepts. Do not make money the mandatory measure of success.

**Show operator intelligence through consequences.** One real transaction/process/decision at a time, plain English, sparse jargon, visible economics, and credible alternative explanations. A financial or operational diagram should reveal the insight instead of asking the audience to read dense tables while the narrator speaks.

**Truth:** precise subjects, periods, geography and numerical labels. Historical facts are sourced. Worked models and composite examples are plainly stated as illustrative, and results never implied to be achieved by named companies. No assumed earnings, public safety guidance masquerading as job training, invented interviews or guaranteed returns.

**Mandatory process:** `script.txt` begins exactly `TITLE: <metadata.title>`, one blank line, then the canonical spoken narration only. Title line never spoken. Preserve full script SHA-256, paragraph IDs, source claim/scene anchors and independently reviewed working narration and visual directors. Director designs one title/thumbnail/first 20–35 seconds and connected purposeful scenes. Release exactly `01_SCRIPT.txt`, `02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md` and `03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md` only when approved voice, visual pilot, all evidence, individual and integration reviews truly pass. Text review alone is not audio/video approval.
