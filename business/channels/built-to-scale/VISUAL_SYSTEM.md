# The Operator's Blueprint: reusable visual toolkit
Simple, premium components that make business decisions concrete, reusable at reasonable outsourcing cost without producing interchangeable films.

1. **One job:** customer call -> quote -> service/job -> time/materials -> invoice -> repeat.
2. **Two operators:** comparable inputs split into two evidence-backed outcomes, with differences gradually revealed.
3. **Decision fork:** open a branch vs buy a competitor, hire vs subcontract, large project vs many small ones; show real opportunity cost and failure modes.
4. **Busy vs productive:** crew hours, travel, rework, material readiness and completed work, without implying workers are lazy.
5. **Customer machine:** leads, repeat work, reputation, service quality and contribution economics.
6. **Owner-to-team network:** decision rights, managers, dispatch, customers and risk when founder stops being the hub.
7. **First-to-regional growth:** one credible unit -> managers -> branches; reveal new cash, labor and management requirements.
8. **Revenue-to-cash waterfall:** price, direct costs, overhead, timing and debt obligations, always historically sourced or marked illustrative.
9. **Operations bottleneck:** schedule, customer promises, materials and workforce; show a delayed handoff accumulating downstream consequences.
10. **Deal model:** purchase, financing, integration, cash generation, ownership and plausible downside.
11. **Skills and training:** learning, quality inspections, mentoring, feedback loops and outcomes without fictional training data.
12. **Evidence timeline:** genuine dated company documents, reports and interviews; label reconstructions and verify rights.

**Example, “Why the Best Contractors Turn Down Work”:** show the attractive job, uncover the true manpower/time commitment and alternative opportunities, compare two honest strategies, then reveal the context where refusal or acceptance is better. Start and end with the same job as the audience's understanding changes. Require real documents or an openly conditional scenario.

Keep animations to meaningful story transformations and reuse modules, not canned cookie-cutter storyboards.
