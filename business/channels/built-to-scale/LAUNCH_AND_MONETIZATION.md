# Built to Scale launch / growth-media hypothesis
**Working brand and proposed editor experiment, not a live channel.**

**Editorial:** The growth playbook for ambitious trade people and owners. Video outcomes include getting customers, hiring, financing, managing crews, expanding or buying businesses without losing control. Alternate between **Growth Playbook, Built From Nothing, Big Decision and Growing Pains**; don't run an entire season of "$100 million" titles or niche foreman instruction.

**Representative five-format pilot mix:** (1) trade-specific growth playbook, (2) first hires/capacity threshold, (3) credit/cash/debt finance fork, (4) a real named-company scaling case study, (5) an acquisition or growth breakdown. Owner chooses exact topics; proposed slate is not a commission.

**Prospective Fiverr arrangement:** User provides topic shortlist; producer may recommend/select one for confirmation, research and write original story/script and visual plan and produce narration/edit. **Owner reviews and approves every full script before production and personally chooses/approves narration voice.** Confirm this division, research/source delivery, revisions, licensing, visual references, price and schedule before agreeing. No contract or editor is yet in place. Keep Green Knight editorial quality gates and history. See EDITOR_WORKFLOW.md.

**Visual test:** a short integrated sample demonstrating customer/crew expansion or financing/acquisition graphics plus authentic trades footage and selected voice. Owner approval required before scaling outsourced batches.

**Commercial future hypotheses:** core owner/operator audience may support B2B trade-software, recruiting, lending/insurance, marketing, services sponsors, owner newsletters and practical products. Validate actual audience composition and sponsor demand; neither high RPM nor demand is presumed. Protect editorial independence and private information.

**Performance:** track honest CTR by traffic source, 30-second retention, full-view satisfaction, return viewers, voluntary audience self-identification, production cost, owner time and operator-quality feedback. More channels don't guarantee more reach. Avoid nearly identical Hard Costs/BTS scripts/footage; differentiate underlying decision and narrative.

**Runtime:** parent Green Knight standard currently targets 8–10 minutes, but the owner has asked prospective producers for a recommended duration rather than locking Fiverr pricing and creative decisions to one length. Revisit configuration/validator deliberately if a different length is agreed; do not silently claim the repo is already duration-flexible.
