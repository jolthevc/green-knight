# Built to Scale launch thesis and second-editor experiment
**This is a planned media experiment, not an operating YouTube channel or proof of demand.**

Two-channel strategy: Hard Costs remains a broad construction documentary brand; Built to Scale tests premium business stories that make operational excellence and owner decisions understandable. Editors, brands, scripts and video topics should be distinct. Uploading minimally altered versions of the same documentary would risk duplication and teach us little.

Pilot: owner selects five varied candidates across growth, operations, important decisions and business comparisons. Ask a different editor to produce one 30–90-second integrated style sample, then one fully reviewed 8–10 minute film before repeat commitments. Avoid assuming editor pricing, weekly cadence, licensing, music, AI narration or agreements not yet confirmed.

Measurement: actual impressions/CTR by source, first-30-second retention, average viewing, returning viewers, production cost, and voluntary comments or opt-in newsletter interest. Five videos are directional, not statistical proof. Track general-interest reach separately from people who self-identify as trade/business operators.

Possible longer-term outcomes **only if validated**: sponsorships from sector vendors (with clear disclosures), operator newsletter, recruitment, research/benchmarks, targeted services and future business products solving recurrent pain points. An owner/operator audience is a potential advantage, not guaranteed RPM or an excuse for sponsor-influenced research. Protect confidential employer/transaction details.
