# Built to Scale: prospective independent Fiverr producer workflow
**Scope being explored, not a signed agreement.** The owner described a different division of work for Built to Scale than the standard Green Knight handoff used with other channels.

## Requested relationship
1. **Owner supplies** a vetted shortlist of possible topics. Producer may choose/recommend one from that list **for owner confirmation** (not automatic commission).
2. **Prospective producer** researches original sources, constructs the story and creates the **complete draft script**, plus proposed visual treatment, graphics, narration choices and production plan. Producer is accountable for providing citations and assumptions sufficient to evaluate all factual/financial assertions.
3. **Owner reviews and approves each complete script before** voice recording, paid animation or video production. Revisions to approved script require owner reapproval. Research/evidence must support the scripted claims.
4. **Owner personally chooses and approves the narrator/voice** through actual audition/reference audio, BEFORE use. Provider access, settings, costs, music/image rights and revision terms to be agreed.
5. Producer then performs footage/graphics/voice production and edit after written approval, using licensed sources, and submits a cut for consolidated revisions and final owner approval. Pricing, deliveries, order dates, length and timeline remain unagreed.
6. The editor may propose stronger dramatic, visual or practical solutions, but substantive factual or script changes require approval.

## Integration with the canonical repository
The global Green Knight editor-handoff standard assumes Green Knight supplies an already-approved exact script and director plan. **For this proposed producer-led commissioning path, the producer's research/script/visual drafts are inputs to Green Knight's approval system, not a shortcut around it.** Once the owner approves them and evidence/editorial/voice/visual gates pass, the canonical story repository stores the approved script, narration director and visual director; release the same exactly-three-file final packet when applicable. Do not falsely mark a draft as production_ready, invent a selected story, bypass source-based audit, or claim the producer was formally hired.

**Voice and scripts are non-negotiable owner approval checkpoints.** Default network video length is configured at 8–10 minutes but producer is invited to suggest optimal duration before new financial/schedule commitments.

This file captures owner preferences, not an executed contract, and does not supersede the shared requirements for truth, compliance, original work and final film quality.
