## Recommended initial five-film composition
One **100-truck/trade-specific growth** film (BTS-I0013 only after hard-costs differentiation), one **customer acquisition** or first big project, one **hiring/organizational ceiling**, one **financing or acquisition fork**, and one **documented real-company case study**. The order is a pitch strategy, not authorization to select topics or place an editor order.

## Minimum episode pitch before choosing
One hook, actual protagonist/company or labeled model, measurable growth ambition, source path, clear operator mechanism, serious failure case, real visual opportunities, and distinction from all other Green Knight videos. Historical company examples require primary-source verification before any numerical claim or narrative assertion. Do not assume a company endorsement, interview or access to records.
