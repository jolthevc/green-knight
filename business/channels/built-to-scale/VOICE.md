# Built to Scale: narration voice
Practical, ambitious, clear-eyed and quietly excited by entrepreneurs who build durable institutions out of ordinary trades. Talk like a remarkable business storyteller showing the viewer how a small operation becomes a professional organization, **not** a get-rich coach, finance instructor or motivational salesperson.

The viewer should feel the big aspiration and the true cost: first customers, a difficult hire, a loan payment, the second location, an acquisition that may go wrong. Respect field workers and owners. Deliver debt, credit, bonding and deal terms in ordinary English; one numeric idea at a time. Give space for a revealing financial diagram or a founder's hard-earned decision.

Real named-company case studies should sound investigative, neither celebratory nor cynical. Facts and competing explanations trump origin mythology. External producer may propose voices, but **owner selects and approves** final voice before audio/video production. narration.json still version 0/unselected; provider, settings and approved audition are not implied.
