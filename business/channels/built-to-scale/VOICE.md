# Built to Scale voice
Conversational, assured, curious and warm. Sounds like a sharp business-documentary narrator noticing how extraordinary businesses actually operate, not a consultant reciting a management framework or an aggressive “scale to millions” coach.

Follow real people and decisions; let consequences, surprises and human stakes carry engagement. Respect technicians, customers, workers and owners alike. Explain one complex operational or numerical relationship at a time, with natural breath and pauses. Avoid fake personal expertise, narration of imagined interviews, jargon and AI-sounding hype.
