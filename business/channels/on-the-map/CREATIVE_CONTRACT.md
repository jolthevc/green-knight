# On the Map: creative contract
The entertainment is **seeing a familiar part of the world differently because one place has an unlikely economic role**. Discovery is the emotional reward, not tourism or a city history lecture.

**Universal hook:** Begin with a rose, chip, aircraft, container, product, daily experience or recognizable outcome. Let the unusual place emerge as the answer to a surprising question; don't require the viewer to care about a place they cannot locate.

**Dramatic motor:** Surprising present outcome -> non-obvious starting conditions -> first real foothold -> why others arrived -> a reinforcing advantage -> alternative/constraint -> who captures value now. Geographic cause and persistence must be source-supported; no success was inevitable.

**Signature payoff:** A lucid explanation of why *there*, why the advantage endured or changed, and what the location makes possible for people or goods elsewhere.

**Creative red flags:** municipal histories, 'strategic location' as a magical cause, place names and historical dates before stakes, vague geopolitical grandeur, tourism promotion, calling a complicated region empty, exaggerating who benefits locally.

**Distinct from others:** The specific geography is necessary for the answer. Business biographies and strategic corporate choices belong elsewhere when removing the place changes nothing.

**Pilot test:** Hide the place name in the first title draft. Does the premise remain irresistible? After listening, can a non-expert name the starting constraint, at least two causal transitions and why the place still matters?

**Strong reference:** Aalsmeer script finds an original insight about digital pricing versus physical delivery. Consider a flower-first opening, and preserve the truth that not every flower passes through the Dutch town.
