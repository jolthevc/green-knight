# On the Map

Discover why the world's money, businesses and opportunities ended up where they did. The central question is **why here?** The deeper payoff is what keeps activity here, who benefits, and what could change it.

The eighth manual business channel. IDs: OTM-I#### for ideas, OTM-V#### for stories. Every finished episode is 8–10 minutes (480–600 seconds), including pauses and closing. No episode, voice, visual identity, editor package purchase or upload schedule is selected by this setup.

2. Apply [Voice](VOICE.md), [Style ownership](STYLE.md), [channel.json](channel.json) and [narration.json](narration.json).
3. Use [Start a chat](START_A_CHAT.md), the shared [workflow](../../WORKFLOW.md) and [idea registry](../../ideas/registry.json).
4. Follow [engagement](../../ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md), [editorial gates](../../EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md), [script review](../../SCRIPT_REVIEW.md), [narration](../../NARRATION_STANDARD.md) and [editor handoff](../../EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md).

The foundation: establish the place before its transformation; distinguish the first foothold from the mechanisms that sustain concentration; show the transitions rather than assuming success was inevitable. Research alternatives, beneficiaries and constraints. Places are inhabited communities, not empty backdrops.

Dalton, Louisville and Aspen are imported as proposed candidates with preliminary source leads. None is selected or fully researched. See [candidate notes](../../ideas/batches/2026-10-11-on-the-map-foundation-candidates.md) and [stories](stories/README.md).
