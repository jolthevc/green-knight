## Adversarial and currency of facts

- **No single geographic gate:** Not every Kenyan or other imported flower enters Schiphol or Aalsmeer. S005 identifies multiple airports and hubs. P011 is a deliberately hypothetical example.
- **Pricing versus delivery:** RFH's March 2026 report S015/S016 found expansion of a proposed centralized/location-independent system lacked expected benefits and caused logistical imbalances. That finding must be attributed to its decision, not converted into a general ban on internet auctioning or proof no flower can ship direct.
- **Failure modes separate:** July 2026 peak-delay report S017/S019 and S018's 102,000 transactions on ONE peak day support physical coordination stresses. Do not assert that the March model caused the July operational delays.
- **Accounting:** 2025 RFH €5.4bn traded product value includes flowers AND plants; about €12m net cooperative profit (S003/S014). Neither is a retail flower price nor a farm-level margin; avoid the invalid ratio.
- **Date and locations:** Original Aalsmeer local auction 1911–12, merged complex opened in 1972, regional hubs Naaldwijk/Rijnsburg; modern airport infrastructure predates neither local growers nor their 1911 auction.
- **Environmental comparison:** Fairtrade commissioned study S007 concerns specified supply chains to Switzerland; S008 describes risk categories, not a survey establishing all wages or injuries.
- **Search versus source access:** S016 and S017 are partial first-party indexed text, while their claims are supported by fully opened publisher/operator reprints S015, S018, S019. S014 annual summary is partially accessible but figures also appear in opened S003.
