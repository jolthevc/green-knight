## Source disagreement and adversarial decisions

- S001/S011 disagree at the precision of a January 1912 starting date, so the spoken history says winter of 1911–12.
- Royal FloraHolland 2025 product turnover and cooperative net profit are separate categories and should not be divided to invent a unit margin. S003 opened; S014 only partially retrievable and not sole support.
- S013's 50/50 discussion concerns a channel mix measure, while a 2025 annual PDF search excerpt indicates a different share of product value; script avoids a precise direct-versus-clock percentage.
- Schiphol trade and other European airports both matter. S005 confirms flow through Brussels, Liège and others; the Scottish-sounding claim 'all flowers go through Amsterdam' was rejected.
- S007 case-study LCA is conditional, not a categorical climate ranking. S008 wages are risks, not a sample of every farm.
- P011 is an invented example explicitly labeled illustrative; no specific actual bouquet, cargo shipment, date or party is asserted.
