## Defensible causal chain

Existing inhabited fishing/farming town and access to Amsterdam → lake reclamation/flower specialism → perishability and trader bargaining power → two cooperative auctions (1911–12) → descending clock, assortment, buyer participation and settlement → regional co-location and municipality retaining merged facility (1972) → Schiphol plus international import routes → expansion of farming abroad/direct contracts → digital trading versus persistent delivery coordination → 2026 policy reversal and Aalsmeer peak-capacity evidence. This is not an inevitable or exclusive path: other Dutch/European hubs and direct routes exist.

## Hook alternatives and chosen opening

- *Chosen:* 'A Kenyan rose can bypass Holland, but a Dutch trading cooperative tried to loosen its location-dependent auction system and backed away in March 2026.' Immediate universal entry and a documented contemporary conflict; no fabricated shipment.
- Rejected: 'All roses pass through the Netherlands', false physical implication.
- Rejected: 'The Dutch invented the flower auction', false given earlier produce and Amsterdam auctions.
