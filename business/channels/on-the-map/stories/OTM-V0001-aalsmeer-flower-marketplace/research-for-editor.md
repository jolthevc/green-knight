## The premise and non-negotiable distinctions

Aalsmeer grew a market out of growers' collective action around 1911–12. Its role today reflects several auction/logistics locations, international farms, Schiphol and other European airports, direct contracted trade and digital buying. The story is not about every imported flower necessarily flying into Amsterdam or passing through the Aalsmeer complex.
