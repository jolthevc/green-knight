# Research standard: explain the place, test the explanation

Read the shared workflow/evidence requirements and CHANNEL.md. Research before settling the thesis. No fixed source count replaces coverage of the decisive claims.

## Define scope in brief.md
Record format, central question, place/boundary, relevant period, outcome metric, familiar entry, provisional explanation and strongest uncertainty. City proper, metro, county, region, corridor and nation are not interchangeable. Define the actual economic catchment even if the familiar city name is used in the title. Check that data match the claim's area, date, unit and population.

## Research packet
In research.md and sources-and-claims.md establish:
1. **Starting conditions:** relevant economic life, people, resources, institutions, access and missing capabilities before the transformation. State what is unknown.
2. **First foothold:** the documented activity, decision or investment; actor, incentive, constraints, funding and chronology. Avoid invented first-ever claims.
3. **Transition evidence:** cause/action → economic consequence → next participant's incentive. Record supporting evidence, confidence and competing explanation for each important link.
4. **Persistence:** reasons firms, workers and customers remain now versus reasons they arrived. Test relocation, congestion, substitution, changing technology and declining advantages.
5. **Alternative:** a plausible comparison location, unrealized option or competing causal explanation. Explain imperfect comparability; distinguish observed evidence from an unobservable counterfactual.
6. **Value and incidence:** who pays, earns, owns, funds and bears risk. Separate throughput, sales, local value added, wages, profits, land gains, taxes and external ownership. A famous employer is not the entire local economy.
7. **Friction and response:** infrastructure, labor, housing, environment, finance, concentration or policy limits where material. Distinguish proposed response, implemented response and measured result. Record cost, trade-off and residual risk. An announced project is not a completed transformation.
8. **People and distribution:** supported effects on residents, workers, owners and newcomers; no invented personal anecdotes or unsupported claims that everyone gained or lost.
9. **Current status and uncertainty:** timestamp volatile conditions and separate historic mechanism from present figures. Recheck consequential current facts before production/publication.

## Source discipline
Use original statistical data, municipal/transport records, company filings, historical archives, academic research and strong local reporting. Company and tourism/investment-promotion sources are interested accounts, useful for facts within their knowledge but insufficient alone for causal or success claims. Corroborate origin legends and superlatives. Do not confuse a search excerpt or unopened lead with inspected evidence.

Trace every consequential script claim to exact source passages and paragraph anchors in the shared evidence schema. Separate facts, inference and illustrative models. When causal identification is weak, use appropriately qualified explanation; do not assert a policy alone caused prosperity. Check alternative explanations and failed examples, not only survivors.

For economic-impact estimates inspect who commissioned them, period, boundary, direct/indirect/induced components and counterfactual. Do not add overlapping figures or treat gross activity as net benefit. Capacity differs from actual throughput; total output differs from per-person income; nominal growth differs from real growth. Do not combine incompatible vintages into a trend.

## Geography and media accuracy
Keep factual location/route/boundary/date notes and useful source links in research-for-editor.md when relevant. This is accuracy support, not a shot list. A schematic is not a measured route; historical borders are not current borders; straight-line distance is not travel time. Label illustrative reconstructions and do not present invented maps, documents or crowds as evidence. Verify the actual delivered media later. The editor chooses presentation.

## Synthesis gate
Before scripted, synthesis-and-outline.md must contain the core answer, starting position, origin-versus-persistence distinction (or a reason it does not apply), supported causal chain, strongest alternative, value-capture distinction, meaningful constraint, before/after understanding by beat, weakest middle stretch and a resolved ending. Reframe or block if the central geographic claim is unsupported.
