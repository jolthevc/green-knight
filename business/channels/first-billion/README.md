# First Billion

**Promise:** Understand, step by step, how a major business or fortune was built, what made it scale, and who captured the value.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrative episodes for curious adults. Historical reconstructions, worked business models, industry wealth stories, comparisons and tests of whether a path could work under different conditions.

**Permanent editorial DNA:** make the path from actual starting resources to massive scale feel **earned**. Every episode should reveal its first viable customer or deal, the counterparty who must say yes, concrete competitors and failure gates, proportionate mitigants, and the financing/ownership bridge. Preserve ambition, but never assume a mature large asset or institutional capital as the first move. Historical and hypothetical formats have different evidence obligations. ## Current production and delivery rules
An episode-creation request develops the research, script, full **working narration director**, and **working visual director** together. Review/revise checks all three and integration, releasing only the three final editor-packet files after approval. The selected narrator must be genuinely approved, not inferred from ElevenLabs prompt drafts.
