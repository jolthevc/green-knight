## Five highest-leverage changes

1. **P1 starting position:** P003 must become a **small independent-owner service-client** pitch, with the real challenge of permission/credibility, no pre-owned property. Prove demand and owner value before ownership.
2. **P1 financing bridge:** Replace assumption of the first ~$5.9m equity raise with a **smaller modeled property** and a conditional, auditable bank/seller-retained-equity/investor joint venture. Seller and lender can say no; no capital-free magic.
3. **P1 competitors and local downside:** Check true facility-count/ownership concentration, recent PSA–NSA *closed* combination, EXR third-party-management platform, 2026 oversupply/concessions before promising an arbitrary giant rollup. Every obstacle requires proposed operating response.
4. **P1 early proof / product mechanics:** Show meager management gross fees, owner contract pilot, property operating improvement and how revenue gains differ from capital purchased, net of real improvement cost.
5. **P1 narrative scale and ending:** Grow from 1 management client to a cluster, regional credibility, future *larger* properties, then 180 hypothetical owned sites and investor stack; close on earned trust and real ownership rights, not "anyone can buy 180 sites".

**KEEP** hypothetical model status, after-property-debt metric, exact 180 large-site target arithmetic as distant possibility, 2025 operator margin check, $463m downside, investor-supplied equity and conditional founder share. No invented retiree interview, quote, actual bank offer or documented zero-to-billion founder. No history rewrite of earlier versions.
