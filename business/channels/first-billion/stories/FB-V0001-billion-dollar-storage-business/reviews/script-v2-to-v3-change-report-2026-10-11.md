It introduces a second related valuation calculation in P010 and P016, which could overload first-listen memory if the voice does not clearly identify that the added $50k is *annual* NOI, and ~$769k is *asset value*, not a one-time gift or cash. A viewer seeking a practical launch timeline still won't get a validated capital-access route, and the title remains ambitious relative to the thought experiment. These are honest limitations, not grounds to invent a founder biography.
