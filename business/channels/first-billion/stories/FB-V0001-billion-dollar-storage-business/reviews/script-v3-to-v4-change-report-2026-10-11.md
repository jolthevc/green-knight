## Did this rewrite make anything worse?

**Yes, materially possible drawbacks:** v4 has more early conditional transactions and local operational stages, giving less time for the actual 180-property end-state math. A new listener may get lost distinguishing seller rollover *equity* from debt and outside cash. Added industry comparisons risk time or geography errors if edited carelessly. And a seemingly attainable management-first route could mislead viewers into assuming an owner will trust an unknown manager, or that a bank will finance a no-founder-capital JV. The script explicitly shows those points can fail; review them in actual voice.
