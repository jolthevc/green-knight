## Did revision worsen anything?

**Potentially yes:** V2 is more explicitly a **worked property-equity thought experiment** and less of an actionable "how to" for entrepreneurs, exposing an honest limitation of the broad title. The financial sequence P014–P019 still needs voice testing, and v2's founder "you" remains illustrative rather than a documented protagonist. No evidence supports a real 180-asset acquisition calendar, acquisition IRR or founder earned upside. These should remain candid, not covered with synthetic personal anecdotes or cinematic promises.

**Benefit outweighs cost:** instead of generic scale arithmetic, the listener learns exactly why a financed billion-dollar property position and billion dollars of founder-created/liquid wealth are not the same thing.
