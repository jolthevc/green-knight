**Thesis:** Capital can assemble a billion dollars of property equity without automatically generating a billion dollars of new investor wealth. Genuine upside comes from future rental returns, attractive purchase basis, productive operations and development spreads, net of capital costs. Founder wealth then depends on actual contractual equity claims.

**Opening P001–P002:** An ordinary empty room produces recurring revenue, but asset value, new wealth and founder cash are separate. Specific modeled US property-equity metric supplied immediately.

**Early unit P003–P007:** 100 SF effective $150/month equivalent across occupancy; whole store 65,000 SF at $18/year/available SF, 65% NOI margin, ~$760,500 annual NOI, 6.5% hypothetical cap gives ~$11.7m property value. Public Storage and CubeSmart 2025 same-store margins benchmark but do not prove the assumptions. This is the first surprising earned valuation payoff.

**Funding P008–P009:** Paying ~$11.7m for an asset assumed worth ~$11.7m does not create instant gain, even though rental investment may earn future returns. A 50% loan requires almost $6m of equity money, and whoever supplies it has claims on the outcome.

**Actual wealth creation P010:** An explicitly fictional incremental $50k/year property NOI improvement could add ~$769k to property value at unchanged 6.5% cap before the costs. This is the missing mechanism in v2. It is conditional, not a performance forecast.

**Expansion P011–P013:** Buy, develop or manage, with different ownership and financing, supported by 2025 Public Storage actual 87 acquisitions/$945.6m and Extra Space 1,856 third-party-managed properties. Supply and prices differ in every location; no proven 180-site pipeline.

**Scaling P014–P015:** At 180 identical sites, gross property ~$2.106bn; debt ~$1.053bn; residual property equity ~$1.053bn. But equity investors funded about a billion of the acquisition price. This is *a balance sheet*, not investor returns earned.

**Additional value P016:** Successful conditional +$50k NOI/site × 180 = $9m extra annual NOI, at same cap ~$138.46m more property value before the costs of gaining it. This illustrates how an operator could enlarge the pie rather than simply acquire more.

**Cash and failure P017–P019:** Portfolio property NOI ~$136.89m; less $63.18m assumed interest, $10m HQ costs, $15m capex allowance leaves ~$48.71m annual remainder before other claims. Stress revenue -10%, margin 60%, cap 7.5%, existing debt fixed yields ~$463.32m residual property equity. Not a prediction.

**Ownership P020–P021:** The founder's 20% stake is hypothetical and cannot be assumed without personal investment or negotiated incentive rights. If actually held, on a rounded $1bn property-equity base it is ~$200m before preferences, illiquidity and tax.
