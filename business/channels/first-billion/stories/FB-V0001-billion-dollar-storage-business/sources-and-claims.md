## Critical accuracy limits

- The modeled "billion" is US property-equity *at a modeled appraisal point*, after modeled property borrowing. It is not annual revenue, personal cash, audited parent-company equity, a known transaction outcome or a documented historical crossing.
- The $18/year/available SF is an effective annual average, inclusive of empty capacity, not $18 per occupied month. The $150/month on a ten-by-ten area is a unit analogy, not an asking-rent price.
- 2025 REIT margins are real reported same-store metrics, not a universal 2026 national storage-company gross margin.
- The 6.5% cap rate, 6% borrowing interest rate, 50% debt ratio, 180 identical stores and 20% founder share are **illustrative assumptions**.
- **The v3 operational uplift** $50k annual NOI/site and $9m across 180 is a *conditional* future improvement. At 6.5%, capitalized value rises ~$769k per building or ~$138.5m across portfolio, before the improvement cost and other changes. Do not claim real-world execution success or net profit.
- PSA 87 acquired facilities and Extra Space 1,856 **third-party managed** sites are source-supported facts with different ownership status and different prices, so don't use managed stores to pad owned store count.
- The stress assumes revenue drop, margin erosion, higher cap rate and **debt held fixed**. $463.32m equity is a snapshot stress, not a market quote, loss realized on a sale or projected probability.
- 20% ownership is expressly conditional on negotiated rights or actual founder capital. A sponsor does not receive a stake for free by finding buildings.
- Final 3-part income/pricing/ownership distinction is conceptual synthesis. The editor owns media treatment; do not present hypothetical artifacts as historical documentary materials.
