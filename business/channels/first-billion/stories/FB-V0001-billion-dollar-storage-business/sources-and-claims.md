## Guardrails

- The **2026 Almanac** estimates >65,000 US active storage facilities; its 19.3% share of facilities operated by five largest is sourced from 2025 top-operator data and includes managed facilities. Their rentable SF share is 35.6%. Other large regional companies account for more market. Do **not** say 80% mom-and-pop owned.
- McDonald's 2025 US 13,706, Starbucks 2025 **North America** 18,311 (includes Canada), so combined US total is even less than 32,017, far below 65,000. Never claim storage outnumbers all fast-food restaurants/franchises.
- July 22 2026 Public Storage NSA transaction is **closed**, a large listed REIT deal, not evidence that all buyers are PE funds.
- First 20k-SF site's **$14 annual effective revenue/SF, $280k revenue, $140k property annual NOI, 5% manager fee, 8% cap, 50/30/20 capitalization and 7% debt** are hypotheses. $14k operator fee is INCLUDED in property expenses that leave $140k NOI. Do not deduct it again or claim a five-percent quote.
- Hypothetical seller *rollover equity* 30% is not a subordinated note; bank borrowing 50% and independent investor cash 20%. Founder has no automatic equity, fee, promote or zero-down financing commitment.
- A sustained $30k/year extra NOI could imply $375k extra gross property value at constant 8% cap, **before implementation cost**, not a received gain.
- At institutional stage 65k-SF *average* sites are not the first site. 180 owned equivalents ×$11.7m assumed gross value = $2.106bn; less $1.053bn property debt = $1.053bn total property equity; outside investors supplied substantial equity. Third-party managed sites are not part of this valuation.
- Stress property equity $463.32m assumes −10% effective revenue, NOI margin 60%, 7.5% cap, **existing debt fixed**; not a forecast.
- Founder 20% is expressly conditional contractual stake, approximately $200m gross paper value on rounded $1bn, before preferences, taxes and liquidity.
