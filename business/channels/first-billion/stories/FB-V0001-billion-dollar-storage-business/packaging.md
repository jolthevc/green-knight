## Video description draft

Could a business built from ordinary storage rooms become worth a billion dollars? We start with one hypothetical facility, calculate what its rent could actually leave behind, and work toward a portfolio of 180 properties. Then we examine the much harder questions: where does the acquisition money come from, what happens when valuation assumptions change, and how much of a billion-dollar company might the founder actually own?

This is a **worked financial illustration**, not a description of an actual company's path or a market quote. Historical 2025 results from Public Storage, CubeSmart and Extra Space are used as benchmarks. Operating assumptions, debt costs, cap rates, ownership percentages and stress conditions are illustrative, not investment advice.
