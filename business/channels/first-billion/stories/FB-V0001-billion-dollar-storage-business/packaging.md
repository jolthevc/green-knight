## Draft YouTube description

If you wanted to build a billion-dollar self-storage business but couldn't afford a single building, where would you start? This episode builds an **illustrative** path from providing operating services to a small independent owner, through an optional seller-retained partnership, into local acquisitions and eventually an enormous investor-financed portfolio.

We'll face real structural obstacles: owners don't have to trust you, management fees can be tiny, national operators are consolidating, local construction can crush rent, lenders can say no, and raising money does not automatically create founder wealth.

All first-property examples, interest rates, management fees, property valuations, seller rollover amounts, founder interests and 180-facility projections are **hypothetical teaching assumptions**, not actual offers, past transactions or investment advice. Historical facts about industry fragmentation, restaurant counts, the July 2026 Public Storage / National Storage Affiliates acquisition and Extra Space's third-party-management business come from original industry and issuer materials.
