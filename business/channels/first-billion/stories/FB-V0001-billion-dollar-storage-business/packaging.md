## Unique description opening

How could an ordinary self-storage facility become part of a billion-dollar business? We build a **hypothetical** US storage portfolio, one property at a time, using financial information from Public Storage, CubeSmart and Extra Space. Follow the money from rent through operating income, property valuation, debt and the ownership stake left behind, then see what happens when rents weaken and valuation rates change. Numbers for the proposed portfolio are illustrative, not market quotes or investment advice.
