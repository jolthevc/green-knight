# Narration and delivery

Sound like a curious, assured guide reconstructing an unexpectedly understandable business story. Warm, concrete and conversational. Respect the viewer's intelligence without assuming finance training. No motivational-guru promises, luxury fascination, constant astonishment or classroom cadence.

Use changing choices, constraints and consequences to create energy. Let the first viable unit and later ownership reveal land. Introduce each number through what it changes; avoid reading long model tables aloud. Vary rhythm and keep sparse emphasis. Important qualifications belong in natural wording, not a flood of sidecar instructions.

Keep valuation, revenue, personal wealth and cash distinct in speech. Read hypothetical outcomes conditionally. Do not perform certainty or triumph that the evidence has not earned. Explain inherited resources, financing risks and unsuccessful attempts without flattening the story into a disclaimer.

Follow [NARRATION_STANDARD.md](../../NARRATION_STANDARD.md). The narrator/provider is unselected. Verify names through authentic recordings where possible; do not guess phonetics. Keep cues non-spoken and tie them to current narration paragraphs/hash. Human narration-guide.md and delivery-cues.json must agree. Never assume unsupported provider controls.

Every canonical script begins with its non-spoken TITLE header. Strip that header for recording; P001 begins with the narration. Full assembled listening and measured runtime remain production checks, not text judgments.
