# Money Moves

**Promise:** Understand the business decision that changed the game, why it made sense at the time, and what it actually changed.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrated strategy stories for curious adults. A recognizable company, a real constraint, meaningful alternatives, a consequential choice and an evidence-backed outcome.
