# Hard Costs
**The business behind what gets built.** The ninth Green Knight channel in `business/`. Premium 8–10 minute narrated business documentaries on construction, trades, infrastructure and the built world's financial mechanics.

We serve two audiences: a curious non-industry viewer looking for a riveting story, and a construction operator seeking precise, defensible and sometimes actionable insight. The primary emphasis is ambitious operator playbooks, famous projects, the hidden companies of construction, money flows, deals, labor and failures.
