# Hard Costs scripts: 8–10 minutes
Write for natural narration and visual causality. Actual final runtime 480–600 seconds including holds; verify by watching real video. Approximate text WPM is not a final approval.

- A story opens on a real construction object, person, project or legible contradiction, immediately fulfilling the title/thumbnail promise. The first payoff arrives early; the deeper question develops through decisions, evidence, setbacks and reversals.
- At least one documentary-grade, evidence-backed construction-business insight must be present, even in engineering/history episodes.
- Every operator “how to” is a case study or well-labeled modeled playbook, **never** tips, guarantees, unearned first customer, fake interviews or implied universal scale. Distinguish annual company revenue, value, margins, owner proceeds and cash.
- In the audio, use one comprehensible economic relationship at a time. Let an evolving diagram carry the arithmetic on screen; don't narrate spreadsheets, laundry lists or excessive jargon.
- Source actual events, dates and numbers; label forecasts, claims and invented numerical scenarios transparently. Discuss plausible objections and real failure modes.
- Internal creative greenlight requires newcomer pull and genuinely useful/expert insight, concrete beat progression and an honest satisfying final explanation. Different subjects demand different narrative forms.

Canonical script.txt starts with exactly `TITLE: <metadata.title>` then one blank line, followed by spoken narration only. The title is never spoken. No internal cues, source links or scene tags in its body. Hash full bytes and synchronize version/cues/evidence.

Episode creation creates complete **working** narrator and visual directions along with the script, including one chosen final title/thumbnail treatment and ~6–9 meaningful visual transformations. Later independent and integrated review releases exactly 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md and 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md only when all gates and approved voice/style profile pass.
