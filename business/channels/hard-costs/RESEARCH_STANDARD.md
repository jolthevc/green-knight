# Hard Costs research standard
Follow the shared research/evidence files and original-source verification process.

For every story, define the real actor, geography, period, contract or asset, outcome, business mechanism and strongest competing explanation. Trace parties and incentives: developer/owner, financier, general contractor, specialty subs, suppliers, workforce and regulators, as relevant. Separate construction profit from cash received and project budget from vendor margin. Explain when money is earned, invoiced, retained, approved and paid.

For operator growth: document initial resources and advantages, first viable paid job, crew/service unit economics, acquisition cost, dispatch/utilization, sales, labor, safety, insurance, capital, overhead, cash conversion, customer mix, expansion funding, acquisition/integration and downside. Distinguish organic growth from purchased revenue. Never infer all small firms could recreate a named success. If modeling, mark every financial assumption **illustrative**, explain scenario sensitivity and do not imply observed company numbers.

For Icons/public works: original budget vs final spend, financing and ownership, builders, scopes, labor and cost changes, economic value distribution, dated/nominal vs adjusted amounts, counterfactual and long-run use. Don't invent dialogue, imply overruns equal fraud or treat public infrastructure as private profit.

For investigations: distinguish allegations, indictments, civil settlements, admissions, findings and convictions; cite original materials and fairly describe contested claims. For labor/legal/regulatory episodes, specify jurisdiction and time. For footage, confirm legitimate source rights; do not fabricate site photos, historical artifacts or charts styled as originals.

A business opportunity, sponsor category or potential venture is NOT evidence. Preserve editorial independence and disclose material relationships. Required sources, source locators, script paragraph anchors, narrative counterarguments and hashes follow the shared Green Knight evidence standard.
