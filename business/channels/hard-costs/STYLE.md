# Hard Costs visual style: Industrial Editorial
**Owner-selected direction to pilot, not a claimed locked/approved visual kit.**

A hybrid of Modern MBA's specific economics/storytelling, Wendover Productions' graphic maps/flows, WSJ's clean editorial financial charts and The B1M's authentic physical-world construction imagery. Practical Engineering is a secondary reference for clarity. Do not clone any channel's branded assets or footage.

**Suggested episode mix, not required quotas:** ~45% actual construction footage/photography, ~30% animated financial diagrams, ~15% sourced archival material/headlines, ~10% maps, blueprints and schematics. Adjust dramatically for Icons versus Operator Playbooks.

Visual grammar: charcoal, steel blue, warm off-white, and muted industrial orange are a **working palette**, unapproved pending a real pilot. Editorial typography, serious photography, phone-readable chart labels and restrained movement. Avoid cartoon hard hats, hazard-tape decoration, kinetic word subtitles, counterfeit archive, generic template montages and expensive one-off custom animation.

Direct 2–3 genuinely excellent explanatory graphic *movements* per documentary: money moves from party A to party B, a contract liability grows, a crew becomes a branch, a bottleneck changes capacity. Recurring environments and diagrams should evolve alongside the narration. Avoid literal illustration of every noun.

Use VISUAL_SYSTEM.md for reusable modules. Once owner approves actual logo, reference clips, typography/templates and integrated sample, version them in channel.json per shared standards. For now visual_profile version 0/status proposed is intentionally truthful.
