# Hard Costs narration
A sharp, warm, assured documentary guide who understands construction without sounding like a contractor training instructor. Curious, visual, sometimes witty, but never a motivational hustle coach or an artificial “top ten secrets” narrator. Calm authority for financial paradoxes, genuine tension when decisions turn, sensitivity in failures, and clear practical takeaways in operator stories.

Explain retainage, bonding, liens, backlog, HVAC or EBITDA in plain English the first time. Numbers should change our understanding, not accumulate. Mark counterfactuals and invented models as conditional. Don't invent quotes, signaled personal experience or claimed interviews.

Follow ../../NARRATION_STANDARD.md and shared script-hash/paragraph cue rules. No voice provider or narrator has been auditioned or approved; narration.json status unselected is intentional.
