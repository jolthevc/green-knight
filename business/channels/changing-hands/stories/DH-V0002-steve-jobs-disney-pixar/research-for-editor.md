## Plain story

Steve Jobs controlled just over half of Pixar as it negotiated a future beyond its five-original-movie distribution arrangement with Disney. Disney and Pixar co-owned covered films; Disney had exclusive distribution/exploitation rights and substantial influence over sequels and related businesses while Pixar's creative team made films. In January 2004 the companies broke off future distribution negotiations. Talks resumed under Disney's new CEO Bob Iger in June 2005; he proposed an acquisition on October 12. By Jan 12, 2006 leadership and Pixar culture questions were partly resolved but price ratio wasn't. On Jan 21 Jobs/Iger arrived at 2.3 Disney shares per Pixar share. Disney announced Jan 24, closed May 5, and issued Disney stock to all Pixar shareholders. Jobs became Disney's largest individual shareholder with ~138m shares (~6.3%), **not** majority controller or recipient of a $7.4bn cash check.
