# Editor research references | Steve Jobs, Disney and Pixar (DH-V0002)

## One-paragraph story

The Disney/Pixar distribution partnership stopped negotiating renewal terms in 2004. After Bob Iger became Disney chief executive, talks resumed in 2005, and on October 12 he raised a full company combination with Steve Jobs. Both boards then considered valuation, Pixar's creative independence and possible paths without the deal. An exchange ratio of 2.3 Disney shares per Pixar share was agreed in January 2006, with closing May 5. The 100%-owned Pixar subsidiary issued no cheque directly to Jobs: the transaction converted his 60,000,002 Pixar shares into 138,000,004 Disney shares, added to his three prior Disney shares, leaving **138,000,007 Disney shares, ~6.3%**, the largest **individual** shareholder block, not corporate control.

## Necessary factual distinctions

- Apple **was not party** to the deal, even though Jobs was Apple CEO.
- 2004 and June 2005: collaboration existed but future distribution terms disputed; other studio contacts remained preliminary.
- Jan 24, 2006 signed; May 5, 2006 completed, and May 8 reported shareholding.
- Steve's ~**50.6% Pixar ownership**, **40% agreed vote support**, and **6.3% Disney beneficial ownership** describe three different things.
- He joined Disney **board**, while Catmull and Lasseter assumed/received operational and creative leadership. No single “Jobs controls Disney” claim.
