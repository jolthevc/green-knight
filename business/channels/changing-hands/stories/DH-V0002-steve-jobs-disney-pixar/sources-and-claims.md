## Critical production guardrails

- S003 2006 proxy **Material Contracts pp85–87**, S002 2005 10-K: Disney and Pixar **co-owned covered films**; Disney had exclusive exploitation/distribution rights and often prevailed on specified derivative disagreements. Pixar did not receive direct theme park revenues from covered films, though derivative royalties/other rights have exceptions. This is not one party's blanket ownership of all characters across all uses.
- Disney was buying **future creators** through an equity exchange, not simply another license to already distributed Toy Story films. The future-value conclusion is a grounded strategic inference, not a statement of private motive.
- Jobs 60,000,002 Pixar + merger ratio 2.3 → 138,000,004 new Disney + 3 older Disney = 138,000,007, approx 6.3% of the enlarged company (S007). Narration rounds to 60m/138m; don't “correct” the precise document for the editorial script.
- Disney 2006 announcement $7.4bn **aggregate fully diluted consideration on then share price**, $6.3bn after Pixar cash, distinct from Jobs personally receiving a cash windfall.
- Jobs **owned ~50.6%** Pixar, pledged **40% of outstanding stock** to the merger vote, then became **~6.3% Disney** holder. Separate scopes.
- Jan 2004 **renewal talks ended** but existing covered film rights still active. Oct 12 2005 Iger proposed a combination; Jan 12 2006 ratio unresolved; Jan 21 handshake on proposed ratio; Jan 24 agreement announced; May 5 closed.
- No out-of-scope contemporary stock advice, unseen negotiations, invented private dialogue, finished film ratings or made-up personal sale proceeds.
