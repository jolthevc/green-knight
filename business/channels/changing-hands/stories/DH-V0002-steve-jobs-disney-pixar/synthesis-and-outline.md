## One-sentence narrative thesis

Disney could already exploit Pixar's existing hit films and much of their downstream life, but risked losing Pixar's *next* generation of characters when their distribution partnership ended. Buying the creative studio with Disney shares let Disney internalize that future, while majority Pixar owner Steve Jobs changed his position into a substantial yet non-controlling 6.3% of Disney.

## Changed-understanding promises

**At click:** How did the Apple CEO become Disney's largest individual holder? **First payoff:** He owned most of Pixar; Disney purchased Pixar with shares. **Second layer:** Disney already held old Pixar film exploitation rights and controlled certain sequels/theme park rights, so it was really bargaining for creators and future output. **Final payoff:** Jobs' majority in a stand-alone studio became a minority stake in the buyer's much larger business. Disney shareholders accepted dilution and acquired future creative output, with substantial execution risk.

## Alternative hooks, reconsidered

1. **Chosen:** the visible post-2006 result, Steve Jobs as Disney holder, with the noncash stock clue. Direct title payoff, earns rewind.
2. **Old-character rights paradox:** Disney already controlled parts of Nemo's commercial future but needed Pixar's next films. Strong second beat, not as broad-interest in first 10s.
3. **Share conversion first:** 60m Pixar becomes 138m Disney. Specific but too numerically demanding before company roles are clear.

## Informed skeptic lens

The old pictures were contractually **co-owned**, despite Disney exclusive exploitation. Scope sequel decisions as “certain” with limited exceptions and theme parks separately; no statement that Pixar had zero possible royalties from any derivative products. Late-2004 The Incredibles and 2006 Cars belong to the chronology: the 2004 breakdown concerns *future* distribution, not complete termination of film rights. Boards considered alternatives; there was no certain post-merger win. Jobs' 6.3% is a snapshot, not control, cash or future net wealth.
