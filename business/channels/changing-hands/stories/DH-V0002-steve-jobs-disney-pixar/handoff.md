## What the episode explains

Disney and Pixar shifted from a difficult independent distribution relationship to one company owning the other. Disney paid with **newly issued Disney shares** rather than giving Jobs a personal $7.4bn check; Jobs went from controlling >50% of Pixar to owning approximately 6.3% of Disney, with Disney purchasing the entire Pixar company. The negotiations involved not just price but Pixar's creative roles and future distribution.
