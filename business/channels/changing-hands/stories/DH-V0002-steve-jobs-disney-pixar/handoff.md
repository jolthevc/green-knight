## One-paragraph editorial promise

Disney already had strong distribution and exploitation rights for the beloved Pixar films, but Pixar's team could make the **next** hit film. As the existing deal approached its end, Jobs, Pixar's majority holder, faced uncertain distributor options. Bob Iger proposed an acquisition in 2005, with price and cultural leadership negotiated through January 2006. In May 2006 Disney acquired all Pixar by issuing Disney shares. Jobs ended up holding about 138 million Disney shares, **~6.3%**, and a Disney director seat. That is a meaningful stake, not cash payout, majority control or Disney CEO role.
