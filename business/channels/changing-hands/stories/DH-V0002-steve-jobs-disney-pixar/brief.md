## Thesis and viewer promise

Disney and Pixar had made blockbuster films together, but Disney already held exclusive distribution/exploitation rights and much of the decision-making influence over derivative projects based on their existing films. Pixar, whose majority holder was Steve Jobs, could seek another distributor for future movies. Disney's 2006 acquisition bought the actual **future animation studio and talent** by issuing Disney shares to Pixar shareholders. Jobs surrendered a majority position in independent Pixar for a minority stake of about 6.3% in the much larger Disney, becoming its largest individual shareholder.

**Audience entry:** Toy Story, Nemo, Pixar/Disney, Steve Jobs. **Surprise:** Disney had substantial film rights *before* it bought Pixar. **Changed understanding:** a buyer can buy a company with newly issued ownership in itself, while the target owner becomes a significant holder of the purchaser rather than receiving cash. **Follow-up:** how did a broken distribution negotiation turn into a $7.4bn announced all-stock merger, and what did each side surrender?

## Before and after, with careful denominators

Before: Jobs ~50.6% of Pixar; Disney/Pixar had co-production rights but separate sets of shareholders; Disney distributed and exploited covered films and could generally prevail in derivative-works disputes, while Pixar did not receive theme-park revenue. Negotiated price is not the value of a personal cash payout.

After May 5 2006 closing: Disney owns 100% Pixar. All Pixar holders receive 2.3 Disney shares for each Pixar share. Jobs held **60,000,002** Pixar shares which became **138,000,004** new Disney shares; plus **three** pre-existing Disney shares, reported **138,000,007**, approx **6.3%** Disney. Jobs pledged shares representing **40% of Pixar outstanding** in support of the merger (a different percentage from his ownership). His Disney board seat was governance influence, not sole management authority. Keep precise numbers in sources, not spoken when they impede clarity.
