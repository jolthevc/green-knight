## Build instructions and provenance

Use named club tiles rather than logo-dependent historical footage. In 1976 use New York Nets, Denver Nuggets, Indiana Pacers and San Antonio Spurs. Do not put Brooklyn branding in 1976. No photorealistic fake checks, documents, signatures, archival scenes or invented headlines.

Document inserts must be actual captured source text with publisher, date and source ID; preserve enough context to distinguish allegations and proposals. Prefer paraphrase labels over lengthy copied excerpts. The court's three-word media term and the 11-word Ozzie quotation are the only necessary direct textual quotations. Screenshots are research references, not pre-cleared licensed assets. No source images or clips have been downloaded or licensed for production.

The illustrative $7m allocations must carry an enduring "Illustrative example" label. Four $1m transfers accumulate to $4m; no historical date. For historical receipts, show $1.35m / 1987-88 and reported $17.5m / 2010-11 as separate cards, with "US dollars, nominal; receipts" and source labels. No interpolation between two observations, no net-profit or portfolio-return graphic.

For the denominator cap, show a shared pot, then added chairs, with a small "Calculation counts at most 28" badge. Do not imply the NBA stopped expanding or every team always received equal amounts. At the ownership reveal, retain the Spirits entity and its right; change the ownership bracket. Show equity investment and borrowing as separate funding arrows. Label "Announced January 2014 structure; full terms not public." No invented equity percentage, lender, rate or debt amount.

Test actual phone-size frames rather than treating those sizes as a guarantee. Use shape and text as well as color, keep lower caption area clear, and reveal no more than one new relation at a time.
