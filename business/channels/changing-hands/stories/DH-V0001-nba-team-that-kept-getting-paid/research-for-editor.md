# Research references for the editor: DH-V0001

**The Team That Made Millions Without Playing in the NBA**

Script v2, SHA-256 `c95ad571754a1802b377e509e678cc6cbee9999667b3d6b7f25634b2a5326e73`. Research as of 2026-10-10. Factual support, not visual direction.

## Essential context
- Spirits played in the ABA, not NBA; operations ceased in 1976.
- Four ABA clubs entered the NBA: Nuggets, Pacers, Spurs and Nets. Nets were New York in 1976, not Brooklyn.
- Basic participation: one-seventh of each of four qualifying television allocations, not one-seventh of all NBA revenue.
- The seven-million-dollar arithmetic example is invented, not historical receipts.
- Receipts are not personal net profit. The income recipient was a partnership; total after-cost returns are not established.
- The arrangement limited dilution beyond a calculation count of 28 teams. It did not stop expansion or guarantee a fixed annual dollar payment.
- Additional media income was disputed. Forum rulings did not decide that every claimed payment was owed.
- The $500 million settlement is attributed reporting. The NBA announcement described a conditional framework, not disclosed final financing terms.
- That framework contemplated the four teams controlling the recipient's common equity, existing owners retaining the remainder, borrowing and distribution. Do not suggest every right was simply cancelled.
- Current ownership/payments, exact debt terms and best-ever returns are not asserted.
