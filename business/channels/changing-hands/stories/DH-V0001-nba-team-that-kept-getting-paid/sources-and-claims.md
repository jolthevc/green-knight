## Critical guardrails

The 2010-11 $17.5 million payment is contemporary reporting and relates to the partnership, not verified personal after-cost profit. A court recorded about $1.35 million for 1987-88. The $7 million four-club calculation is invented for teaching. The contract's 28-team floor limits dilution of the percentage, not changes in absolute dollars. New-media underpayment is an allegation; state courts decided forum, not ultimate merits. The $500 million settlement is reported but its exact allocation is not publicly documented here. NBA's January 2014 proposal conditionally included controlling equity for the four clubs, new borrowing and distributions, with no complete closing terms opened.
