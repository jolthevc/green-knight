## Adversarial review

The strongest objection is that the popular story confuses gross receipts, personal profit and an optimal investment outcome. P015 and P032 explicitly reject those leaps. The reported $500m is attributed, while P024-P028 explain only the NBA's announced financing and control framework. We did not inspect executed loan or closing documents, and do not present debt proceeds as newly created wealth.

The courts establish payment arrangements and procedural history. They do not establish that every alleged additional media payment was owed. P020-P021 preserve that distinction. The original signed 1976 contract was not obtained; the fraction and 28-team protection rely on identified reporting and reproduced complaint language. S009 is partial and never the sole evidence for a consequential fact.

Conflicting introductory purchase/settlement amounts, early-payment chronology, cumulative returns and venue-capacity claims were omitted. The $7m example is invented and labeled in both speech and visuals. Historical dollar markers are nominal US receipts with distinct source types, not an audited return series. No fabricated dialogue or reconstructed private negotiations.
