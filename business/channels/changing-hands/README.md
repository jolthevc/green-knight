# Changing Hands

**Working name:** Changing Hands. Earlier concept labels: The Trade / The Deal. A future rename must preserve the story IDs and ledger history.

**Promise:** Understand the bet or transaction, the money at stake, and the mechanism that decided the outcome.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrated financial stories for curious adults. Legendary trades, squeezes, takeovers, buyouts and consequential market contests, explained through people, positions, constraints and turning points.
