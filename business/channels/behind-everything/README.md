# Behind Everything

**The world's most familiar things have extraordinary stories.**

Behind Everything is Green Knight's broad-audience, entertainment-first documentary channel. It turns things ordinary people already care about into gripping, factual stories about hidden incentives, design choices, businesses, money, culture and systems. **Entertainment is the invitation; the surprising explanation is the payoff.**

**Click promise:** "I know that thing. Wait, why does it work like THAT?"
**Watch promise:** Real predicaments, escalation, counterintuitive discoveries and a genuine payoff. No encyclopedia recap, corporate profile for its own sake, sterile 'explainer,' false mystery or AI-slideshow content.
**Share promise:** "You won't believe why [familiar thing] works the way it does." The insight must actually be sourced.
**Runtime:** Target 9 minutes; 8 to 10 finished minutes, never padded. Four episodes per month is the *planned* experimental cadence, not proof of commissioning.
**Budget premise:** Approximately $60 per outsourced edit, to be confirmed per editor and scope. Maintain an achievable documentary visual language.
**Status:** Editorial infrastructure and unresearched, proposed slate only. No narrator, designer, editor contract, YouTube account, commissioned episode or publishing action is implied. Name/handle/trademark clearance not verified.

## Three channels, distinct viewer motivations
- **Internet Famous**: the culture and mechanics of online attention; people, memes, platforms and virality.
- **Fame & Fortune**: celebrity-specific fortune, deals, ownership and commercial power.
- **Behind Everything**: familiar experiences, services, brands and systems, seen through a surprising real-world contradiction. Not restricted to celebrity, tech, failures or finance.
- Other dormant business concepts remain valid in their own lanes; Behind Everything is not an excuse to replicate their existing scripts or proposals.

## No production without selection
The 30 concepts are proposed pitches, not researched facts or user-selected scripts. The owner approves the topic and full final script/voice before production. The final editor-facing packet has exactly three approved files: 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md, 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md. Deliverable expectations remain final MP4, thumbnail, narration audio, SEO/chapters package and SRT.

**Standard:** "Never boring" is a requirement to reject or rewrite work, not permission to make inaccurate claims, invent tension or sensationalize people.
