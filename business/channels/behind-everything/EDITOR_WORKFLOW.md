# $60-per-video production workflow and approvals

**Working model:** AI-assisted internally reviewed scripts, outsourced human editor, proposed 4/month. $60/edit is the owner's *planning figure*, not a verified contract. Quote footage, voice, revisions, thumbnail, source files and music separately where necessary; avoid hiding costs in a fake low price.

## Before commissioning
1. Select a qualified topic and source-verifiable package from the shared registry.
2. Create full research/source log and exact title/thumbnail Promise Lock.
3. Draft complete clean script and full narration/visual directions.
4. Run first-30s retention audit, weakest-middle audit, cold-read and specific sourced payoff check.
5. Present full script and actual narrator sample for owner approval. No paid/full production implied until approved.

## Send exactly THREE approved editor instructions
- `01_SCRIPT.txt`: exact clean narration with TITLE metadata line not voiced.
- `02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md`: exact spoken copy plus clearly non-spoken voice performance guidance and segment/paragraph anchors.
- `03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md`: chosen package, first-frame/first-30s storyboard, scene map, archival/source directions, economical graphics, visual proof, rights and music guidance.

## Video pilot and acceptance
Request a real integrated **60-90 second** cut first if the editor relationship is unproven. Review opening, voice naturalness, title/thumbnail match, on-screen text spelling, factual graphics, music pacing, actual asset provenance, cuts and narrative clarity. Approval of pilot is distinct from approval of final.

## Five expected final deliverables
1. Finished 16:9 MP4, target 1080p or higher.
2. Custom title-matched thumbnail (phone-readable).
3. Clean narration audio file.
4. SEO package: approved title, description, relevant tags and true finalized chapter timestamps.
5. Synced SRT subtitles.

Editable project/source and media licenses are separate terms unless contracted. Provide consolidated revisions and distinguish must-fix misstatements from aesthetic preferences. Compare the exported movie to source-backed approved script, not merely screenshots.

## Common failure points
- Full footage is literal image after image, no idea progression.
- Visual text errors or unverified graphics.
- Artificial synthetic performance.
- Intro takes 20-30 seconds to reach the actual premise.
- Midfilm becomes a business lecture.
- Overcomplicated animations consume budget and reduce polish.
- Final scene doesn't resolve title.
- Footage/audio rights are undocumented.
- Editor silently changes approved wording or adds unsupported claims.

Do not instruct an external editor to begin work, transfer payment or upload videos without express owner authorization.
