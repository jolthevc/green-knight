# Research basis, platform guidance and learning loop
Checked against official YouTube resources on 2026-10-19. These are guidance, not proprietary 'algorithm rules', and our creative choices are working hypotheses.

## Official guidance
- [Understand recommendation-system performance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en): titles/thumbnails communicate a promise; the first seconds must deliver on expectations; target real satisfaction, not simply headline CTR.
- [Key moments for audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en): first-30-second retention, dips, flat stretches and replay spikes need context. A replay spike may signal value OR confusion.
- [Video content analytics tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?co=YOUTUBE): compare intros against videos of similar length; refine title/thumbnail match and the actual opening.
- [Thumbnail Test & Compare](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714?hl=en): where eligible, Studio permits tests of up to three thumbnails. Do not report an A/B win that hasn't happened; check current access.
- [Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en): mass-produced/repetitive, interchangeable content is ineligible. Distinct original creative substance matters even when using AI.
- [TeamYouTube clarification on AI use and inauthentic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/356734251?hl=en&msgid=357300241): AI-assisted storytelling is not categorically disallowed; true originality and relevant disclosure are still required.
- [Altered/synthetic content disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en): recheck the actual narration, music, real-looking imagery and context at upload.

## Our hypotheses to test
Package clarity, early earned reveal, compelling character/object journey, meaningful midfilm turn and short satisfying closer may improve the viewer experience. 'Every 30 seconds a twist', a universal CTR benchmark, a specific cuts-per-minute rate, guaranteed RPM or a guaranteed viral title are NOT established YouTube rules.

## Measure after uploads exist
Capture source-specific impressions and CTR; first-30-second retention; dips with timestamps; average view duration and average percentage viewed; new and returning viewers; audience source; suggested follow-on viewing; views from older uploads; cost and script/editor revision burden. Compare by video length and traffic context, not crude global CTR. Keep a 7-day and 28-day postmortem with a specific *change to test next*, without reverse-engineering one noisy result into an invented rule.

## Research standards
Every documentary needs primary reporting when obtainable, credible independent corroboration, date/units/scope on facts, opposing explanations, and rights-aware visual evidence. Current-market or brand claims must be verified near scripting and release. Pitches in PILOT_SLATE are unverified questions, NOT preapproved assertions.
