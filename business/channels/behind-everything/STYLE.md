# Behind Everything: accessible premium visual identity

## Look
A visually satisfying documentary about familiar everyday life. Real object and place photography, truthful reporting screenshots, product footage, simple motion graphics, clear maps and selective real data. A restrained visual design with bold, highly legible typography and a consistent palette; final brand colors/fonts require human taste approval, not a guess.

**Creative reference territory:** mainstream curiosity documentaries, the clear visual reasoning of the best business explainers, and cinematic editorial storytelling. Borrow craft, not branding or others' proprietary footage/graphics.

## Design tests
- Could a viewer understand the frame in one second on a small phone?
- Does this image ADVANCE the story rather than decorate narration?
- Is the on-screen amount, chart, logo, quote or headline sourced, legible and correct?
- If the voice stopped, would the visual communicate a part of the actual causal process?
- Could an editor execute it within the agreed $60 scope without resorting to fake, typo-filled images?

## Visual toolkit ordered by preference
1. Original simple compositions from licensed/public-domain/permission-cleared real photography or footage.
2. Screenshots of verified source materials with context, attribution and rights assessment.
3. Clean object tracking, prices, timeline steps, maps, flow diagrams, before/after layouts, restrained chart transitions.
4. Custom schematic illustrations when essential and inexpensive.
5. AI-assisted illustrative images only with review, appropriate disclosure, and labels where needed, never as fake documentary evidence.

Avoid a slideshow of literal images, random stock b-roll, fake articles/posts, AI hands/logos/text, blurry collage, frenetic motion masking no narrative progress, neon business aesthetics and text walls. Editors must manually proof ALL on-screen text.

## Rhythm
Cold open should have one striking unmistakable object, not five irrelevant cuts. Visual changes coincide with story revelations. Faster at genuine pivots, quieter at hard decisions or surprising human costs. Simple typography and chart reveals beat elaborate low-quality character animation.

## Thumbnail system
One familiar anchor, one contradiction or consequence, high separation, minimal words, no imitation of official logos/brands or false imagery. Prepare three *materially different* concepts before selecting one and inspect at mobile size. Rights for thumbnail photos/logos are evaluated separately.

## Rights
Asset sources and permission/licensing status should be recorded by the producer; accessible online does not mean reusable. Respect copyright, privacy and the context of third-party clips. Never present synthetic reconstruction as archival proof.
