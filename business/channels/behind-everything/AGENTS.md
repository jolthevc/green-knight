# AI instructions for Behind Everything

This folder is governed by business/AGENTS.md, business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md, business/ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md and the channel-specific contracts here. Fetch *live* repository content and registry at the start of each project. Do not rely on stale memory for approvals or story status.

## Operating order
1. Read README, CHANNEL, CREATIVE_CONTRACT, TOPIC_GREENLIGHT, CLICK_AND_HOOK_STANDARD and all relevant shared standards.
2. Read the **entire shared business/ideas/registry.json** for the same subject, cause/mechanism, and viewer promise across every channel. Different title is not new subject.
3. Generate and score a broad idea pool. Proposed ideas remain proposed until owner selection. Never silently select or commission.
4. Before research spending, write three materially distinct package hypotheses, a one-line plain-English paradox, a first-frame idea, two opening approaches and a specific payoff to prove.
5. Research primary evidence and serious alternative explanations; rebuild the angle when facts do not support the exciting version.
6. Write a dramatic beat map with what changes in viewer belief at every movement, a substantial first payoff, a midfilm surprise/reversal when supported, and an earned closer. The weakest middle is a required edit target.
7. Draft COMPLETE spoken narration; audio-first, 8-10 minutes measured with the intended voice, no mandatory filler. No invented scenes or data.
8. Independently review packaging, spoken script, actual visual plan, research/evidence, narrator direction and cross-file alignment. If boring, misleading or derivative, RETURN TO REVISE.
9. Owner approves full final script and selected narration voice. Only then release exact three-file editor packet and request 60-90s integrated pilot sample before full production approval.
10. Verify actual deliverables and rights. Publication and analytics require actual evidence, not a draft or producer message. Update ledger and statuses together, do not invent completed stages.

## Nonnegotiable editor objectives
Recognizable subject; legible thumbnail; opening that fulfills the click; distinct story escalation; a genuine surprising insight by the first substantial movement; strongest evidence through pictures; no pointless motion or generic graphics; close with a completed promise and one next-watch invitation after payoff. Clear, human, clean narration and dynamic but economical visual treatment.

## Integrity / cross-channel / budget
Truthful but emotionally vivid, sharply skeptical without defamation. Do not claim secret knowledge when public facts suffice. Never reproduce copyrighted media without considering rights. Do not invent views, CTR, retention, published videos, editor contracts or legal clearance. $60/video is a budget assumption, not license to ship low-quality filler.

Do not modify the legacy channels/, Sheets, Drive or n8n without explicit scope. This folder uses the *manual* business workspace. Use BE-I#### and BE-V####. The name is owner-chosen but availability/clearance is unverified.

## Handoff
Report exact files changed, current idea IDs and status, the branch/commit, what was validated, and what still requires actual research, script approval, voice choice and producer action.
