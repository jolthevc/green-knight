# Creative contract: earning the click and every next minute

**Priority:** Maximum *truthful* audience attractiveness. Accuracy is a hard floor; entertainment and real insight must both clear the bar.

## The package-story-payoff contract
The chosen topic, title, thumbnail, first frame, first 30 seconds, story engine and closing sentence must be expressions of the *same* precise viewer promise. If one is incongruent, fix the package/story before production.

We do not write 'Why [industry] matters.' We write 'You think this familiar thing works one way. Here is the strange real decision or incentive that changes that assumption.'

## Dramatic engines to favor
- An apparent contradiction with one concrete real-world example.
- A customer's simple goal colliding with a business's opposing incentive.
- A clever solution that creates a new, unexpected problem.
- A second-order consequence hiding inside a familiar process.
- A famously ordinary object tracing an extraordinary route.
- A business constrained by its own success.
- A consumer experience whose observed design only makes sense when its constraints are revealed.

## Movement standard
A beat earns its place when it (1) changes the viewer's working explanation, (2) introduces consequential evidence/action, (3) raises/reframes stakes, or (4) creates earned emotional contrast. A transition merely repeating 'but there's more' fails. The weakest middle must be named and repaired with actual changes in evidence, scene, order or length.

## The two-viewer test
Newcomer: I immediately understand the object, question and stakes. Expert: I discover an earned, specific insight or well-supported synthesis beyond generic journalism and common lore. Neither should need a finance degree.

## Nonnegotiable rejection signals
- Title could describe 200 unrelated videos.
- Thumbnail needs 10 words or microscopic logos.
- Opening spends 30 seconds explaining the premise rather than showing the paradox.
- Script uses a list of 'three reasons' when a real causal story exists.
- Main revelation is fully exhausted before the middle and nothing deepens it.
- Consequence, number, intent, behavioral effect or causal inference is unsupported.
- The editor needs a costly animation production to make a fundamentally boring premise work.
- Last minute offers a vague life lesson instead of resolving the exact click promise.

## Working philosophy
Aim for the story quality of premium documentary entertainment and the interpretive precision of a rigorous investigator. Our goal is repeatable viewer satisfaction, not 'algorithm hacking.'
