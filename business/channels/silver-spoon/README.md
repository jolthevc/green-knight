# Silver Spoon

**Promise:** Understand how a family fortune is built, passed on, controlled, protected or lost.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrated business/wealth stories for curious adults. Dynasties, succession, inheritance, ownership structures and family contests, explained through assets, incentives and turning points.
