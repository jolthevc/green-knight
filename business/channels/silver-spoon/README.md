# Silver Spoon

**Promise:** Understand how a family fortune is built, passed on, controlled, protected or lost.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrated business/wealth stories for curious adults. Dynasties, succession, inheritance, ownership structures and family contests, explained through assets, incentives and turning points.

## Working decisions

Name and 8–10 minute runtime are set. The refined illustrated dynasty style below is an implementation proposal; earlier images are not assumed to be accessible or a finalized brand kit. Exact palette, typography and production details require pilot review.

Voice, price, cadence and finished visual sample remain open. The earlier Hermès family topic is imported as proposed and unresearched. No episode is selected or completed. Additional ideas should be generated through the shared format and duplicate checks.

## Current production and delivery rules
An episode-creation request develops the research, script, full **working narration director**, and **working visual director** together. Review/revise checks all three and integration, releasing only the three final editor-packet files after approval. The selected narrator must be genuinely approved, not inferred from ElevenLabs prompt drafts. Completed video QA follows [network Delivery QA](../../DELIVERY_QA_STANDARD.md): MP4, thumbnail, clean narration, SEO title/description/tags/verified chapters, and synchronized SRT. Read [Operations Quickstart](../../OPERATIONS_QUICKSTART.md). The editor executes our visual narrative with reasonable production freedom.
