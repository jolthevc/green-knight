# Silver Spoon: creative contract
The entertainment is **family drama told through the rights to a fortune**. Affection, ambition, loyalty, status, succession and loss of control become concrete in ownership decisions.

**Universal hook:** A famous brand, recognizable family, surprising transfer or loss. Start from the irony that having the family name may not mean controlling the business.

**Dramatic motor:** Fortune created -> who actually controls it -> transfer or rivalry -> changed incentives -> point of no return -> which members kept wealth/control and which did not. Keep a small cast with relationships introduced only as necessary.

**Signature payoff:** An emotionally resonant, sourced statement about the difference between a family identity, its money and its control over the underlying business.

**Creative red flags:** long genealogy, luxury montage instead of economics, gossip masquerading as evidence, false 'family went broke' headlines, ownership percentages without consequences, invented family motives.

**Distinct from others:** Multigenerational control and relationships determine the story, not the technical mechanics of a takeover (Changing Hands) or an operating turnaround (Money Moves/Fallen Angels).

**Pilot test:** Could the audience draw a basic three-party rights map after one listen? Are the people understandable and consequential without a wall of surnames? Is the ending about changed family power rather than merely stock price?

**Strong reference:** Gucci's family name endured after family ownership disappeared. Preserve the distinction and tighten the family/map and investor sequence around emotionally legible choices.

## Current director-led creative production rule (all eight channels)

Green Knight now owns the title/thumbnail promise, first frame, detailed first 20–35 seconds, narration dynamics, story movements and breathing points, visual sequence design with recurring environments and transformations, selective evidence/text, and ending callback. The editor executes the work, rather than independently choosing the story's visual conception. Read business/EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md and business/DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW.md; these supersede any earlier channel-specific editor-owned guidance.

Per story, release only the three fully reviewed final files in `editor-packet/`: `01_SCRIPT.txt`, `02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md`, `03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md`. Script, performance, visual plan and their integration MUST all pass independent and joined QA after all revisions. No formerly scripted episode automatically qualifies for packet approval. Preserve this channel's distinctive audience promise and visual identity; do not force identical transitions or formulaic “Chapter 1” cards.

## Required popcorn-good channel contract (2026-10-11)
Read business/POPCORN_STANDARD.md. **Earned engagement is the first creative test, with truthful evidence as the minimum floor.** A first-timer follows understandable human stakes in a family wealth/control story without an impenetrable cast or genealogy lesson. An expert learns an overlooked ownership, voting, trust, succession, legal or governance mechanism that materially explains the outcome. **BAD:** rich-family gossip and net-worth lists. **GOOD:** the viewer watches control shift despite apparently unchanged economic ownership and comes away with a surprising, warranted strategic understanding. Avoid invented motives or family dialogue.

Run TWO explicit tests on every new channel concept, idea and scripted episode: **newcomer pull** (immediate human stakes, first-listen comprehension and real desire to continue) and **already-expert reward** (a specific warranted new lens or insightful causal model). Name the weakest likely middle sequence, create authentic reversals, and show how the best strategic insight *unfolds inside the plot*. A correct but predictable lecture, a generic takeaway or beautiful prose without audience momentum is REVISE/REFRAME, never 'ready.' Never invent twists or inflate unsourced certainty. On review/revise, record both verdicts and actual paragraph/beat fixes rather than praising general quality.
