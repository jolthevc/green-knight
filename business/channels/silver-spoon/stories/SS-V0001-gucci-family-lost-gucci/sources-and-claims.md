## Adversarial editorial decisions

- **First purchase:** 47.7% intermediary stake in April 1988 and later completed 50% first block are differently dated descriptions, not conflicting claims of one exact closing.
- **Identity timing:** Morgan Stanley's April announcement describes an *unidentified client* (S010). The buyer Investcorp was openly identified by June (S011). Never silently imply the client was publicly known in April.
- **Board custody:** Reuters/LA Times reports four of eight board seats and blocked fifth seat (S011). Do not present this as unanimous official board-minute proof or as a successful takeover.
- **Entity separation:** U.S. court's ownership statement specifically concerns Gucci Shops (S003), whereas group SEC chronology states broad acquisition of Gucci (S002).
- **Private motives:** Family spokespeople's claims and investor/management allegations remain attributed views.
- **Chronology:** 1993 exit predates 1995 murder; 1994 recovery predates De Sole's formal July 1995 CEO designation; founding date contested.
- **Finance:** Historical sale consideration is a reported estimate, not personal net worth. Gucci's >$1bn 1998 **sales** are not net profit or Investcorp's return.
- **Final title:** “lost Gucci” is idiomatic for sold-through ownership, not confiscated surname.
