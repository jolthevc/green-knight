# Fame & Fortune: repeatable low-cost production toolkit

## Reusable visual modules
1. **The face + money tension:** recognizable licensed star portrait, one documented deal object, short factual overlay.
2. **The paper reveal:** authentic article or filing crop with readable publisher, date, highlighted exact supporting words. Never fabricate a citation.
3. **The ownership split:** person, holding company, operating company, licensee/distributor, investors. Animate equity versus contractual payments accurately.
4. **Before/after leverage:** endorsements on left; equity or IP ownership on right; or sold business versus retained rights.
5. **The partnership:** two authenticated photographs/logos with what each side supplied; no fictional meeting or invented conversation.
6. **The bet:** product shelf / tour / platform, marketing spend / capital at risk, risk and reward symbols backed by reporting.
7. **The twist:** return to prior photo/headline, reveal missing qualifier or alternate evidence, then revise visual numbers.
8. **The closing callback:** same first-frame object, now with accurate, more interesting commercial meaning.

## Shot direction required for each A-H segment
`SEGMENT | paragraph range | visual start -> factual change -> visual end | on-screen exact text | required verified sources | voice/music/hold | must not imply | optional cheaper substitute`.

Target is a **limited modular set of 6-10 substantive visual sequences**, not hundreds of short stock cuts. Mix photo/document motion with simple explanatory animations. Source every real-money figure and quote. Clear rights for personality images, paparazzi, TV/music clips, interviews, brand footage and song excerpts. Do not assume a publicly viewable image can be commercially reused.

## First 45 seconds
Choose one decisive thumbnail composition first. Match it with the first frame and one verified, legible fact. Drive from photo -> headline/filing -> financial twist. Every shot must reinforce the specific question the title sold.

## Editor freedom
MUST: accurate assets, legal rights, factual hierarchy, exact promised reveal and segment mapping.
SHOULD: create recurring motifs and deliberate changes in composition.
OPTIONAL: software, paper-tear transition, parallax implementation, exact zoom style.
