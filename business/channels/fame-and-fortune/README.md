# Fame & Fortune

**The money stories behind the most famous people on the planet.** Fame & Fortune is Green Knight's twelfth manual business-documentary channel.

**Audience promise:** You know the face. You think you know the fortune. Here is the unexpected deal, fight, ownership structure, risk, or strategic bet that actually explains how fame became money.

**Primary goal:** Broad-audience business entertainment. Celebrity recognition earns the click; an exceptionally juicy, genuinely documented commercial mystery earns the watch. Think a sharply written premium mini-documentary, not a celebrity biography, gossip slideshow, wealth-tour video, or MBA lecture.

**Runtime:** 10 to 20 minutes (600 to 1,200 seconds), with **15 minutes / 900 seconds** the normal creative target. Longer runtime is intentionally specific to this channel, not a change to other Green Knight channels.

## Workflow and rules
1. Read [CLICK_AND_HOOK_STANDARD.md](CLICK_AND_HOOK_STANDARD.md), [CREATIVE_CONTRACT.md](CREATIVE_CONTRACT.md), and [CHANNEL.md](CHANNEL.md) for story and packaging.
2. Read [SCRIPT_STANDARD.md](SCRIPT_STANDARD.md) and [SCRIPT_SEGMENT_TEMPLATE.md](SCRIPT_SEGMENT_TEMPLATE.md) for narration architecture, paragraph IDs, segment boundaries, editor handoff, and timing.
3. Read [STYLE.md](STYLE.md), [VISUAL_SYSTEM.md](VISUAL_SYSTEM.md), and [VOICE.md](VOICE.md). The default editing language is low-cost, premium newspaper/documentary: authentic photographs and reporting, licensed press and product imagery, filings and actual documents, simple money/ownership graphics, restrained motion.
4. Read [EDITOR_WORKFLOW.md](EDITOR_WORKFLOW.md) before briefing a Fiverr producer and [START_A_CHAT.md](START_A_CHAT.md) to operate in a fresh chat.
5. Browse [PILOT_SLATE.md](PILOT_SLATE.md); proposed FF-I#### ideas are stored in the common [registry](../../ideas/registry.json), not yet selected or researched.

**Outputs to an execution editor:** Exactly three files once fully sourced and owner-approved: 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md and 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md. The first is clean spoken narration. The second and third carry unspoken named segments, paragraph ranges, approximate timestamps, dramatic objectives, performance notes, music, visual actions and transitions. An internal segmented read-through may assist drafting but is NOT a fourth editor-facing file.

**Status:** Channel documentation and unverified proposed ideas only. No public channel, verified name clearance, editor contract, appointed narrator, approved pilot, approved script, sourced claims or publication is implied.

Fame & Fortune differs from Silver Spoon (generational ownership), Money Moves (corporate strategic decisions as protagonist), Fool's Gold (deception), and Fallen Angels (company collapse). The star and the exact fame-to-financial-power mechanism must be the central engine. Read Green Knight [POPCORN_STANDARD](../../POPCORN_STANDARD.md), [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), [EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD](../../EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md) and [DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW](../../DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW.md).
