# Fame & Fortune: editorial look

**Proposed, not yet owner-approved.** Hollywood/celebrity recognition with an intelligent newspaper-and-money documentary finish, optimized for low edit cost and excellent story pacing. Reference emotional ambition: premium investigative mini-doc, not tabloid montage.

## The visual language
- **Foundation:** authentic celebrity photographs, press red carpets and event images when licensed; brand/product shots; ownership diagrams; public filings; real article screenshots with publisher/date/context; interviews only where usage rights allow; general-purpose stock.
- **Palette:** bone/cream paper, editorial black/charcoal, muted gold and restrained burgundy accent. Exact brand kit requires pilot approval.
- **Type:** readable bold editorial headline font plus clean financial number typography. Make key information readable on a phone.
- **Paper/doc effects:** headline reveals, document highlights, understated stamp/underline, page crop, quote pull-outs if sourced, simple cover-line changes.
- **Motion:** slow layered pan/zoom, photo-to-document shift, foreground cutout, clean wipe, flow-arrow/ownership stake reveal, single well-built value waterfall. Reuse templates rather than animated every-sentence objects.
- **Audio:** cinematic but restrained, with real silence at the biggest truth turns. No artificial constant dramatic stings.
- **Avoid:** unsourced AI celebrity faces, AI-generated counterfeit headlines, fake boardroom/confession footage, unrelated luxury stock, generic yachts/ferraris as filler, source-free "EXPOSED" cards, or full-time karaoke subtitles.
- **Filmable economics:** contrast endorsement fee versus stake/royalties, who controls rights, marketing reach versus durable demand, gross sale price versus actual personal proceeds.

Maintain a recognizable star/brand image at every major movement, but use documents/graphics to expose the money. Follow the director-led three-file handoff and licensing requirements.
