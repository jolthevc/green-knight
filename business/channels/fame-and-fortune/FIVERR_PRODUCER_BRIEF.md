# Fame & Fortune: producer/editor brief

## What this channel is
**Fame & Fortune** makes dramatically entertaining, faceless YouTube documentaries about how famous entertainers, athletes and creators actually turn attention into businesses, control, power and wealth. A celebrity fan should click for the face and stay for a remarkable, high-stakes true business story.

Think "the deal nobody explained" rather than biographies, celebrity gossip, generic celebrity net worth, or finance lectures. The reference ambition is a Netflix-worthy money story made with clean, inexpensive documentary editing.

## Non-negotiable priorities (in order)
1. **An explosive yet truthful click:** immediately recognizable star, provocative specific title, legible high-contrast thumbnail, real financial mystery. Use commercial stakes, contracts, ownership, rivals or extraordinary reversals, not fake scandals or lies.
2. **First 15 seconds:** no greetings, no channel intro. Start in the middle of the real drama. Give the audience a question it *needs* answered.
3. **First 30-45 seconds:** deliver one concrete verified reveal or partial answer, then open the larger mystery. Do not postpone every payoff.
4. **Keep building intrigue:** every sequence should change what we think we know. Avoid biographical filler and lists of unrelated acquisitions.
5. **A genuinely satisfying ending:** answer the thumbnail/title question, explain who truly earned/owned/controlled what, then close on a memorable insight.

**Style:** dramatic and salacious in real, sourced business stakes, but not defamatory, dishonest, artificially scandalous or overacted.

## Length and narration
**Target about 15 minutes. Acceptable final runtime 10 to 20 minutes.** The normal 8-10 minute Green Knight package DOES NOT apply. Narration should be natural, compelling, premium, conversational, curious and confidently dramatic. Slow down for facts and numbers, allow silence around major revelations. The narrator profile will be selected and approved; do not assume voice ID/model.

## The eight film segments
These are **non-spoken section names** and flexible estimated time targets:

| Label | Target | Narrative job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: COLD OPEN / HOOK | 0:00-0:35 | Shockingly interesting documented question and stakes |
| B: PROMISE / SETUP | 0:35-1:30 | Give first answer, ask bigger question |
| C: ACT I / PUBLIC IMAGE | 1:30-3:20 | Celebrity leverage, minimum essential context |
| D: ACT II / THE DEAL | 3:20-6:00 | The transaction, gamble, constraints and choice |
| E: ACT III / FOLLOW THE MONEY | 6:00-9:00 | Who owns what; cash versus equity versus royalties |
| F: ACT IV / THE CATCH | 9:00-12:00 | A real complication, risk, friction or reversal |
| G: ACT V / WHAT HAPPENED | 12:00-14:15 | Documented outcomes, nuance, source-grounded resolution |
| H: CLOSER / PAYOFF | 14:15-15:00 | Resolve opening and land unforgettable final line |

Timecodes are approximate. We value a captivating 13-minute film more than a padded 15-minute film.

## Editing style: designed for repeatability
Celebrity and brand photographs, licensed product/stock footage, real newspaper clippings and article screenshots, actual deal/filing documents, simple dated finance/ownership charts, paper cutouts, subtle zooms, sound design and selective text overlays. Keep production polished but light: no expensive animation requirement, no parade of random stills, no fake AI-generated celebrity photos or counterfeit archival evidence.

**Every segment should tell the editor and narrator** the exact P paragraph range, approximate in/out, what the audience believes, what changes in the scene, what fact is revealed, how delivery should feel, music/pause/hold guidance, and the visual start/change/end. Section headers are NOT narration.

## Approval and files
Topics and full scripts require owner approval before recording/editing. Final production instructions comprise **exactly three** approved files for each episode:
- **01_SCRIPT.txt**: canonical exact spoken narration. The initial TITLE header is not read aloud.
- **02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md**: section-by-section speaker directions plus full unchanged spoken text, clearly distinguished from NON-SPOKEN performance directions.
- **03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md**: chosen title/thumbnail, first-frame and first-45s choreography, precise segment visual direction, asset sourcing and sound/hold transitions.

Only feed spoken words into voice-generation software. If commissioned to research/write a draft, submit the full draft with supporting source links **before** recording/editing. Final approved packet comes later.

## Final delivery scope to confirm before ordering
Final MP4 at 1080p or better, thumbnail, separate narration audio, SEO set (final title, description, tags and actual chapter timestamps), and SRT subtitles. Agree pilot/10-episode pricing for **15-minute average length**, number of revisions, rights clearance, licensing and reuse of assets.

Start with a 60-90-second integrated opening test before a recurring package, unless a pilot has already been reviewed and approved. No editor engagement, rate, voice choice, episode selection or video production is presumed by this brief.
