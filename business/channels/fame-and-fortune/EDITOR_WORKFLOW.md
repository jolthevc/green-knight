# Fame & Fortune: Fiverr editor and producer workflow

## Engagement model
Default: Green Knight researches/writes/approves the script and the visual/narration director files; Fiverr editor executes the film, thumbnails and final deliverables. If the editor is explicitly commissioned to write first drafts, get **full script and verifiable primary-source leads for owner review first**. No narration or finished edit until the owner has approved the final script and voice.

## The target
A video around **15 minutes**, acceptable finished runtime **10-20 minutes**. Do not quote/order based on the older 8-10-minute Green Knight standard. The editing style is economical but premium: photographs, newspaper-style real archival clippings, brand shots, stock, simple financial flows, meaningful zooms and motion. No expensive character animation required.

## Must-pass 60-90s test
Deliver an integrated sample with:
- A **genuinely irresistible first 15s** built on a documented financial surprise.
- A 30-45s real source-backed partial payoff.
- A coherent photo -> real clipping -> simple-money graphic transition.
- A narrator with suspense and breathing room, not robotic intensity.
- A phone-readable proposed thumbnail.
Assess story clarity, veracity, source visibility, usage rights, vocal quality, transitions, pacing and cost.

## Script approval and editor instructions
When final production direction is approved, the episode's `editor-packet/` contains **exactly three** files:
1. `01_SCRIPT.txt`: word-for-word spoken copy, TITLE non-spoken.
2. `02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md`: full unchanged narration plus NON-SPOKEN segment A-H maps, voice intent, pauses, pronunciation and estimated timecodes.
3. `03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md`: one chosen title and thumbnail, full 0-45s edit plan, paragraph and A-H shot sequence, real sources/rights, on-screen text, pauses, transitions and closing callback.

The editor should follow all section boundaries but must **never put production headings into the narration**. Editor may propose lower-cost execution alternatives that preserve the same dramatic revelation. Final story/picture/title approval remains with owner.

## Final deliverables to agree explicitly
Completed MP4, thumbnail, separate clean narration audio, YouTube SEO pack (chosen title, description, tags, chapter timestamps), and SRT subtitles. Agree quality, captions, revision limits, ownership, licensing and 15-minute pricing before contracting. Production project files only if separately agreed. Do not imply any Fiverr agreement or order currently exists.

## Acceptance questions
Does an ordinary viewer want to hear what happens after 15s? Does the first minute repay the click and raise a better question? Does the middle actually shift our understanding? Is the wealth/valuation/equity story accurate? Is every source image real and usable? Does the conclusion deliver a specific, memorable answer? Is the editor packet unambiguous enough to make a sample without improvising the thesis?
