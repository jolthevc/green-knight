# Fame & Fortune: narration voice

**Intelligent, magnetic, a little conspiratorial in curiosity, never breathless or sleazy.** Sound like a world-class documentary host who knows the inside story but lets the evidence speak. Natural American or neutral English is acceptable after audition; no voice selected yet.

**Performance arc:**
- A hook: low and direct, with urgency on the specific contradiction.
- B promise: intrigue, quick facts, clear question.
- C image/economics: warm, recognizably human; avoid celebrity worship.
- D big bet: quicker tempo where the decision lands, then room to understand it.
- E ownership/mechanism: slightly slower, extremely clear on numbers and differences.
- F catch: measured tension, not an angry allegation.
- G outcome: earned confidence, disclose source limits.
- H closer: reflective, punchy final line and silence.

Default test read around 140-155 spoken WPM with meaningful variation, not forced monotony. Adjust runtime only from actual approved sample. Pause for legible key financial figure. Pronounce brands, surnames and contract terms in a non-spoken guide. Editor should render segment-sized takes for retakes and pacing, and never read cue tags as dialogue.
