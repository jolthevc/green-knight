## Trial selection
Test recognizable household names with **distinct narrative engines**, not 10 biographies. Strong possible mix: Ryan Reynolds (deal), Taylor Swift (control), Rihanna (distribution), Jessica Alba (operating risk), Michael Jordan (licensing). Do not claim these are selected or fact-checked. Avoid assuming private deal terms were publicly disclosed. Before a title is approved, identify the actual primary document or credible contemporaneous report that supports its key surprise.
