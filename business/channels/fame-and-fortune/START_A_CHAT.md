# Fame & Fortune: start a fresh episode or topic chat

> Work from the current main branch of https://github.com/jolthevc/green-knight/tree/main/business. Read POPCORN_STANDARD.md, AGENTS.md, CONTINUITY_HANDOFF.md, README.md, WORKFLOW.md, LANE_ROUTING.md, IDEATION.md, EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md and DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW.md. Read the **full** current ideas/registry.json and Fame & Fortune's README, CHANNEL, CREATIVE_CONTRACT, CLICK_AND_HOOK_STANDARD, SCRIPT_STANDARD, SCRIPT_SEGMENT_TEMPLATE, STYLE, VISUAL_SYSTEM, VOICE, EDITOR_WORKFLOW, PILOT_SLATE, channel.json and narration.json.
>
> Fame & Fortune is a **15-minute-first, 10-20-minute** celebrity business thriller/documentary channel. Optimize for a very wide audience. Make the premise, clickable title and opening **juicy, salacious in genuine documented stakes, and dramatically irresistible**, but never knowingly misleading. The star must be recognizable, the contract/deal/ownership surprise must be real and the economic explanation must exceed common celebrity trivia. Do NOT use the general 8-10-minute target here.
>
> On new ideas: cross-check every channel's title, subject, mechanism and aliases in the live registry. Prepare 3 packaging/hook angles internally and propose a sourced research path. Save only **proposed** ideas using FF-I#### until owner selection. No FF-V#### before selection.
>
> When an FF idea is selected, reserve FF-V#### and research high-quality original sources. Build a sensational but truthful click promise, a documented first-15s conflict, a real partial payoff by 45 seconds, and the bigger mystery. Build the full **eight-segment** A-H narrative around 15 minutes: cold open, immediate promise, public image versus business, the deal, follow the money, the catch, outcome, and closer. Preserve flexible runtime between 10 and 20 minutes; don't pad.
>
> Draft complete clean spoken narration in canonical script.txt with a TITLE line only; keep section headings and paragraph anchors in non-spoken segment-map.md / segmented-readthrough.md. The editor must receive unequivocal segment boundaries and close alignment of approximate timecodes, paragraph ranges, narrator performance, pauses, music and visuals via the 02 and 03 documents. After full source/editorial/voice approval, and only then, release exactly three editor-facing files 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md and 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md, with 01 byte-identical to approved canonical script.
>
> Owner selects the idea and approves final script and voice before full Fiverr production. Run newcomer and skeptical-business-expert engagement reviews and the strict claims/rights review. Save/verify changes to the repository and do not invent research, sourcing, production progress or a selected voice.
