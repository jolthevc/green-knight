**Status:** selected with a complete **working first-draft spoken script**, not fully approved and not production-ready.

**Selected title (working):** The $600 Million Deal That Made Forbes Take Back Kylie Jenner's Billionaire Title

**Viewer promise:** How can a $600M transaction make a billionaire title less defensible? The answer involves old revenue assumptions, Coty's 2019 financial disclosures, a journalist's correction, Kylie's rebuttal and the distinction between business price and personal net worth.

**Logline:** The Coty deal looked like proof of the largest Kylie Jenner wealth claim. Coty's disclosure materials gave Forbes a reason to question its own earlier estimate. But the public backlash obscured a more intriguing truth about who owns brands and what celebrity attention is worth.

**No defamatory framing:** No inference of criminal fraud, court judgment, financial fabrication or business collapse. Reputational allegations always attributed and rebuttal included.

**Opening first 45 seconds:** Forbes crowns at age 21 -> $600M Coty purchase announced/closed -> Forbes reverses in May 2020 -> $360M 2018 Forbes estimate versus ~$177M Coty 2019 trailing period and 40% growth. Deliver concrete sourced mystery early.

**Core mechanism:** Sale price / stake acquired / implied enterprise value do not equal personal wealth or net realized proceeds; celebrity distribution + operational partners can produce extraordinary strategic value distinct from press wealth estimates.
