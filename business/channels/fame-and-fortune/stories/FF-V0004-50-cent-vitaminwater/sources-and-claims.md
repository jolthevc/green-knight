1. "$4.1B" is Coca-Cola's purchase price of the WHOLE Glacéau business, not 50 Cent's stake.
2. "$100M" is Forbes' estimated 2008 after-tax figure and not independently confirmed through private payment statements.
3. "$400M" was a widely repeated rumor from a purported 10% stake; do not depict it as fact. Charnas' account discusses a lower range.
4. Credit Glacéau founders Darius Bikoff and Mike Repole, marketer Rohan Oza, and 50 Cent's manager Chris Lighty, not just 50 Cent.
