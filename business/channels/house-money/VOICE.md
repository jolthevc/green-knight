# House Money narration
Curious, intelligent, grounded and quietly cinematic. Sound like an exceptionally good financial storyteller showing how the place people call home is also a complicated network of businesses. Accessible to people who have never seen a mortgage statement, interesting to housing professionals, respectful to renters, owners and builders.

**Delivery:** personable, confident, restrained; human stakes before jargon. Use genuine surprise when a capital flow or ownership right flips the apparent situation. Treat contentious housing issues with empathy and rigorous sourcing, not partisan certainty or a contemptuous finance-bro tone. Never adopt a realtor's sales pitch or a mortgage/real-estate investor coaching voice.

Speak clearly about principal, interest, APR, mortgage-backed securities, equity, NOI, origination/servicing, land options, cap rates and escrow only when defined or illuminated by graphics. Pause after big payment or unit-economics reveals. Do not speak symbolic visual cues as part of the script.
