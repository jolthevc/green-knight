# House Money: Residential Economics Editorial
**Proposed direction, not an approved production kit.** A premium documentary/illustrated economics treatment: Modern MBA's accessible business case studies + WSJ The Economics Of's rigorous financial graphics + Wendover's maps and system explanations, with authentic real housing/builder/development footage and archival documents. The B1M is a secondary reference for physical construction scenes, **not** the dominant housing-finance storytelling voice.

**Ownable visual distinction:** Show a recognizable physical home or block, then reveal the institutional layers behind it. Housing feels tactile and consequential to a real person's life; use human-scale imagery with precise financial analysis rather than Wall Street tickers or luxury real-estate glamour.

**Suggested art system, to pilot:**
- Backgrounds: warm paper/off-white and deep slate/ink, with earth-green and muted copper accents; editorial serif sparingly for chapter titles, strong legible sans for money/claims, crisp monospaced source notes if helpful.
- Documentary material: real suburban developments, apartment buildings, manufactured communities, bank documents, property photos, city maps, homebuyer experiences, insurance damage, title/escrow offices.
- Graphics: one home's price waterfall; mortgage balance/interest timeline; rent-to-NOI waterfall; land ownership vs home ownership; builder lots and financing; developer capital stack; tax/insurance/HOA monthly cost; city supply constraint layers.
- Emotional contrast: optimistic new-home footage can sit beside difficult financing; treat people as people, not inputs in a chart.
- Animate a small number of **meaningful transformations**: a mortgage sold but serviced by a different firm, a monthly payment divided, land options changing builder exposure, a site buildability bottleneck.
- Genuine report/file screenshots carry correct citations and dates; illustrative records are clearly labeled, not fake invoices or deeds.

**No rigid footage quota:** some films are historical and image-led; others require evolving diagrams. Use enough sourced footage to keep every money explanation tangible. Never endless generic key handovers or drone tours, fake company records, misleading historical recreations, hyperkinetic meme editing or unsolicited claims of a specific viewer retention outcome.
