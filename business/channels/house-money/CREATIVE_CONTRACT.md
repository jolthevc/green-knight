# House Money: creative contract
**The invitation:** Everybody needs a place to live. Underneath that familiar fact is a maze of companies, incentives, credit, ownership rights, regulations and capital flows. Show viewers one piece of this machine that turns out to be very different from what they assumed.

**Best hooks:** “You bought a house. Why might the company you pay be different from the lender who gave you the loan?” “A developer advertises a lower mortgage rate. Who funds the difference?” “You own the house in a manufactured-home park. Who owns the ground?” “A subdivision is approved, but why can't they build yet?” Only use conclusions, distinctions and figures that research genuinely supports.

**Dramatic structure:** start with a recognizable resident/developer/home problem -> establish the intuitive model -> reveal the participant/transaction the viewer can't see -> expose its economic incentives, financing and risk -> countercase or friction -> what actually changes for consumers, operators and market supply -> precise earned final insight.

**Humans matter:** Tenant, buyer, owner, builder, lender and insurer motivations can conflict without anyone being a cartoon villain. Respect genuine tradeoffs, geographic variation, distributional impact and the limits of public data.

**Expert reward:** Show the actual mechanism: land-option accounting, negative amortization where applicable, servicing rights, mortgage buy-down economics, net operating income, replacement cost, title liability, HOA governance or lender loss allocation. Never just recycle a housing-news headline.

**Newcomer reward:** Financial ideas should be graspable on first listen; introduce acronyms only when useful. Animated diagrams must animate the *choice or money flow*, not cover gaps in the story.

**Visual signature:** a real house, development or invoice as a familiar anchor; layers pull apart to reveal land, builder, originator, servicing, owner, investor, insurer and government flows. Real sourced records and clean illustrative model overlays. No generic wealth-content tropes or fake mortgage documents.

**Workflow:** Green Knight owner retains selection and editorial/voice/design approvals, and a prospective producer/editor has no autonomous authority to select, script and enter production without an actual agreed scope. Comply with the network's exact three-file director-led editor-packet, research and QA standards. No editor, brand kit, narration voice or production budget has been selected.
