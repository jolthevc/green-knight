# House Money launch experiment
**Hypothesis, not a launched channel or an investment recommendation.**

**Position:** the business of housing, not a news feed, real-estate investing guru or builder-only show. Build a recognizable visual genre around real homes, the money behind the property, and surprising economic rules. Core audience is broad housing-curious adults; informed housing professionals represent a second high-value audience but not the only viewer.

**Suggested five-film pilot composition:** one starter-home/development supply puzzle, one mortgage money flow, one builder business case study, one renting or manufactured-home community economics story, one transaction/closing-cost explanation. Owner picks actual concepts and commissions only after creative greenlight.

**Brand test:** The words House Money and The Business of Housing are working editorial names; no handle, trademark, URL or channel availability checked. No logo, references, narrator, editor, fees, production timeline, approved footage kit, subscriber or monetization data claimed.

**Audience evidence:** compare honest impression CTR by discovery source, viewer retention by beat, average viewed duration, repeat viewing, search topics, self-identified buyer/renter/operator interest, factual corrections and production costs. Five trials may reveal direction, not statistically establish winning niche.

**Potential future monetization:** advertising, disclosed sponsorships across real-estate services, mortgages, insurance, construction, property software or research; housing-industry newsletter and original products only after audience proof. Sponsors do not control findings or source choice; lending/insurance content requires careful compliance and disclosures.

**Production economics:** balance licensed housing footage, public documents, maps and reusable financial explanatory graphics. Important first pilot should prove an actual home-to-money animated mechanism, a clear human hook and evidence-grounded payoff. Default current repo runtime remains 8–10 minutes, not a YouTube rule.
