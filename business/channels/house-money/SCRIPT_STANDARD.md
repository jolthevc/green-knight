# House Money: script standard
Configured documentary runtime is **8–10 minutes** (480–600 seconds including intro, narration, pauses, holds and closing). Actual finished duration must be measured; a revised target requires an explicit cross-repository validator/config change.

**Make it a story, not a textbook.** Open on a resident's familiar moment, a surprising building/business fact, a company decision, or an honest economic contradiction. Reveal the first concrete answer early; every scene should turn the question into a better question. The financial explanation should produce the drama, not interrupt it.

**Audience:** First-time buyers, lifelong renters and general YouTube viewers should understand one honest model. Informed developers, lenders, owners and housing professionals should encounter a second, sharper causal layer and serious exceptions. Do not obscure important disagreements over affordability, agency programs, zoning or pricing.

**Required outline movement:** real entry -> intuitive assumption -> true parties/incentives -> money/property/credit flow -> decision/constraint or surprising reversal -> counterexample -> precise memorable answer with limits. Historical company docs need specific observed decisions and evidence, not mythology.

**Truth:** No unsupported “nobody builds,” “all luxury,” “Wall Street owns everything,” “mortgage companies always…” or ROI claims; working titles can be revised after sourcing. Use dated percentages and specify markets, samples and calculations. A $500,000 sample transaction is a worked illustration, not an actual invoice. Titles, cuts and thumbnail must not promise more than the evidence.

**Green Knight script interface:** canonical script.txt begins exactly TITLE: <metadata.title>, a blank line and the exact spoken narration only. Delivery cues, source IDs, paragraph and scene IDs, slide directions, source links and visual notes belong in sidecar files. The non-spoken title isn't read aloud. Track script SHA256, approval/version and exact visual+spoken correspondence.

**Editorial gate:** comply with shared POPCORN_STANDARD, EDITORIAL_REVIEW, NARRATION_STANDARD, CREATIVE_GREENLIGHT, EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD and DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW; exact three final editor-facing files only after actual voice audition, visual pilot, evidence and independent integrated signoff. Do not assume a producer/editor has been contracted by creating the folder.
