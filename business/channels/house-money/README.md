# House Money
**The Business of Housing.**

House Money is the **eleventh** Green Knight business-documentary channel. Explain how the vast housing economy makes, moves and captures money: **homebuilding, land, mortgages, renting, ownership, transactions, insurance, services and new housing models.**

**North star:** Reveal the surprising businesses, incentives, financial structures and decisions behind a familiar experience: where people live. A person buying a home, paying rent, shopping for insurance, browsing Zillow or watching a new subdivision rise should suddenly understand a hidden part of the machine. Housing is both a human necessity and a capital-intensive asset; the tension powers fascinating stories.

**Audience:** intelligent broad-viewing adults, plus informed residential developers, lenders, investors, agents, owners, tenants, operators and builders. Both newcomers and knowledgeable viewers must be rewarded. Do not become a daily home-price forecast, residential real-estate investing guru, renovation tutorial, partisan housing debate or generic news recap.

## Seven recurring editorial territories
1. The business of building homes
2. The mortgage and housing finance machine
3. Rental housing and property operation
4. Buying, selling and the transaction economy
5. Ownership: insurance, taxes, HOAs and home services
6. Land, entitlement, supply and development infrastructure
7. Housing business-model experiments and new formats
