# House Money: reusable visual storytelling toolkit
Animated graphics are a way of showing relationships, not a filler list of assets.

1. **The housing stack:** resident -> land/building -> developer/builder -> lender -> broker/title/servicer -> investor/insurer/tax authority. Reveal each relevant layer, never imply all appear in every transaction.
2. **Home price waterfall:** illustrative price -> land and lot development -> hard/soft costs -> builder expenses -> margin, with geography/date assumptions and no double counting.
3. **Lot pipeline:** raw land -> option/control -> entitlement -> utility capacity -> finished lot -> construction -> sold home; risk/capital/time displayed.
4. **Mortgage transfer:** borrower note/loan, originator, warehouse credit, buyer/investor, servicing rights and monthly remittance. Ownership vs servicing carefully separated.
5. **Monthly housing bill:** interest/principal, taxes, insurance, HOA, utilities and maintenance; no confusing balance transfers with consumptive cost.
6. **Mortgage incentive economics:** builder promotion, rate buydown cost or other financing incentive versus nominal sales price and buyer payment.
7. **Rental-property waterfall:** gross scheduled rent -> vacancy/concessions -> effective rent -> operations -> NOI -> financing/capex -> owner cash, with defined periods.
8. **Title/closing map:** buyer and seller, agent commissions where applicable, lender charges, title/escrow, transfer taxes and prorations, no legally outdated standard splits.
9. **Single-family portfolio map:** neighborhood homes, leasing/maintenance route density, financing and rent-vacancy sensitivity.
10. **Manufactured-home ownership split:** resident's home, underlying leased plot, park infrastructure, rent and mobility constraints.
11. **Homeownership risk pool:** geographic damage exposures, premiums, deductibles, reinsurance, claims and insurer solvency with carefully verified data.
12. **HOA monthly flow:** member fees -> maintenance/reserves/insurance/administration -> special assessments and governance; jurisdiction differences.
13. **Apartment feasibility model:** land price, permitted units, construction capex, financing, rents and operating costs with subsidy/legal context as relevant.
14. **Building method comparison:** factory vs site construction, delivery, labor, customization, site work, permitting and financing; compare like for like.
15. **Company/capital timeline:** genuine acquisitions/land positions/new markets and annual-report data from a named homebuilder/lender/service platform.

In every film, the signature change is conceptual: a money flow reveals a beneficiary or burden, an asset splits into rights, a home value becomes an operating P&L, or a land plot becomes buildable only after capital and time. Label estimates **illustrative** in narration and graphics; evidence screens must be actual source material.
