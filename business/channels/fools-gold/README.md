# Fool’s Gold

**Promise:** Understand what people were led to believe, how the deception worked, and what exposed it.

**Format:** 8–10 minute narrated business/finance investigations for curious adults. Financial schemes, false accounting, counterfeit markets and manufactured credibility, told as evidence-backed puzzles.
