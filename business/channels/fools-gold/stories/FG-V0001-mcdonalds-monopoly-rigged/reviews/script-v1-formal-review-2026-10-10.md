## Source challenge and counterevidence

**Opened primary legal sources:** [DOJ August 2001 initial allegations](https://www.justice.gov/archive/opa/pr/2001/August/422ag.htm); [2004 11th Circuit United States v. Contemporary FBI/DOJ allegations and Washington Post reporting also reopened. Initial press-release reporting is not adjudication; the guilty plea and court findings carry different evidentiary weight.

**New useful document:** In `Simon Marketing v. Gulf Insurance`, the California appellate court records the security director's specific role and Simon's asserted ~**$21m redemption value** across a different broader period. **Do not mix $21m with the August 2001 >$13m allegation or Jacobson's actual take.** It is potentially valuable for a researched contextual sidebar, not required to add spoken figures.

The 2004 `Chandler` court held that the government had not proved the four appellants knowingly joined the *charged conspiracy* and ordered acquittals; it also identified the government's shifting theory as trial error. It did **not** establish that no others knowingly assisted Jacobson. Avoid narration implying every transferred ticket or rule violation amounted to criminal mail fraud. The film-like moral “fraudulent provenance” pertains to pieces Jacobson stole; the status of individual later holders may differ.
