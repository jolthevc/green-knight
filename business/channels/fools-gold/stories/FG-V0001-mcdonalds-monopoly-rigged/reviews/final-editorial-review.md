## Counterevidence / uncertainty

Initial charges do not establish all winners' guilt. The Chandler court reversed four convictions because the government failed to prove these appellants knowingly conspired to redeem stolen stamps; violating contest rules or receiving a stamp from someone else did not establish charged conspiracy. Jacobson's guilty plea admitted his theft. We do not know full internal security procedure, every winner's knowledge, a fully reconciled lifetime jackpot total or what restitution was actually collected. Do not add unsupported claims or imply the high-value game pieces were counterfeits.
