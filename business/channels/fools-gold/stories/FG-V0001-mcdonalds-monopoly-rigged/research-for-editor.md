## One paragraph

McDonald's used Simon Marketing to operate major Monopoly and related sweepstakes. Simon's security worker Jerome Jacobson had access to genuine rare winning game pieces that should be sent through random customer channels. He took pieces and arranged their movement through recruiters to apparent prize claimants. Claims could result in real official payments despite the fraudulent way the pieces reached claimants. The FBI's Jacksonville investigation began from an anonymous tip and traced relationships; in 2001 McDonald's cooperated while a contest continued under observation, followed by August arrests. Jacobson pleaded guilty and received 37 months in 2003. In a different part of the litigation, four other men's convictions were overturned in 2004 because prosecutors had not proved their knowledge of stolen game pieces.
