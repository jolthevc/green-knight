# Deception mechanism story: 8–10 minutes

Research before deciding the reveal. Develop the thesis and internal outline, write the whole spoken draft in one pass, then review and revise internally.

## Narrative architecture

| Approximate window | Story job |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | A specific apparent reality and an evidence-backed contradiction |
| 0:25–1:30 | Explain the promise, legitimate baseline and first useful insight |
| 1:30–3:00 | Establish why the claim appeared credible |
| 3:00–5:00 | Reveal the money, records, goods or reputation mechanism |
| 5:00–7:00 | Follow the verification gap and evidence of exposure |
| 7:00–9:30 | Explain established consequences, unresolved issues and the specific insight |
| Final 5–20 seconds | Relevant next-video invitation when available |

Windows are estimates. Finished runtime is 8–10 minutes including holds and closing. Narrow the mechanism or period if the case is too large.

## Hook and retention

Prepare two or three supported hook options internally. Start with something comprehensible that did not match the appearance. Do not invent a quote, amount or accusation to strengthen the hook.

Match the title/thumbnail in the first 30 seconds. Establish the basic discrepancy early. Curiosity should grow through the mechanism and evidence, not through withholding every answer until the ending.

Every beat changes the viewer's understanding: apparent promise, credibility, real flow, failed check, evidence and consequence. About every 30–60 seconds check whether something meaningful progressed. This is an editorial diagnostic, not a platform rule.

Avoid generic greetings, early subscription requests, repeated “you won't believe” promises and an endless perpetrator biography.

## Mechanism and facts

First explain how the legitimate system would work. Then show the deviation and why it was hard to observe. Use concrete examples, clearly labeled when illustrative.

Distinguish claims, allegations, admissions, established findings and interpretation. Attach status to consequential assertions without burying every sentence in legalistic language. Recheck material current case status when relevant.

Trace numbers and causal assertions to opened sources. Distinguish assets claimed, money raised, cash available, losses and recoveries. Do not combine overlapping figures for a larger headline.

Do not invent internal scenes, private motives, dialogue, documents or victim stories. Use symbolic visuals to teach without presenting them as actual events.

## Tone and spoken writing

Clear, varied, conversational language. Explain technical terms through the mechanism. Let real evidence create intrigue. Avoid gloating, criminal admiration, accusations by innuendo and jokes about harmed people.

script.txt contains only spoken words. Source IDs, headings, timestamps and delivery/visual directions stay in sidecars. Paragraph-linked reveals must match the current narration.

## Ending and CTA

Answer how the discrepancy was sustained and what exposed it. Give one specific insight into verification and trust, with its limits. Do not imply that every similar business or person is fraudulent.

A short main CTA follows the payoff, usually a related published video. If none exists, use a launch-safe channel invitation. No phantom links or stack of engagement requests.

## Internal review

Check legal/evidence status, actual versus claimed money flow, source independence, numbers, missing evidence, counterpoints, title promise, early value, progression, empathy and production feasibility.

Complete the shared package with appearance/reality map, verification-gap analysis and exposure timeline in research/synthesis. Validate anchors and timing estimates. Final production approval requires measured duration and listening to the actual cut.

## Broad-audience discovery

Apply [ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md](../../ENGAGEMENT_STANDARD.md). This channel's story engine is an appearance-versus-reality mystery. Establish the legitimate baseline, then reveal a specific discrepancy and verification gap. Return to an initially credible object or record after evidence changes its meaning. Keep allegation and established fact distinct; no staged accusation or forced magician reveal.

Use the newcomer and informed-skeptic lenses. Record each beat's changed understanding and why the next follows. Choose an earned first payoff and a specific final insight. Aim for substantial learning delivered through the story, with warmth and room to follow, rather than a list of business frameworks.
