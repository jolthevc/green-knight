# Choose the lane by the promised answer

Use this before reserving a story. The subject alone does not determine the channel.

| Channel | One-sentence answer the episode must deliver | Internal explanatory structure (not editor directions) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pretty Penny | This is how the money and incentives behind a familiar thing work | Money-flow diagram or supported cost model |
| Money Moves | This is why this strategic choice made sense, and what it changed | Options, constraint and consequences |
| Fallen Angels | These pressures and responses explain how the advantage or business failed | Deterioration timeline and causal model |
| Changing Hands | This position/transaction and its constraints explain the financial outcome | Positions, obligations, funding and ownership |
| Fool’s Gold | This discrepancy and verification gap explain the deception and exposure | Apparent versus actual flow |
| Silver Spoon | These rights and transfers explain how the family kept, divided or lost wealth/control | Family/ownership/rights map |
| First Billion | These steps, unit economics, capital and ownership explain a defined wealth/business milestone | Starting resources, scale and ownership bridge |
| On the Map | These location-specific conditions and reinforcing effects explain why activity began, persisted or changed here and who benefited | Starting geography, foothold, transitions, alternatives, value capture and constraints |
| Hard Costs | This construction-specific project, company, deal or operator growth mechanism works or fails because of these money flows, decisions and physical constraints | Real asset/job/operating unit, contract/cash chain, labor/capital bottlenecks, changes with scale, replicable and nonreplicable lessons |
| Built to Scale | This specific operator choice or organizational process helps explain how a business grows, works better or fails and what others can learn conditionally | Source-led operating dilemma, real options and their cost, comparable outcomes, causal mechanism and limits on transfer |

## Resolve overlaps

Write the central question and likely final answer without the proposed channel name. If two channels would deliver substantially the same answer, commission one episode and route it to the better fit.

A company can return when the mechanism, period or question materially differs. Record related_idea_ids and a plain-language distinction in the brief. Do not reserve multiple near-identical versions simultaneously to fill the network.

Examples of framing, not researched facts:
- A fund's position unwind is Changing Hands; its organizational decline is Fallen Angels.
- Family voting control is Silver Spoon; takeover bid financing is Changing Hands.
- A pricing system is Pretty Penny; the decision to introduce it is Money Moves.
- A complete wealth-building path is First Billion; one strategic decision within it is Money Moves, a discrete deal is Changing Hands, and intergenerational transfer is Silver Spoon. First Billion needs a materially different final answer, not a longer founder biography.
- On the Map requires location and its surrounding system to be essential to the answer. Pretty Penny follows an ongoing money system, Money Moves a strategic choice, First Billion a wealth-building path. A broader ecosystem is not a relabeled founder biography. Compare any Emirates/Dubai proposal with MM-I0002 before reservation.
- Concealment mechanics are Fool’s Gold; a broader operating collapse is Fallen Angels.

If research changes the lane, preserve the idea ID/history, record the reframe, and allocate a destination-channel idea linked to the original if a new story is commissioned. Archive the original proposal rather than silently changing its prefix/channel. Before story creation the user should understand a material change in the selected promise; routine narrowing within the promise does not need another gate.

## Hard Costs overlap
Hard Costs follows construction economics, physical project risk, trades and practical operator growth. First Billion is a broad defined-billion milestone path where construction mechanisms are incidental. Pretty Penny is familiar consumer spending. On the Map needs location and regional clustering to drive the explanation. Fallen Angels owns a company collapse when loss of advantage is the answer; Money Moves owns one consequential strategic choice. Landmark histories belong in Hard Costs when construction/financing is the mechanism, rather than tourism, geography or company biography. Check the final takeaway, not the subject's industry.

## Hard Costs vs Built to Scale
Hard Costs asks what the business and economics behind a physical project, structure or construction industry are. Built to Scale asks which **operating choice, customer system or organizational process** made a business better, for whom, and under which circumstances the idea transfers. Hard Costs retains its broad construction focus, including existing operator stories. Do not commission a BTS version of HC-I0001, HC-I0007 or HC-I0010 with the same mechanism under a new label. A famous bridge's funding is Hard Costs; why a service company runs reliably without its founder is Built to Scale. A defined wealth-billion outcome normally belongs in First Billion. Cross-link any genuinely distinct related concepts; only archive or reroute with owner agreement.

## Built to Scale growth-first routing update (2026-10-14)
Treat Built to Scale as the **growth playbook for owners/aspiring owners/operators of trades and construction firms**, not a general operations curiosity lane. Its core answer is *how did this contractor gain customers/capacity/talent/capital/markets or acquire firms without (or despite) breaking at scale?* A management, pricing or operations story belongs here when tied to a meaningful growth transition, and should expose an operator-transferrable mechanism. Quanta/A1/Roto-Rooter/Comfort Systems/Neighborly are unverified case-study research leads for acquisition, customer distribution, branding, franchising or operator management. Hard Costs remains the broad economic/history/business story of what gets built; same company is allowed only for truly different mechanisms, source narrative and audience promise. Do not merely re-edit or retitle HC-I0001, HC-I0007, HC-I0010.
