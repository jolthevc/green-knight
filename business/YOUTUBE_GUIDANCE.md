# YouTube guidance and our editorial choices

Reviewed 2026-10-10 UTC. Recheck official pages when platform behavior or a launch decision depends on them. Guidance informs experiments; it does not guarantee reach or monetization.

| Official source | Supported guidance | Our implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en) | The intro metric describes viewers remaining after 30 seconds. Package alignment and compelling early content matter. Review later strong moments, dips and ambiguous spikes. | Open directly on the promised puzzle, deliver an early useful insight, and diagnose losses by exact scene/paragraph. A spike can mean confusion. |
| [Titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en) | Accurate, concise titles and readable, uncomplicated thumbnails; evaluate CTR by audience/traffic source. | Produce distinct honest title/thumbnail pairs, put the subject early, inspect at phone size, and avoid judging overall CTR in isolation. |
| [End screens](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en) | End screens can occupy the final 5–20 seconds; relevant elements and performance measurement are available. | Finish the answer, then use one relevant next-video invitation in the closing window. Reserve layout space; never point to a nonexistent upload. |
| [Tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en) | Tags have a small discovery role except useful spelling variations. | Spend effort on the idea, package and viewing experience; use a small relevant tag set rather than keyword stuffing. |

## Our hypotheses, not YouTube rules

A concrete puzzle, progress through the explanation, varied delivery and satisfying payoff should help this channel. A fresh development about every 30–60 seconds is an editing diagnostic, not a required cut frequency. Let important explanations breathe. A visual change is useful when it advances understanding.

The proposed opening is roughly 20–30 seconds, with a first useful reveal early. Do not withhold the entire answer until the last minute. Curiosity comes from progressively harder questions, not repeated promises that a secret is coming.

Place at most one main spoken CTA after value has been delivered, usually a relevant next-video invitation at the end. A specific comment question is optional. Avoid greeting, subscription requests before the story, engagement bait, generic like/comment/subscribe stacks and a long goodbye. This placement is our editorial choice.

No fixed publication cadence, universal retention threshold, RPM guarantee, mandatory jump-cut rate or mandatory thumbnail expression is established by these sources. Start with a sustainable schedule after pilots establish production capacity.

## Packaging deliverable

Supply three accurate title options, two materially different thumbnail concepts, a chosen pair with its specific promise, a unique description opening, selected source links, draft chapters and a concise relevant tag set. Final chapter times come from the edited cut. Check that the opening and conclusion honor the chosen title/thumbnail.

## Originality and disclosure at upload

[YouTube monetization guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en), checked 2026-10-10: original, authentic substance matters; generic, repetitive or mass-produced content is a problem. Reusable identity does not justify interchangeable scripts or minimally varied videos. Our implementation is distinct research, explanation and scenes for each story, including across channels. AI use or handmade assets alone do not guarantee eligibility.

[YouTube's synthetic-content disclosure guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en), checked 2026-10-10, distinguishes unrealistic/minor assistance from realistic or meaningfully altered content requiring disclosure. Review the actual narration, imagery and music at upload; its examples include AI-generated music. Do not assume that illustrated scenes remove disclosure requirements for every other asset. Record the upload decision and reason in packaging.md using current policy.
