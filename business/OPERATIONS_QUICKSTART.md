# Green Knight: Operations Quickstart

**Highest creative bar:** [POPCORN_STANDARD.md](POPCORN_STANDARD.md), the two-viewer popcorn-good mandate. Episodes must be deeply entertaining AND reveal a non-obvious defensible business mechanism to viewers already familiar with the story. A script can pass evidence QA and still fail creative QA.

These rules apply to all eight business channels. For details, follow AGENTS.md, WORKFLOW.md, EDITOR_HANDOFF_STANDARD.md, DIRECTOR_PACKET_REVIEW.md, and DELIVERY_QA_STANDARD.md.

## Episode lifecycle

1. **Ideas:** Request ideas for a channel. Check the live cross-channel registry; recommend a candidate and let the owner choose.
2. **Create episode:** Request an episode by idea ID. Complete research and the full script, plus complete **working** narration and visual direction, including thumbnail and cinematic opening. Save to GitHub. Creation is not final packet approval.
3. **Review and revise:** Review all three components separately and together. Revise and re-review. When all gates pass and an actual selected channel narrator is recorded, release exactly three instructions in story/editor-packet/: 01_SCRIPT.txt, 02_NARRATION_DIRECTOR.md, 03_VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md. An unselected narrator blocks release but not thorough working drafts.
4. **Review delivered video:** Compare accessible media and packaging against the approved three documents. Use DELIVERY_QA_STANDARD.md; save a timecoded report in story/delivery-qa/ and give the editor one prioritized revision message.
5. **Review revisions:** Recheck earlier issues, regressions, final deliverables and final acceptance. The owner approves commissioning, payments and publishing.

## Outgoing and incoming

The editor receives **three instruction files**: exact script, performance/narration director and visual director. The visual director selects one thumbnail/opening and shows what changes on screen; editor retains technical/animation flexibility.

The editor returns **five final deliverables**: finished MP4 (1080p+), standalone thumbnail, clean narration audio, SEO title/description/tags with final clickable chapter timestamps, and synchronized SRT. Draft cuts may be reviewed with pending attachments.

## Batch coordination

The owner may prepare and send all ten approved packets together. Five-plus-five payment milestones, production deadlines, revision entitlements and parallelization depend on actual editor agreement. Ask for realistic targets for five videos and ten, including revision time. Review the first aligned video before increasing concurrent work. Do not assume daily script delivery.

## Readiness

Narrator profiles remain unselected unless actually approved in the channel narration.json. Old scripts are not automatically three-file editor packets. No amount of text QA constitutes a full watch/listen of an inaccessible or unplayed film. GitHub documents do not create or publish YouTube channels.

## New editor onboarding agent command
`Onboard a new editor for [CHANNEL]. Follow business/EDITOR_ONBOARDING_PLAYBOOK.md; prepare a concise first Fiverr message, an internal intake/terms checklist, pilot and milestone decisions, the three outgoing packet rules, and five required final files. Do not message, book or pay without my authorization.`

For editor replies, use the same playbook to preserve confirmations and avoid repeating questions or introducing unsupported obligations. Maintain one evidence-backed editor-specific intake record where requested. A ten-video batch may be sent all at once when approved materials are ready, not as an invented date promise.
